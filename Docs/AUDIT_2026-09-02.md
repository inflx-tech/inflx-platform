# PLATFORM AUDIT — 2026-09-02

**Phase 1 of `Docs/THE_FINISH_LINE.md`.** Every screen in all three mobile apps, read
top to bottom the way a thumb sees it, judged against the locked design
(`features/screen-atlas/`) and the current architecture.

**Scope covered:** 120 mobile screens — brand 45, influencer 29, follower 46.
**Not yet covered:** backend, admin, brand-web. Queued.

**Nothing in this document has been fixed.** Founder decision 2026-09-02: full list
first, then burn down in one pass.

## PART 1 — The five that break the spine

If nothing else is fixed, these five still must be, because each one stops the
campaign→sign→sell journey dead.

### 1. A campaign built the only supported way cannot be published
`screens/brand/BrandCampaignReviewScreen.js:80`

Every creator added by search triggers *"her agreement is no longer live — remove her, or
agree terms again from the Creators tab"*, which is a **blocker**, which greys out
Publish. So the entire search → invite → set flow shipped on 2026-09-02 dead-ends at the
last step.

**This is the same bug fixed that morning** in `BrandSetWizardScreen.js:164`
(`draft.isRealAgreementId` guard). One instance was fixed; the class was never grepped
for. One-line fix. **No test covers it** — `sb37PublishReview.test.js` and
`publishGateConsistency.test.js` contain no cold-invite case at all.

### 2. A creator cannot start a negotiation. From anywhere.
`screens/influencer/NegotiationScreen.js:206` · `services/api.js:429`

She taps "Negotiate" → lands on a page reading *"No negotiation yet — tap Negotiate on an
offer to start a conversation."* That is the button she just pressed.

`negotiateOffer` (POST `/offers/:id/negotiate`) — the **only** endpoint that creates a
negotiation thread — is called by **zero screens**. The backend is correct and complete;
there is no door to it. This is the single largest gap between today and the founder's
stated goal of reaching a signed contract.

### 3. A second Publish button that invites nobody
`screens/brand/BrandCampaignDetailScreen.js:242`

Publishes the campaign without sending a single invite, then says *"creators can be
invited"*. Everyone pencilled in is lost and nobody is ever contacted. The correct path
(`BrandCampaignReviewScreen.js:141-168`) sends invites first. Reachable from the Home tab.

### 4. Three of the creator's notifications go nowhere
`screens/influencer/NotificationsScreen.js:76, 89, 92`

- *"the brand replied to your terms"* → "No negotiation yet" (ID passed under the wrong name)
- *"your contract is ready to sign"* → error popup, then "Contract not found"
- *"a brand asked you into a collaboration"* → dumps her on Profile, nothing opens

Every one is a wrong-parameter or wrong-stack mistake, not missing functionality.

### 5. Anything opened from the follower's Profile tab is half-dead
`navigation/FollowerNavigator.js:172-210`

`ProfileStack` does not include the shared screen list every other tab includes. So:
open a creator you follow from Profile → tap any product, category, store or posts
banner → **nothing happens**. The identical screen works perfectly from Home. Also kills
"Pay Balance" on a booking and the "You might also like" cards in the bag.

One missing line; many dead taps.

## PART 2 — The themes

Grouped by cause, because each theme is one decision rather than many.

### Theme A — The retirement stopped at the wizard's edges (~15 findings, brand)

The set wizard itself was cleaned correctly. The screens on either side of it were not.
They still treat agreements as stock to be spent:

- Creators tab still has an **"Agreed" sub-tab**: *"Nobody has agreed terms yet — when a
  creator accepts, they wait here until you build a campaign."* That pool is exactly what
  set-first deletes. (`BrandCreatorsScreen.js:38, 279`)
- Rows read **"READY TO BRIEF"** and are not tappable — a promise you can spend her, then
  nothing happens. (`:270`)
- Empty state: *"Find creators, then ask one, a group, or everyone who matches your
  filters"* — none of those three actions exist. (`:365`)
- Publish review says **"5 agreements consumed"** when nobody agreed anything. (`BrandCampaignReviewScreen.js:262`)
- Campaign builder cards read **"3 agreed"** for creators never contacted. (`BrandCampaignBuilderScreen.js:165`)
- Set wizard step 1 still says the aim is locked because *"the agreements you brief into
  it were made against it."* (`BrandSetWizardScreen.js:458`)
- An AGREED creator has **no button at all** — a dead end. (`BrandCollabRequestsScreen.js:270`)

**Note on the group fix from 2026-09-02:** the full-screen copy was corrected, but the
**inline copy the founder actually sees in the Creators tab was missed**
(`BrandGroupsList.js:57`) — and `BrandGroupsScreen.js:166`'s own empty state still
contradicts the corrected explainer four lines above it. Two places doing one job; one
updated.

### Theme B — "Go use the web dashboard" (5 findings, brand)

Campaign creation moved to the phone. The app still sends you away:

- Empty account, very first instruction: *"Create campaigns from the web dashboard."* (`BrandHomeScreen.js:294`)
- A permanent card above your campaigns: *"Open web dashboard — full campaign management."* (`:243`)
- Every agreed creator: *"Build the campaign on the web dashboard — their terms arrive
  pre-filled."* Wrong twice. (`BrandCollabRequestsScreen.js:283`)
- Creator profile invite panel: *"Create one from the web dashboard first"* — and it
  filters out drafts, so you cannot invite into the campaign you are building. (`BrandInfluencerProfileScreen.js:343, 236`)

### Theme C — Dates and money read from retired fields (~8 findings, brand)

The move from posting-window to go-live day, and from set-level to per-creator money, was
done in the wizard and not in the screens that display the result:

- Every set built the new way shows **no date at all**. (`BrandCampaignSetsListScreen.js:162`)
- Every campaign row reads **"— no dates set —"**. (`BrandCampaignListScreen.js:373`)
- Campaign detail reads **"— → Ongoing"** on every campaign. (`BrandCampaignDetailScreen.js:285`)
- A sales set where every creator has her own offer still says **"Follower offer: not
  chosen"** and never shows READY. (`BrandCampaignBuilderScreen.js:169`)

### Theme D — The old world still ships underneath the fashion app (~40 findings, follower)

The fashion rebuild layered on top; nothing was removed. A real shopper today can reach:

- **Cars** with a hardcoded **4.8 stars and "124 reviews"**, "Insurance: Covered", and a
  pickup date on screen ("Apr 10") that **differs from the date actually booked**
  (tomorrow). (`CarDetailScreen.js:116-146` · `CarBookingScreen.js:174`)
- **Restaurants** showing a green **"Open now"** regardless of hours; a table booking that
  completes with a **fake payment ID** (`pay_mock_…`) taking no money, after which the
  next screen states an advance was paid; one invented slot list for every restaurant.
  (`RestaurantDetailScreen.js:291` · `RestaurantBookingScreen.js:47, 127`)
- A **Find tab** listing Pest Control, Dental Clinics, Wedding Planners, Legal/CA — with a
  search box that accepts typing and filters nothing. (`FindScreen.js:20-91, 143`)
- A **wallet** explaining a cashback scheme the platform does not run. (`FollowerWalletScreen.js:160`)
- Every store still carries a **"Deals (n)"** tab and *"% OFF via influencer deal"* — the
  retired offers model. (`BrandStoreScreen.js:401, 335`)

**Most of this is deletion work, not repair work.** See Decision 1.

### Theme G — Two of everything (5 findings)

The same job done twice, with only one copy maintained:

### Theme H — Promises about money that the server does not keep (4 findings)

Flagged separately because these involve real rupees:

  nothing. (`BrandBookingsScreen.js:56` vs `brandBookings.controller.js:190`)
- Converting an enquiry always says **a creator has been paid commission**, even when no
  creator was involved. (`BrandEnquiriesScreen.js:103`)
- Saving bank details says **"You can now receive payouts"** — a second step on a different
  screen is still required. (`BrandWalletScreen.js:212`)
- Three creator screens hardcode **"30 days"** as the commission wait; the real value is
  per-category and set by admin. (`ContractScreen.js:615` · `CommissionDetailScreen.js:308` · `EarningsScreen.js:108`)

## PART 5 — The decisions needed

Only the founder can make these. Each one collapses a large part of the list.

### Decision 1 — Is the follower app fashion-only? *(collapses ~40 findings)*
If yes, the restaurant / food / car-rental screens are **deleted**, not repaired, and
Theme D largely disappears. If they must stay, they need real data and real payments —
substantial new work.

### Decision 2 — Does the pre-agreement pathway survive at all, in any form? *(collapses ~15)*
Brand-initiated is retired and creator pitching stays — but that leaves live rows and
live screens in between: `BrandCollabRequestsScreen`, `BrandAgreementDetailScreen`, the
"Agreed" sub-tab. Do those become **read-only history**, or go entirely?

### Decision 3 — Is SB-41 "Which set?" in v1? *(unblocks 2 dead ends)*
Without it, an accepted pitch and a profile invite have nowhere to land.

### Decision 4 — What is the v1 spine?
Recommended: brand creates campaign → invites creator (search/group) → creator negotiates
→ both sign → shopper buys → money settles. Everything off that line is parked in writing.

### Decision 5 — Usage rights on a cold invite
`contract.controller.js:196` sources usage terms from the retired `collab_agreements`. A
cold invite has none, and no `usage` column exists on `campaign_influencers`. So SB-45's
"How you may use it" currently has no data source. Needs a product answer.

## Appendix — how this was produced

Three parallel read-only audits, one per app, each given the current architecture and the
locked Atlas and asked to read every screen's render output top to bottom. Every
`BLOCKS_USE` finding above was then **re-verified by hand against source** before being
written down.

Two findings were retracted during verification rather than reported.

Known limits: this is static reading. It cannot catch anything that only appears with
specific live data. The emulator harness (`Docs/THE_FINISH_LINE.md` Part 0) now exists to
close that gap during Phase 3.
