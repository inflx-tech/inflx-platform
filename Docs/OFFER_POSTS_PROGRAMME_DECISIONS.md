# Offers → Creator terms → Shoppable posts — founder decisions (28 September 2026)

Three founder documents were read together: (1) offer creation, (2) creator terms / what she can
sell, (3) shoppable post creation. Surveyed against the code the same day. This file records the
founder's answers to the combined doubt list. Screens are drawn and locked from this before any code.

## The chain

Brand builds offers from product pools (one product, one offer) → brand gives a creator offers +
products without offers in her terms → creator makes posts (one brand, one collaboration, one offer
or one price band) → shopper buys through the post → that post's creator is credited.

## Decided

### Offer engine
| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| 1 | Whole-store offers are removed. Pools only. |
| 2 | "MRP" in the spec = the product's single price (MRP retired July 2026). |
| 3 | **Margin floor dropped for now** — discuss later. No margin checks at publish. |
| 4 | Clean break: old offers archived, new shapes replace old. |
| 5 | Where offers are created: **open** — website unless the phone proves workable. |
| 6 | Product removed from a live offer: old price honoured only if payment already started. |
| 7 | **Offers are creator-only.** "Publish to store" removed. Every purchase happens through a creator's post; the brand store itself is made of posts. |

### Offer dates vs posting date
- Posting date = the day creators go live on social media. Creators can join a set until posting
  date − 3 (time to negotiate, shoot, build posts).
- **An offer can be given to a creator only if it is valid on the posting date or the day after.**
  Example, posting 10 Oct: an offer running 1–31 Oct CAN be given (valid on the 10th); an offer
  starting 15 Oct CANNOT; an offer that ended 5 Oct is not shown. End date is the brand's choice.
- **"Product without an offer"** = no offer holds it on or after the posting date. Jeans whose offer
  ended 5 Oct can be given as a normal-price product for a 10 Oct posting.

### Creator terms
| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| 10 | **No adding offers after signing.** A new offer = a new collaboration = a new set (one live collaboration per creator per set). |
| 11 | Agreement lists her offers as of signing (with "offers may end"). |

### Shoppable posts
| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| 14 | One brand per post (multi-brand dropped). |
| 15 | **Collection** = creator names it and picks either her posts or her allocated products. Nothing more. Each product in a collection behaves like a one-product post from the collaboration she picked it from (its offer on that item alone, her commission). Collections need no admin approval. |
| 16 | Admin approves every post before it goes live. |
| 17 | Posts are created after the shoot plan (business rules to be shared by founder). |
| 18 | **Pay for Sale / Hybrid collaborations have no end.** They end only when the offer's end date passes, or all products in the post are out of stock. |
| 18a | **When an offer ends, its posts are REMOVED from the shop** (not sold at normal price) — the media promises the offer, selling at normal price would be unfair. Price posts (no offer) end only when all their products are out of stock. Replaces doc 3's "post stays live, badge disappears". |
| 19 | Minimum shoppable posts: count approved posts in the collaboration by posting date; if missed, flag to brand, no penalty. |
| 20 | Price change on a product live in posts: warn the brand before saving, notify affected creators (Claude to propose detail). |
| 21 | An offer post carries every category its products span. |
| 22 | Price post category may be picked at any level; post stores its products' leaves. |
| 23 | **Offers apply strictly within one post** — one post, one creator, one collaboration. No completing a bundle across posts. |
| 24 | Commission per item, to the creator whose post the item was added from. |
