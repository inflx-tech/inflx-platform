# Step 3 · What we are NOT doing

- **No screens.** Not one. The two numbers get a doorway; nothing displays them yet. That is
  Stage 1 and Stage 3.
- **No history screen.** The diary can be read by then, but nobody looks at it until Stage 1.
- **Nothing visible to a real follower.** No practice shop, product or post is switched on for
  the public. See the decision below.
- **No second summary.** The brand's existing "what needs me" answer gets extended, never
  duplicated.
- **Not opening brands to pitches.** You said yes to that — it belongs to Stage 1, with its own
  go on the day.

# The one decision Stage 0 needs from you

**Where does the practice world live?**

The live system is the only database, your phones point at it, and real people are signing up
this week. So we cannot simply fill it with fake shops.

### What I propose

**Practice brands, creators, agreements, campaigns, shoots and posts are created in the live
system but left switched off** — not published, not active, not live.

They are then **naturally invisible to real followers**, because the follower app already only
shows things that are published and active. Nothing new has to be hidden; they simply never
qualify. You see them by logging into the brand and creator apps. Nobody else sees anything.

That covers **Stages 0 through 5 completely** — every one of those is brand-and-creator work.

**Stage 6 is the first stage where practice data has to be visible to a follower.** We decide
that when we get there, and by then you may have real creators posting anyway. I would rather
not switch fake shops on in front of real customers three stages early.

**What I need from you:** **two phone numbers you actually hold** — one for the practice brand,
one for the practice creator. Logging in sends a real SMS and there is no way around it. Ideally
a third for a practice follower, but that can wait until Stage 6.

### The alternative, honestly

Build a separate practice system. It needs its own database and your phones would need a
separate app build pointing at it — so what you test stops being the thing you actually ship.
Slower, and further from the truth. **I do not recommend it, but it is your call.**
