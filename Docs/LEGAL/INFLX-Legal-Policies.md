# 3. Community Guidelines

**Applies to:** Everyone who posts, sells or buys on INFLX.

## 3.1 What INFLX is for

INFLX is a place where brands run campaigns, creators promote offers to their own followers, and shoppers buy through creator storefronts. These guidelines keep that useful and honest for everyone. They apply to brands, creators and shoppers alike, and cover what you post, what you sell, the reviews you write and how you treat other people. This version takes effect on 1 August 2026.

They are part of our [Terms of Use](/legal/terms). Breaking a guideline is breaking the terms. If something here is unclear, ask us at contact@inflx.in before you post, not after.

## 3.2 Be authentic

The platform only works if numbers and opinions are real. So:

- Do not buy followers, likes, views or comments, and do not use engagement pods, bots or click farms.
- Do not fabricate or edit your audience or performance figures.
- Do not write or arrange fake reviews, and do not review something you have not used.
- Do not pose as an ordinary customer when you were paid, gifted or otherwise connected to the brand.
- Do not create extra accounts, sell or rent your account, or use someone else's identity.
- Do not place orders through your own storefront, or arrange for others to, in order to generate commission for yourself.

Where we find this, we can remove the content, reverse the commission it earned, and act on the account.

## 3.3 Disclose paid promotions

If you were paid, gifted, discounted or otherwise connected to what you are promoting, say so. Put the disclosure where people will see it, before they read or watch the rest — not only in your bio, at the end of a post, or buried in hashtags.

This applies even when nobody asked you to mention the product and the opinion is genuinely your own. Your opinion should be honest and based on real experience of the product. If you give specialist advice about finance or health, say what qualifies you to give it.

The detailed rules on labels and placement are at [Advertising Disclosure](/legal/advertising-disclosure). The duty sits on the brand as well as the creator, and undisclosed endorsements can attract penalties under applicable Indian law.

## 3.4 Declare AI-generated content

If any part of an image, video or audio you post was generated or meaningfully altered by AI, declare it when you upload and keep the label visible on the content. This covers fully AI-generated visuals, AI-generated product shots or models, altered faces and bodies, objects added or removed, and synthetic voiceovers.

Ordinary cropping, colour correction and standard filters do not need a declaration. Removing or hiding a label that has been applied is itself a breach.

Never use AI to make a real person appear to say or do something they did not, or to alter someone's face or body without their consent. That content is not allowed at all.

## 3.5 Respect and safety

Disagreeing with someone is fine. The following is not.

- Harassment, bullying, threats, or organising a pile-on against someone.
- Hate speech, or attacking a person on the basis of religion, caste, gender, sexual orientation, disability, ethnicity or region.
- Publishing someone's private information — address, phone number, documents — without their consent.
- Sexual content, and anything that sexualises, endangers or exploits a child.
- Sharing a person's intimate images, or morphed images of them.
- Impersonating a brand, a creator or any other person.
- Encouraging self-harm, dangerous stunts, or unsafe use of a product.

Brands are responsible for what is posted from their accounts and outlets. Creators are responsible for the comment sections they moderate on a paid campaign post.

## 3.6 What you must not post

Do not post anything unlawful, obscene, harmful to a child, invasive of someone's privacy, or that infringes another person's intellectual property. Do not post anything that misleads about where it came from, impersonates someone, or is designed to damage a computer system.

The full list of content that may not be hosted or shared on INFLX is set out in our [Terms of Use](/legal/terms). Copyright complaints are handled separately — see [Copyright](/legal/copyright).

# 9. Advertising & Sponsored Content Disclosure

**Applies to:** Creators who post about brands, and the brands who pay them. Shoppers can use it to check whether a post was declared.

## 9.1 Why disclosure matters

A shopper judges a recommendation differently once they know it was paid for. Hiding the payment takes that judgement away from them. That is why disclosure is required by law in India, and why we enforce it on INFLX.

This page applies to every post made through an INFLX campaign, and to any post about a brand you have a connection with, wherever you publish it. It takes effect on 1 August 2026.

## 9.2 What counts as a paid connection

A paid connection is anything of value that passes between you and a brand, or any relationship a viewer would want to know about. It is far wider than cash.

- Money for the post.
- Free product — including product that turned up unsolicited.
- A discount, a special price, a gift, a hamper or a seeding box.
- Entry into a contest or a giveaway.
- A trip, a hotel stay or an event invitation.
- A barter — product or service against coverage.
- Commission you earn on sales, through a link or your storefront.
- Any business or personal relationship — a job, a retainer, a stake in the brand, or a family tie.

Something you bought yourself, at full price, with no arrangement, does not count. If you are unsure, disclose — nobody is penalised for labelling a post that did not strictly need it.

## 9.3 How to label the post

The label must be **clear and visible** — at the start of the post, where a viewer sees it before they engage with the content. On an image or a video, put it on the visual itself, large enough to read on a phone.

A label only in your bio, only at the end of a long caption, hidden behind a "more" click, or buried among hashtags is not disclosure. Where the platform gives you a paid-partnership tool, switch it on as well.

Use wording that plainly says the post is paid for. Commonly accepted labels include:

- **Advertisement** or **Ad** — the post is a paid advertisement.
- **Sponsored** — the brand paid for the post.
- **Collaboration** or **Partnership** — you and the brand worked together, or have an ongoing arrangement.
- **Affiliate** — you earn commission on sales through your links or storefront.
- The platform's own paid-partnership tag, such as Instagram's **Paid Partnership** or YouTube's **Includes Paid Promotion**.

Write the label in the language of your post. Wording you invent — "thanks to", "in association with" — does not do the job, because it does not tell the viewer the post was paid for.

## 9.4 When you must disclose

Disclose **even if the brand never asked you to mention the product**. The duty comes from the connection, not from the instruction. Disclose **even if the opinion is genuinely your own** — liking something you were given does not remove the connection.

- On every post in a campaign, not only the first one.
- On reposts, on short cuts taken from a longer video, and on stories that repeat the content.
- On affiliate content, and on your INFLX storefront listings where you earn commission.

Your opinion also has to be real. It must be what you actually think, reasonably current, and based on real experience with the product or adequate information about it.

Both the brand and the creator are responsible. Neither can point at the other and walk away.

| The creator | Label the post clearly, place the label where it will be seen, and keep the opinion genuine. |

| INFLX | Publish these rules, act on what is reported to us, and remove or restrict content that stays non-compliant. |

# 13. How Ranking & Recommendations Work

**Applies to:** Shoppers browsing INFLX, and the brands and creators whose products appear.  
**Shown in:** Follower app, Brand app + web dashboard  
**Web address:** inflx.in/legal/ranking

> **In short:** What you see on INFLX is a mix of rows our team organises, deals creators choose to publish, and what is relevant where you are. Anything paid for is labelled. Paying us more does not move anything up.

## 13.1 Why we are telling you this

When you open INFLX, something is at the top and something is four screens down. That order is not an accident, so you ought to be able to find out how it was decided.

We publish this because we think you are owed it. If you read something here that does not match what you see in the app, tell us — the gap is a bug in one of the two, and we want to know which one.

## 13.2 What you are actually looking at

What appears on your screen comes from three places at once. Some of it is organised by us — rows and collections our team sets up, each with its own rules about what belongs in it. Some of it is chosen by creators, who publish the deals they want on their own storefronts. And some of it is simply what is relevant to you, mostly the city you are browsing in.

Once a row is set up it fills itself from whatever is live and matches, so what you see changes as brands add products, start offers and sell out — even on a day nobody touched anything.

## 13.3 What influences what you see

In plain language, these are the things that decide whether a product turns up in a row and where:

- The **category and type** of the product — food, fashion, furniture, beauty and so on.
- The **price**, and whether it falls inside the range a row was set up for.
- Whether there is an **offer running** on it, and what kind.
- Your **city** — rows can be switched on, switched off or filled differently by city.
- Whether **our team has featured it** — somebody chose to put it there.
- What a **creator has published** to their own storefront.
- Whether it is **still available** — live, in stock, and the offer has not ended.

Featured means exactly that and nothing more. It does not mean the brand paid us, and it does not mean we inspected the product or vouch for it.

## 13.4 What does not influence it

People assume some of these are levers. None of them are.

- **Paying us more.** The platform fee a brand pays does not buy position. There is no auction and no way to outbid another brand for a spot.
- **Wallet balance or campaign spend.** A brand with a large wallet does not rank above a brand with a small one.
- **How much commission a creator earns.** A higher commission does not push a product up for shoppers.
- **Who you are.** Your name, gender, age, phone number and payment history are not ranking inputs.
- **Anything from outside INFLX.** We do not buy data about you and we do not follow you across other apps and websites.
- **Your messages.** Chats, negotiations and support tickets do not feed into this.

> **Paid placements are labelled**
> If a brand has paid for a position, that position carries a label saying so, wherever it appears. There is no unlabelled paid placement on INFLX. If you ever see one, it is a mistake, and telling us is doing us a favour.

**Prices are set by the brand and are the same for everyone who sees that listing.** Nothing on this page changes what you are charged.

## 13.5 Creator storefronts work differently

A creator’s storefront is not ranked by us. It shows what that creator chose to publish — the deals they accepted and pushed to their own store. If a deal is not there, it is because the creator did not put it there.

A creator earns commission on sales that come through their storefront and share links, so they have a reason to publish what they believe their audience will actually buy. That is the incentive, and it is worth reading a storefront with it in mind.

A creator posting about a brand they have a paid connection with has to say so on the post itself, clearly and upfront. If you see a post that reads like an ad and carries no label, tell us.

If you are a shopper and a row makes no sense to you, or you are a brand and want to know why your product is not showing, ask. Write to contact@inflx.in or +91 86394 48121 with what you were looking at, and we will tell you what put it there or kept it out.
