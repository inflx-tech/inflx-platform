Pay for Sale, from the first listing to the first rupee

Left: what the brand does. Right: what the creator does. The middle says what passes between them. INFLX is only a tag inside a box where it approves or pays. The story ends in the shopper app, because the deal they make is only real when a shopper buys through her post.

Meera Fashions — the brandWomen's fashion, Hyderabad. Website for listings and offers; brand app for everything with creators.
Asha Menon — the creator42,000 Instagram followers. Creator app only.
Priya — the shopperFollows Asha on Instagram. Follower app; no login until she buys.

Pay for Sale means: no fixed money. Asha earns a commission on every sale through her posts, for as long as they sell. Where Pay for Content or Hybrid differ, a dashed grey line says so. Those are the only differences.
