The creator app and admin, in numbers
42 creator screens  ·  5 admin screens.
NEW 9  ·  CHANGE 26  ·  KEEP 9  ·  SWEEP 3

Across both apps: 62 brand + 42 creator + 5 admin = 109 screens with a permanent address, each drawn once.

The creator app's new screens are not the same shape as the brand's. His were dominated by the set — a middle layer that does not exist. Hers are dominated by money she can see but cannot reach: SC-33 explains a returned sale, SC-34 is the withdraw screen her Home already links to, and SC-29 splits one number into "ready" and "not yours yet".

Her second cluster is being told what happened to her work — SC-26 when a shop post is sent back, SC-16 when she goes live per channel, SC-12 when a remote shoot cannot start without her address.
