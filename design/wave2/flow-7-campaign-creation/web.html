Step 2 · Sub-step 3 of 5

Who will post this?

Two ways to find influencers for this set. Pick whichever feels right — you can change before publishing.
★
Handpick influencers
You choose each person yourself
Browse our roster, filter by niche / city / followers / ER, pick the exact people you want. You'll set individual terms (pay, offer) for each.
  • Precise control over who represents your brand
  • Set different pay for different influencers
  • Best if you already know your audience
⊞
Open group (broadcast)
Influencers matching your filters apply
Set criteria (tier · niche · city · engagement). We invite everyone who matches — first N to accept get the slots. Same terms for all.
  • Fast — no manual picking needed
  • Great for bulk campaigns with a fixed cap
  • Best when you don't care who, as long as they fit
