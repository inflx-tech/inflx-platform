Drawn from your "Followers app skeleton ideas" with the recommendations you accepted on 28 September, in the same visual language as your own "Post Code and Links" screens (black, white, INFLX yellow). Rules: Docs/OFFER_TERMS_POSTS_RULES.md §5. Not drawn yet: Discover (you'll explain it), and checkout, returns, addresses and pay at store (next discussion).

NEW every screen here is new; the shopper app is profile-only today
Rules built into these screens
  • Bottom bar: Home · Discover · ▶ Play · Saved · Profile. Search and the bag (with count) sit top-right on every screen.
  • Every purchase goes through a post: Shop this post on every post, and the brand's Offers page leads to posts.
  • Guests browse, follow and save (kept on the phone). Liking and commenting need sign-in.
  • Her city comes from the 146 offered cities. No exact location, ever. "Near" = a range around the city centre, set by admin.
  • Feed at launch: followed creators first, then newest, in her categories. Never the same creator twice in a row, no repeats, no sold-out or removed posts, 15% of slots for new posts.
