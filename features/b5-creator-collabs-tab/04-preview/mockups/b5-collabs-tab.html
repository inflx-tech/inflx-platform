What finishes a collaboration, per the founder:
Pay per sale — done when her content is live on social. The campaign itself runs on; sales keep arriving and keep paying her.
Pay for content — done when the final payment lands, after the admin-set wait.

Two aims, two endings, and neither of them is "when the deliverables run out" — which is why nothing could compute it before.

Three things in her app that are broken or missing today, all recorded.

All three now have answers — and all three need one thing: a place to record a live post that is not a deliverable.

1 · Live links, two rules. Pay for content — one per thing they bought, all required, all confirmed by the brand, and the second payment waits on them. Pay per sale — one link, for reference, gating nothing. Both need a home, and a commission creator has no deliverable to hang one on.

2 · Two endings, not one. Pay per sale is done when her content is live; the campaign runs on and keeps paying her. Pay for content is done when the final payment lands. Neither is "when the deliverables run out" — which is why nothing could compute it.

3 · Where she works goes on the pitch, required, two answers. Not on her profile: it is a per-deal answer.
What B5 costs. Four new screens, eight changed, eight kept.
