For lock and the gap prompt

Rules behind these screens

TopicRule
The gateThe shoot opens only when both signatures exist and, for Pay for Content and Hybrid, the funds are locked and the first 25% has been released (§10). Pay for Sale opens on the signatures alone, because it has no fixed money. This is the rule §10 has always stated and nothing has ever enforced: today the only check is that both signatures exist, and two endpoints do not check even that.
Who starts itThe brand. The mode comes from the agreement and can never change inside the shoot plan (§11).
What the plan carriesThe branch, inherited from the set and not editable; the person to ask for and their number; the date and time; roughly how long; and anything to bring or know. The last two are optional and neither is negotiable.
The addressThe creator has only ever seen the branch’s city. The full street address is revealed when the plan is sent, before she agrees a time, so she can judge the journey first.
What can be negotiatedThe time, and nothing else. It runs through the same negotiation engine as items 6 and 8, with the same admin round limit stamped when it starts. There is no declining a time — the only alternative to accepting is proposing another.
The time pickerNever offers a date after the set’s posting date. A shoot cannot happen after the day it was to be posted, so the error is made impossible rather than explained.
ReschedulingEither side may move an agreed time before it happens. The same thread reopens rather than a new one starting, and the new time is still capped by the posting date.
The time passingIf the agreed time passes with nothing marked, neither side is blamed and nothing is recorded against anyone. The brand sends a new time; they sort out what happened between themselves.
Marking it doneThe creator marks that she attended. The brand confirms. If the brand does nothing for an Admin-controlled period, three days by default, it confirms itself — which is safe because completing a shoot moves no money and only opens the content step.
What it recordsBoth timestamps: when she marked it and when it was confirmed, or that it confirmed itself.
Past the posting dateA job marks the collaboration Delayed the day after the posting date if the shoot is not complete, records the stage it stopped at, and tells both sides. The shoot stays fully usable, nothing moves out of its set, and no money moves in either direction. Moving a delayed collaboration into its own set is a later item.
MoneyNothing in item 10 locks, releases or refunds anything. The first 25% was paid at signing; the rest waits for the content going live (§12).
Out of scopeTransport, travel routes and costs are deliberately outside the platform (§11.1). Remote is item 11.
PrivacyThe creator never sees the campaign description or the brand’s internal notes. The brand’s contact number is shown to her only once the plan is sent.
States on listsLoading, empty and couldn’t load, as in items 2–9.
