For lock and the gap prompt

Rules behind these screens

TopicRule
When it opensThe shoot is marked done — the Brand confirms a Store Visit, she marks a Remote shoot (§11). Pay for Content only; §12.1 is the Pay for Sale and Hybrid equivalent, in items 14 and 15.
What she owesThe deliverable rows on the agreement: a content type and a count. Each type names its platform (Instagram post, reel or story; YouTube video or short). The rows cannot be added to, removed or renegotiated after signing (§22).
What she uploadsPhotos (JPG, PNG, ≤ 10 MB) and videos (MP4, ≤ 200 MB) from her phone, at most 10 files against a row, plus an optional caption of ≤ 500 characters per row. Free to change until she submits.
SubmittingOne package covering every row. A row short of its count cannot be sent, because a partial package is non-compliant, never partial work (§22). Submitting locks it. Nothing is published anywhere before this.
The reviewOne verdict on the whole package: approve, or ask for changes with a required reason (≤ 500 characters) and optional notes pinned to individual files.
The roundsAn Admin number, two by default, stamped when she first submits. On the last allowed round the Brand is warned. Once used, the Brand may only approve or report to INFLX, which records a dispute (item 18). A third round never exists.
No deadlinesNeither side is on a clock in the review. Whoever must move is in Needs You and reminded every 48 hours, as in items 10 and 11.
ApprovalLocks the content. What she posts is what was approved.
PostingShe posts on the platforms the rows name and gives a public link for each. A Story is proved by a screenshot instead, because it disappears within a day. She then confirms she has posted.
Confirming liveThe Brand opens the links and confirms. That releases the second 25% and stamps the waiting period. There is no automatic confirmation here — the money is the Brand's and an unseen release would be wrong.
Brand silenceAfter an Admin number of days, seven by default, counted from her confirmation that she posted, she may tell INFLX. That records a dispute and moves no money by itself.
Posted something elseThe Brand does not confirm; it reports, which is a dispute. There is no third answer.
The final 50%A job releases it after the Admin waiting period stamped at the Brand's confirmation. Nothing is re-checked in that week; whether the content stays up is the agreement's usage terms, not a second confirmation.
CompletionThe collaboration is Completed when the final 50% is released (§19).
MoneyItem 12 releases the second 25% and the final 50% of the creator's fee from the amount locked at signing, and nothing else. The INFLX campaign fee was charged to the brand once at signing and is never part of her split, never deducted from her payments (§3). "Still locked" means her fee still in escrow.
Past the posting dateDelayed, as in items 10 and 11, with the stage: nothing submitted, in review, changes requested, approved but not posted, posted but not confirmed. Both told once; nothing moves.
Needs YouCreator: send your content; make the changes; post it and add the links. Brand: review the content; confirm it is live. Reminded at 48 hours.
PrivacyThe files belong to that collaboration: only that Brand and that Creator see them, and Admin only when a dispute is raised. They never appear in the shopper app and are never reused elsewhere.
Admin numbersNew: content change requests (2) stamped at her first submission; Brand silence before she may report (7 days) stamped when she confirms she posted. Existing: the final-payment waiting period (7 days) stamped at the Brand's confirmation.
Out of scopeShoppable posts and follower commerce (items 13–16), Hybrid's version of this step (item 15), dispute handling itself (item 18). Item 12 records a dispute and stops there.
States on listsLoading, empty and couldn't load, as in items 2–11.
