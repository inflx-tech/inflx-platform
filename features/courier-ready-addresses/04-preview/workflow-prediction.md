# Workflow prediction — Courier-ready addresses

What a person will see and do on the locked screens (`mockups/courier-ready-addresses.html`).

**A · Edit branch / Add a branch (brand app)**
1. The Street address box shows a line under it — "Shop or door number, building, street and
   area. A courier has to find it." — and a counter to 140.
2. Below it a new Landmark box (optional, counter to 45).
3. Pincode and State, side by side, as today. Then **City** (was "Nearest major city") with
   "Not on the list? Pick the nearest city." The list is the same 146 cities.
4. A new Store phone box, already holding the login number. It is required on the screen; a
   brand that never touches it keeps the login number.
5. The blue note now names the courier as well as the store visit.
6. Save branch is the one yellow button. With a street that has no shop or door number the red
   line under the box says so and nothing is sent.

**B · The red line** appears in the same words on brand registration (app and website), on
re-submit after rejection, on Add / Edit branch and on the website's profile page.

**C · Book the parcel (brand app)** — a branch a courier would refuse is greyed in "Collected
from" and reads "Needs a shop or door number" (a branch with no address at all still reads
"Needs a street address and pincode"). It cannot be picked.

**D · Your delivery address (creator app)** — the House and street box gets the hint "House or
flat number, building, street and area." and stops at 190 characters, with a counter.

**Not on any screen:** the courier is given the brand's name instead of "Main Branch", the store
phone, and the landmark as a second address line.

**Predicted questions from a first-time user**
- "My shop has no number." → the hint and the red line both say shop OR door number; a building,
  plot or ward number passes.
- "My town isn't in the City list." → the helper line answers it.
