Choosing creators, terms and invitations

The brand picks creators into a set, agrees what each one gets, and sends the invitations. The creator receives one invitation, with the brand's fixed terms and their own personal terms kept apart, and accepts, counters or declines. Based on rules doc v1.16 (§7A, plus §5, §5A, §5B, §7, §8A, §15.1, §21, §25). Negotiation is item 6 and the agreement is item 7. Names, numbers and dates are example data.

For lock and the gap prompt

Rules behind these screens

TopicRule
Who can be pickedApproved creators who pass the item 2 checks for this set: not paused, not blocking the brand, brand category matches, the set's Aim and mode are ones they accept, and for Store Visit the branch city is one they can visit. Creators already in the set are shown as invited and can't be picked again. A creator can be in more than one set of a campaign.
GroupsInviting a group runs the same check per member and lists who can't be invited, with a short label. The group is never shown to creators.
CutoffNothing can be sent after the set's cutoff (posting date − 3 days). The screens show the cutoff throughout.
Terms: Pay for SaleCommission % (required), what she can sell (whole store, categories or chosen products), follower offer (optional), minimum shoppable posts shown read-only from the admin setting.
Terms: Pay for ContentFee in whole rupees (required), deliverables as rows of type and count (at least one), optional note, usage rights off by default (duration 30/60/90 days or forever, and where). Payment split 25/25/50 shown as a fact.
Terms: HybridFixed fee (required, never ₹0) plus everything from Pay for Sale. No content package.
MinimumsA fee or commission below the creator's own minimum shows a warning on the terms screen and on Review, and never blocks sending (item 2, soft condition). The creator is never told the brand saw a warning.
Many creators at once"Use these terms for everyone" copies the current creator's terms to the rest; each creator can then be changed before sending. Every creator still gets an individual invitation and an individual agreement.
Follower offerThree choices: none, a saved offer from the brand's library, or a special offer for this creator only. A saved offer is copied onto the invitation, so later edits to the library never change a live deal. Special offers cover money off, buy more save more, and buy one get one; the "applies to" choice is hidden for buy one get one. Optional "also save to my offers". Dates default to the posting date with no end.
Offer previewBefore sending, the brand can see what a shopper pays and what the creator earns on an example product.
SendingSending makes the set live and locks its Aim, posting date, mode and branch (item 4). Each creator gets one invitation with the set terms and their own terms.
Creator's answerAccept, counter or decline, until the cutoff. No reply by then → Expired. Accepting moves to "waiting for the agreement" (item 7). Countering opens the negotiation (item 6). Declining asks an optional short reason, which the brand sees.
After sendingTerms can never be edited. The brand can withdraw an unanswered invitation, or remove a creator until the agreement is signed. A withdrawn or removed creator is told plainly, with no reason.
Grouping in the setWaiting to answer · Negotiating (item 6) · Accepted, waiting for the agreement · Signed · Asked for another date · Declined · Expired · Withdrawn and closed.
Asked for another dateA creator who asked for a different date has answered: she is not waiting, she is never expired with the set, and she sits in her own group until the brand puts her in another set. New invitations no longer offer this answer — a date that doesn't suit is a counter (item 6) or a decline.
Withdrawn and closedWithdrawing pulls back an unanswered invitation; removing takes a creator out until both signatures exist. The creator is told plainly, with no reason: “{Brand} withdrew this invitation” or “{Brand} is no longer going ahead with this collaboration.” Neither ever destroys a signed agreement or moves money.
Decline reasonsFee too low · Dates don't suit · Not my kind of brand · Too much work · Other. One is required; the note is optional and capped at 200 characters. The brand sees both, in the list and in the notification.
Shoppable postsOne, set by INFLX, shown read-only on Pay for Sale and Hybrid. It becomes an admin setting in the admin-parameters item.
CommissionMore than 0 and at most 100, to one decimal. Above 50% the brand is asked to confirm once, because it is more than half of every sale.
SendingOne action, all or nothing. If any creator fails, nothing is sent and the brand is told which ones, so she can fix or drop them and send again.
Never shownCreators never see the campaign description, the group they came from, other creators' terms, or the brand's warnings. Brands never see why a creator failed a check.
Small server fixes riding alongRecord which offer produced each sale, and respect a creator offer's start date.
States on listsLoading, empty and couldn't load, as in items 2–4.
