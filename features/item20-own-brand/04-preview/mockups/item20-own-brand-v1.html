A creator who owns a brand

A person who runs a store and also makes content for it links the two accounts once, then posts for his own store straight from the Create tab. No campaign, invitation, agreement, shoot plan or commission. INFLX still approves each post. Drawn from the screens the apps already have. Names, numbers and dates are example data.

The whole item

Four things

Link once

He says he runs a brand and proves it with that brand's own login code. If both accounts are on the same number, it is one tap.

Post straight away

Create, pick his brand, pick what to sell. No campaign, invitation, agreement, shoot plan, minimum or posting date.

INFLX still approves

Every post goes through the same INFLX check as any other. The brand approves nothing, because the brand is him.

A normal sale, without commission

INFLX takes its usual share. The rest goes to the brand's wallet after the return window. He records where he shared the post, to see which channel sells.

Yours to call

Five decisions

Each is drawn the way I recommend. Say “lock” to take all five, or tell me which to change.

D1
Price posts carry only products that are not in an offer Yours to call

This is today's rule for collaborations. A price post always charges the normal price, so a product that is in a running offer would show at full price in his price post while the offer sells it cheaper.

Recommended, and drawn as: Keep the rule. A product in an offer is sold through an offer post.
D2
A story needs no link and no screenshot Yours to call

A story is gone in a day and has no lasting link. The collaboration step asks for a screenshot because the brand must confirm it; here nobody confirms.

Recommended, and drawn as: Place and date only for a story. A link for the other five places.
D3
The record is for own-brand posts only Yours to call

His posts for other brands already have a social step, where that brand confirms.

Recommended, and drawn as: Own-brand posts only for now. Offering the same record on every post is a small later step.
D4
Creators already on INFLX are not asked Yours to call

New creators get the question at sign-up.

Recommended, and drawn as: No pop-up. Existing creators see the new Profile row and nothing else.
D5
Linked creators is on the brand phone only Yours to call

The website shows the notice in its bell.

Recommended, and drawn as: Phone only for now. The website's Profile says the list is managed in the app, as it already does for Branches.
