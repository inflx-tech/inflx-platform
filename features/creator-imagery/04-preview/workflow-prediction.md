# Workflow Prediction — Per-context Creator Imagery (Phase 7)

Predicted behaviour the mockups demonstrate (confirmed in 06-diff; 7b mobile via Maestro, batched).

## Creator upload (`creator-upload.html`, influencer app)
1. In the Studio / a product row → **Add your photo**.
2. Upload a shot → it auto-detects aspect → **tag which product** it's of.
3. My Shots: each shot shows Pending / Approved / Rejected.
4. Approved shots feed the creator's experiences + storefront.

## Brand image choice (`brand-image-choice.html`, brand-web/brand app)
1. **Creator Images** queue — approve / reject each creator shot (thumbnail + product + creator).
2. On a product → **Whose photo shows here?**: radio Own · Rahul · Komal (only approved creators listed).
3. Pick Rahul → the brand store now shows Rahul's shot for that product; the creator's own store always shows their own.

## Follower (the payoff)
- **Rahul's storefront:** the products wear Rahul's photos.
- **Brand's store:** each product wears whatever the brand chose.
- **Rahul's Street Style experience:** renders with his real photography, aspect-picked per layout.

## What this proves
The four-way imagery model works: one product, many contexts, brand-controlled, creator-owned — the piece the whole "Creator Commerce" pitch rests on. And with the flag off, every store looks exactly as it does today.
