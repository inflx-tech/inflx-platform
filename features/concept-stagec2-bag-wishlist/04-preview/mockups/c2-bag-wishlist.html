What this needs underneath (honest map)

Exists already (reused, no change):
· Bag = live cart rails (CartContext, offer preview engine, guest addresses v209, Place Order gate)
· Product data + effective prices from PDP endpoints

New backend (small):
· follower_wishlist_items (follower_id, product_id, created_at)
· follower_wishlist_collections + item↔collection link (one item can sit in many collections)
· Endpoints: list (live product join — dead products flagged), add/remove, collections CRUD, assign

Guest rule (locked law): wishlist works logged-out too — device-local first, synced to the account at the same one login moment (identical to guest addresses).

Entry points wired in this slice: heart on PDP · heart on shop-sheet cards · “Move to Wishlist” in bag.
