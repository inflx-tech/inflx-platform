Which page?

“Default” keeps today’s behaviour (this shelf’s See-all opens the product grid). Choosing A page sends the section tap to that custom page’s own section stack.

When “Category browser” is selected the sub-picker becomes a category-node picker instead of a page dropdown. “Default” / “Product grid” show no sub-picker.
