## Answers to the three probe questions (01-spec §7)

1. **Instagram post URLs in `username[]` work.** Both the `/reel/` and the `/p/` form were read. The reel came
   back with `url` in `/p/<shortcode>` form, so matching must be by `shortCode`, never by the URL string.
   Per result **US$0.0027**, no per-run fee (3 × 0.0027 = 0.0081 on the account). A URL that does not exist
   answers an item `{ url, error: 'not_found', errorDescription: 'Post does not exist' }`.
2. **YouTube: `/shorts/`, `youtu.be/` and `watch?v=` are each read on their own** — no rewriting needed. Items
   carry `id`, `type` (`shorts` / `video`), `viewCount`, `likes`, `commentsCount`, `commentsTurnedOff`, `date`,
   `duration`, `channelUrl`, **`channelUsername`, `channelId`** (the store page said these were absent; they
   are present), `collaborators`, `isPaidContent`. Per video **US$0.004**, no per-run fee. A non-existent id
   answers **nothing** — three items for four URLs — so a missing answer is read as `not_found` by set
   difference on `id`, never by position. 143 s for four URLs: the daily job must allow minutes, not seconds.
3. **Not-found shapes** are the two above. A **private** post was not probed (no safe target); the build treats
   any error item whose text says private as `private`, as item 3b does for profiles.

## Two things the probe found that the spec did not expect

- **Hidden likes are real on a campaign-style reel:** `likesCount: -1` on `DbnfYVVv8f-` (847,937 plays,
  703 comments, `paidPartnership: true`). The "Likes hidden" state is not an edge case.
- **A post on her own profile can be owned by someone else.** `Dc1GyQhhwFF`, taken from her Level 1 posts
  fixture, answers `ownerUsername: 'vishnu_singareddy'` with `coauthorProducers` and `taggedUsers` filled: a
  collaborative post. The ownership check (P8) must count her as the owner when her handle is the
  `ownerUsername` **or** appears in `coauthorProducers[].username`; only then is a mismatch flagged. This is
  why P8 flags and never refuses.
