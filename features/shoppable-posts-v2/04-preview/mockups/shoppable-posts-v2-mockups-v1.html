How a creator makes a shoppable post

Drawn in the creator app's own parts (the same ones as the invitation and content screens). The flow follows your posts document: brand → collaboration → offer post or price post → products → theme → format → media → caption and review. The Create tab, In review, Sent back and Your posts screens come from the locked screen atlas (SC-21, SC-25 to SC-27), updated for the new model. Rules: Docs/OFFER_TERMS_POSTS_RULES.md §4.

Rules built into these screens
  • One post = one brand, one collaboration, one offer (or none). Multi-brand and "everything you have" posts are gone.
  • Offer post: pick the offer type first (only types she has, with counts; empty types hidden), then one offer, then products from its pool.
  • Price post: only her products without offers. Pick a price band (5 set by INFLX), then categories, then products.
  • Theme tags: at least 1, at most 1 per group (Season, Occasion, Style, Use). Fixed once the post is live.
  • Category, offer and price band are filled in automatically. INFLX approves every post. A warning about copyrighted music shows at upload.
  • Posting opens after both signatures and the shoot plan (shoot-plan rules to come from the founder).
  • A post is saved as a draft automatically at every step until she sends it (added 28 Sep).

Creator app · Collections

Collections: create, see, edit

A collection is made from the Create tab, like a post. Exactly as you described: a name, then either her posts or her products, and nothing more. A collection can mix brands. No INFLX approval is needed. A product in a collection sells like a one-product post from the collaboration she picked it from.

Before you lock this board

Five questions these screens raised

Each has my recommendation, and the screens follow it. Reply "agree all" or name the ones to change. The post link and code screens come separately, from your screens.

1 · Can she edit or take down a live post?Tags are fixed once live (your rule). What about the caption, or removing the post herself?Rec: no edits once live, since INFLX approved exactly what's there. She can take a post down herself; it leaves the shop and keeps its earnings history.
2 · What can she change when a post is sent back?Rec: media, caption, products and theme tags. Not the brand, collaboration, post type or offer; for those she starts a new post (P13).
3 · LimitsToday's server allows 1–10 photos and up to 30 products per post.Rec: keep 10 slides and 30 products. Video up to 90 seconds per clip.
4 · Product tags on photos: required or optional?Rec: optional. Every picked product is in the post's product list anyway (P11a).
5 · Collections: editing, hiding, deletingSee P16–P20.Rec: she can rename, add or remove items, or delete a collection any time. A collection stays either posts-only or products-only. It hides itself from shoppers when nothing in it is still in the shop, and deleting it never touches her posts.
