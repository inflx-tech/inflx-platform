And building the missing half would not be enough. A creator who joined an open group carries no product allocation and no commission rate (a Part 4 leftover). Since Part 4, having something to sell is exactly what makes a creator visible to shoppers — so they would be approved, appear in the brand's roster, and be unable to sell anything.
Nothing is filling this gap. Brands already reach creators three ways, including automatic invitations to everyone matching their filters. The missing route is a creator spotting a live campaign and knocking — a fourth channel, not a hole in the flow.
Recorded, not left to rot. A test pins that no way to apply exists, so whoever builds it later does so deliberately. One loose end noted for them: publishing an open group notifies creators with “Apply now!” and links to a screen that exists in neither app. It has never fired — no open group has ever been created.
