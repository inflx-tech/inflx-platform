Stock asked once

Today a product can hold two stock numbers that disagree: the product's own "Stock" and the stock on its sizes. The Listings page shows the first; the shopper app obeys the second. Ten sunglasses showed "20 in stock" to the brand and "Out of stock" to shoppers. After this change stock is typed in one place only, and the total is added up for you. These are the only screens that change. Dashed red = removed. Green = new.

What does NOT change
  • The Listings page keeps its layout. Its "Stock" column simply becomes the total of the colours and sizes, so it always matches what shoppers can buy.
  • A product with sizes looks and works the same in the shopper app.
  • Bag, checkout and order pages still write "White · Free Size" on the line, as they do today.
  • Prices, offers, orders, returns and money are not touched.
  • The brand phone app and the creator app are not touched.
