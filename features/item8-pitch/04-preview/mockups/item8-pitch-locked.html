The creator asks first

Everything so far started with a brand. Here the creator goes to a brand and proposes a deal. The brand accepts, counters or rejects — and accepting agrees the money without creating a collaboration: she joins the brand's pool and waits for a set, exactly like a creator who asked to be moved in item 6. From there it is the ordinary path, so item 8 adds a front door and no new machinery. Based on rules doc v1.21 (§8, plus §7B, §8A, §5B, §7A, §9, §25). Names, numbers and dates are example data.

For lock and the gap prompt

Rules behind these screens

TopicRule
Who may pitchOnly where the brand’s pitch settings (item 2) allow it: accepting pitches, the Aim, the mode, niche, minimum followers and engagement, languages and cities, and not blocked. A brand that has worked with her before may allow her anyway. Only the Aims that brand accepts are offered.
What a pitch carriesAlways: the Aim, when she could post, how she would work, and a message. Pay for Sale: the commission, and optionally what she would like to sell. Pay for Content: the fee and the deliverables. Hybrid: the fixed fee and the commission. Never: a follower offer. Usage rights are not asked for and are settled in the agreement.
LimitsOne open pitch per brand per Aim, and at most three open pitches to any one brand. No limit across different brands.
How long it stays openThe brand has an admin-controlled period, thirty days by default, and is reminded at seven days and at twenty-one. A pitch also closes when her proposed posting date comes within seven days. Whichever comes first ends it. No reply is recorded as such, and she may pitch that brand again immediately.
The three answersAccept, counter or reject. Accepting agrees the money and creates nothing else.
NegotiatingItem 6’s thread, with her pitch as the first turn: the same admin round limit stamped at the start, whole terms in every turn shown old beside new, acceptance whole or not at all, and no automatic decline for silence.
What acceptance createsA pool entry (item 6), brand-level, holding the Aim, the agreed terms, the date and mode she asked for, and where she came from. She is not a collaboration and no money moves.
Out of the poolThe brand builds a set from the pool exactly as in item 6, and item 5 sends her an ordinary invitation with the agreed terms pre-filled and marked. She accepts, counters or declines, then signs the agreement (item 7).
Her settings, on the way backFor a set that matches what was agreed — same Aim, same mode — her Aim, mode, city, category and minimums are not applied against her, because her pitch is the more recent and more specific statement. For a set that differs, every setting applies as normal. A creator who has since paused or blocked the brand can never be invited, and the brand is told only that she is not available.
Rejection and the cooldownA rejection pauses her pitching that brand again: admin-controlled, thirty days by default, per brand and not per Aim, and she is told the date. It never stops the brand inviting her, and never stops her accepting. A brand that wants no further contact blocks her instead.
No cooldownA pitch she withdraws, one that closes unanswered, and one that closes because the date passed.
WithdrawingShe may withdraw at any time before the agreement is signed. After acceptance that means leaving the pool, which item 6 already allows for either side.
What each side seesThe brand sees her profile, her numbers (item 3b), her proposed terms and her message. She never sees the brand’s internal notes, its other pitches, or why she failed a setting.
Admin numbersThe response period and the cooldown join the item 6 platform settings page. Each is stamped when it is applied, so a later change never moves something already in flight.
States on listsLoading, empty and couldn’t load, as in items 2–7.
