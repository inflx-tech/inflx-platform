For the record

What these screens decide

They decide: the entry point, the order (old number first), the both-phones warning, the this-role-only warning, the three refusals and their words, and that confirming signs out every other session.

They don't decide: what happens when the old phone is lost — the team handles that offline, no self-service this version.
