Deals in orders: one card, one item replaced, one item bought alone

The rules are the ones you accepted on 5 Oct 2026. They are drawn here for your lock; nothing is built yet.

Decided by you
  • D1. A deal is cancelled and returned as one. My orders shows it as one card, with one Cancel and one Return. A plain discount stays one card per item.
  • D2. One item of a deal is not right (the size, damaged, not as shown): that item is replaced, the deal stays, no money moves. When it is the store's fault and the store cannot replace it: only that item's share is refunded and she keeps the rest. When she has changed her mind: the whole deal goes back, or she keeps it all.
  • D3. Never one piece back at the deal's price for no reason.
  • D4. One item of an offer can be bought alone at its normal price, with the offer shown beside it. No "alone" for buy one get one. A set stays whole unless the brand says its pieces may be sold alone. In the bag, a nudge to complete the offer.
Decided by me, for you to overrule
  • E1. Buy more save more counts as a deal, for the one card and for replacing: its items were priced together. Spend-or-buy-for-a-gift does not: its items keep their own prices.
  • E2. A replacement is asked for inside the same return window as a return, and INFLX decides it on the same page. One replacement per item; if that one is wrong too, she returns.
  • E3. For a size that doesn't fit she may choose any size of the same product that is in stock, in the same or another colour. Never a different product.
  • E4. "Damaged" and "not as shown" are the store's fault; "the size doesn't fit" is nobody's. So with no other size in stock she keeps it or returns the whole deal; one item's share is never refunded for a size.
  • E5. While a replacement is open, the store's, the creator's and INFLX's shares of the sale wait, as they do for an open return. They are added once the replacement is delivered and the return window, counted from that delivery, has closed.
  • E6. The store brings the replacement and collects the other item in the same visit. INFLX arranges no courier, the same as a return today.
  • E7. When the items in her bag from one post complete the offer, the offer applies by itself. She is not asked.
  • E8. The switch "Pieces can also be bought alone" is the brand's, for each set offer, off to begin with, on the brand website where the offer is built. Switching it on or off never changes an order already placed.
  • E9. No photos of a damaged item in this round: the shopper app cannot send a photo yet. She writes a note, and INFLX may call her.
  • E10. After a replacement is delivered, the return window starts again from that delivery. She can still return the whole deal, or have that item's share refunded if the replacement is damaged too. Not a second replacement of the same item.
  • E11. A replacement INFLX does not accept reads "Replacement not accepted" with INFLX's note. The deal stays; she may still return the whole deal while the window is open.
  • E12. Cash on delivery: a replacement moves no money, so nothing differs. A refund of one item's share is sent to her by INFLX directly, as a cash return is today.
Your answers
  • Q1 — answered on 5 Oct: the brand pays. The store pays to bring a replacement and take the other item back; the shopper is charged nothing. (As drawn.)
  • Q2 — still yours. The refund-policy sentence, which you put off on 5 Oct. A reminder is set for when this round is ready to release.
  • Locked by you on 5 Oct 2026. These screens are what gets built, in this order: buying one alone, My orders by deal, replacing one item.
