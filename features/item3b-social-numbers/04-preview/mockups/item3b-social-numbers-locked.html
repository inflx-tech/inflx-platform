Social numbers

How INFLX measures a creator's Instagram and YouTube through Apify, and where the numbers show: a card per platform on the creator profile, a detail screen for each platform, and a "Your numbers" screen for the creator with every state. Measuring starts only after admin approval. Profile numbers refresh weekly and the last 90 days of posts every 15 days. YouTube long videos and shorts are measured separately. Names and numbers are example data.

For lock and the gap prompt

Rules behind these screens

TopicRule
SourceApify (already configured in the backend). Instagram: profile + posts. YouTube: channel, plus its Videos tab and Shorts tab read separately. Public data only.
When it runsFirst run when admin approves the creator (nothing at registration) · after admin approves a changed handle · profile numbers every 7 days · 90-day posts every 15 days · a creator's "Check again", at most once a day per platform. Only approved creators are measured.
Profile numbers (weekly)Instagram: followers, following, total posts, verified, account type · YouTube: subscribers, total videos, total channel views
Post window (every 15 days)Every post published in the 90 days before the run, no cap. Suggested safety ceiling of 500 per platform per run: if reached, use the newest 500 and flag admin.
Stored per postPlatform post id, type (reel / photo / carousel · long video / short), published date, duration (YouTube), likes (empty if hidden), comments, views (reels, videos, shorts), link. Kept so averages can be recalculated and top posts added in a later version.
Instagram numbersPosts in 90 days and per week (posts ÷ 12.9) · post mix · last post date · avg likes (posts with hidden likes left out) · avg comments · avg reel views (reels only) · engagement = (avg likes + avg comments) ÷ latest followers. No top posts in 3b.
Long video or shortBy the channel tab Apify read it from (Shorts tab = short, Videos tab = long video), so YouTube decides. Fallback only if the tab is unknown: 3 minutes or less = short, longer = long video. Live streams are left out of every YouTube number.
YouTube numbersUploads in 90 days and per week · last upload · then separately for long videos and shorts: count, avg views, avg likes (hidden left out), avg comments, engagement = (all likes + all comments) ÷ all views of that type. Engagement uses views, not subscribers. No top uploads in 3b.
Formatting48.2K, 2.3M, 612 · percentages one decimal · "—" when there is nothing to average, never 0 · dates as 9 Sep
Discover and filtersNo change from item 3: cards, the Engagement filter and the engagement sort use Instagram engagement only. Followers and subscribers filters use the latest profile numbers.
Account statesWaiting for approval (creator not approved yet) · Waiting for INFLX check (changed handle) · Measuring · OK · No posts in 90 days · Not found · Private · Failing (last good numbers kept, retried next day, admin flagged after 3 failures in a row) · Not added
What brands seeOK and No posts: numbers. Waiting for INFLX check or Measuring: "Numbers are being updated". Not found or private: "Numbers not available", no reason. Failing: last good numbers with their date. Not added: no card.
Handle changeConfirm first. Old numbers removed from the profile immediately; old posts not used again. The new handle goes to admin to check ownership; it is measured only after admin approves. If admin rejects it, the creator is told to add the right account.
RegistrationAt least one of Instagram or YouTube. Format checked on the device and server. Nothing measured until admin approves; existence is found by the first measurement.
OwnershipChecked by admin at approval, as today, and again for every handle changed after approval. No code-in-bio step in 3b. Admin needs a way to approve or reject a changed handle (found in the gap check).
HistoryKeep a snapshot of the calculated numbers per run, so growth over time can be shown later. Not shown in 3b.
