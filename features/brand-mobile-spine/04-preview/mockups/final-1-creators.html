Its live consequence: 1 of 26 brands is currently pitchable — a restaurant with no address. Until the default is decided, the creator's whole outbound route has nothing to point at.
Every filter here is already enforced server-side on submit. Putting them on a screen changes nothing about the rules — it just lets a brand set them, and lets a creator see them before writing.
