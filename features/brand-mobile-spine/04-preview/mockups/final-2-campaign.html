Why the shell is so thin. Everything that used to sit at campaign level — budget, end date, who posts — either belongs to a set or was already settled at the agreement. What's left is a name and a start date.
A set is the unit of work. One aim, one group of creators, one scope of products, one offer. Two sets in a campaign are two independent arrangements that happen to share a name.
Step 1 · Pick the aim
LEDGER 64
This one choice decides every other field in the wizard. The card says what each aim actually commits you to.

Set 1
STEP 1 OF 4
The aim
What is this set for?
Sales NO MONEY UP FRONT
Creators earn a commission on what they sell. They drive the traffic — you're not buying posts. No budget, no end date, and a shopper offer is required.
Content YOU PAY A FEE
You buy agreed posts for an agreed fee. Nothing is being sold. Budget and end date are both calculated for you, and there's no shopper offer.
Both FEE + COMMISSION
A smaller fee, plus commission on sales. You specify deliverables and set a shopper offer.
You can't change the aim after creators are added — their agreements were made against it.
Each option states its commercial consequence, not just its name.64

The row that matters most: shopper offer. On a sales set the creator has nothing to sell without one — allocation gives her products, the offer gives her a reason for someone to buy. On a content set there's no shop involved at all, so asking for one would be nonsense.

Section ① is why this fits on a phone. The old wizard asked the brand to invent commission, dates and terms per creator. After the handshake all of that exists — this section shows it and offers one control.
Auto-join is the difference that matters. Store and category scopes pick up new products silently; hand-picked lists don't. A brand who hand-picks and then adds stock will wonder why she isn't selling it — so the option says so before they choose.
Publishing is blocked while any creator on a sales or both set has no allocation. Since Part 4, having something to sell is the gate for appearing in the shopping app — a creator with none would be contracted and invisible.
