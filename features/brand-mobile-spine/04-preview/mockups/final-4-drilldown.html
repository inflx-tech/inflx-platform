THE ACTION ITSELF
The rule that makes the ladder work. A number at any level is the sum of everything beneath it, plus anything owed at that level itself. So a badge on Campaigns is never a mystery — tap it and it decomposes, every time, until you reach a button.
You can also go straight there. Home → Needs you lists all six flattened, each row deep-linking to level 5. The ladder is for when you're exploring; the flat list is for when you're clearing.
What was missing from FINAL 2. Levels 2, 3, 4 and 5 — the whole running half. FINAL 2 stopped at the landing and the creation wizard, so a brand could build a campaign and then had nowhere to watch it happen.

The tab bar badge is the only number that spans both halves. 6 from Campaigns plus 4 from Creators. Home's Needs-you list flattens all ten, each row deep-linking straight to level 5 — so a brand can clear work without walking the ladder.

Where each kind of owed item is born

LevelItems that originate here
Creator stepAnswer a counter · answer a slot · resolve a shortfall · countersign · approve a reel · confirm live
SetOffer expiring or expired · a creator with no allocation · empty set · everything in scope out of stock
CampaignWallet short · never published · 30 days of zero sales
Creators tabCounters and pitches awaiting an answer · agreements about to expire
A badge never lies about depth. If Festive Season shows 4 and its two sets show 3 and 0, the missing 1 is the campaign's own — and it is listed at the top of level 2, not buried. Numbers that don't reconcile are how people stop trusting badges.
Ended campaigns carry no badge, even if something technically remains. Nothing owed on a finished campaign should pull attention from live work.

Build order

#SliceNote
1Step derivation, server-sideOne function both apps read. Levels 3 and 4 are meaningless without it.
2Needs-you roll-up, server-sideOne count per node. Computing it client-side would let the levels disagree.
3Level 2 and 3 — sets and creatorsBoth are lists over data that already exists
4Level 4 — the brand's spineMirrors the creator's; existing screens hang off it
5Level 5 linksWiring FINAL 3's screens onto their steps
Two things must be server-side. The step number and the needs-you count. If either is derived in the app, the brand's screen and the creator's screen will eventually disagree about the same collaboration — and that is the failure this whole spine exists to prevent.
