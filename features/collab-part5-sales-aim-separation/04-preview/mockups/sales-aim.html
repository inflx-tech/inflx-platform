The three clocks
Keeping these apart is what the whole part is really about.
ClockBelongs toSales campaignPaid campaign
When the creator must post the set post by 15 Sep post 5–25 Oct
How long selling runs the campaign ONGOING 25 Oct (derived)
When a discount expires the offer 30 Sep (optional) 30 Sep (optional)
The mistake this table prevents. Every set has a post-by date — sales sets included. Deriving the campaign's end from posting windows for all aims would have shut a sales campaign down on 15 Sep and pulled every saree from the app while stock remained. Posting and selling are different clocks, and it is now a test that fails loudly if anyone ever conflates them.
Offer expiry already works — delivered in Parts 1 and 4. A 20%-off offer can expire on 30 Sep inside a campaign that runs forever, and when it expires it stops both applying and advertising.
