How we handle them: one case, five steps

Not eighty separate fixes. One way of working that every row above goes through, so both sides always know where a complaint stands and INFLX works from one list.

Raise

“Tell INFLX” on every screen of a signed collaboration, for both sides, at every stage. A short list of reasons that fits the stage, a note, photos. When a step has been stuck too long, the system raises it by itself.

Hear

The other side is told exactly what was said and has a set number of days to answer with their own proof. Either side can say “we have sorted it out” and the case closes.

Hold

While a case is open, that collaboration's locked payments wait; nothing pays out by itself. The work can carry on, and her shop posts keep selling.

Decide

INFLX works from one list, Admin → Disputes, oldest first, with the whole file in one place: agreement, diary, content rounds, parcel record, money. It picks one decision and writes a reason both sides read.

Close

The money moves in the same step, tied to the case. The collaboration gets a named ending both sides see, the reminders stop, and the campaign can finish.

The decisions INFLX can make

A small fixed set, so every case ends in a way the apps understand.

DecisionWhat happens to the collaborationWhat happens to the money
Carry onThe complaint is closed with a reason. INFLX may set one new final date, or do the missing step in place of a side that stayed silent (confirm the visit, approve the content, confirm it is live).Nothing moves by hand. The normal payments run again.
Ended, in the creator's favourClosed by INFLX. Her work is treated as done.What is still locked goes to her, all of it or the part INFLX decides.
Ended, in the brand's favourClosed by INFLX. The brand owes nothing more.What is still locked goes back to the brand, all of it or the part INFLX decides.
Ended by agreement, or nobody's faultClosed by INFLX with both sides' consent, or for a reason outside their control.Split as the two agreed, or as INFLX decides.

Two clocks sit on top. One on the two sides: after a set number of days of silence the waiting side may raise it, and after a longer number the system raises it. One on INFLX itself: acknowledge within 24 hours, decide within 7 days, with late cases marked in the list.

Things that disagree today

Places where the two rules documents, the signed agreement, the public pages and the app say different things. A dispute would turn on each of them, so each needs one answer whatever we build.

Cash on delivery is the one with money at risk now. The master rules (17A) say INFLX collects the cash through the delivery partner. The Offers rules and the brand website say the store's courier collects it and the store owes INFLX the two shares. Today the store ships with its own courier and keeps the cash, and after the return window INFLX also adds the store's share and the creator's commission to their wallets. Nothing takes the shares back from the store. Cash on delivery is on, up to ₹5,000 a checkout.

Suggested order of work

The build sequence scopes item 18 as brand and creator disputes with an admin resolution screen. The same case list can take the rest afterwards without being rebuilt.

Round 1 · item 18

Brand and creator

  • The case: raise, hear, hold, decide, close
  • “Tell INFLX” on every collaboration screen, both sides
  • Silence clocks, and INFLX acting for a silent side
  • Admin → Disputes with the whole file
  • Money decisions of any size, tied to the case
  • Named endings, so collaborations and campaigns can finish
  • The existing five reports moved into the case list
Round 2

Shopper orders and wallet questions

  • “Need help with this order” for the shopper
  • Refund and close an order that shipped and never arrived
  • The store's answer on a return
  • A list of refunds that failed
  • “Ask about this” on a wallet line or a withdrawal
Round 3

Behaviour and live posts

  • Report a person or a post
  • Take an approved post out of the shop, and put it back
  • What suspending an account does to open work and money
  • Outcomes kept on each account's record
