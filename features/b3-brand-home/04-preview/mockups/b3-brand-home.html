The one genuinely new screen in B3. Today an empty Home says "no campaigns" and points at a laptop. ⚠️ Adding products is still web work and this says so — the honest answer, not a pretence that everything is on the phone.

What B3 costs. One new screen, three changed. The smallest chunk in the programme, and it comes last because Home is a summary of everything else.

The real find is a sentence, not a screen. "Create campaigns from the web dashboard" is the first thing a brand with no campaigns reads, and it has been wrong since Stage 2 shipped. A screen nobody re-read after the feature it described was replaced.

Home must stay a summary. Every number on it belongs to a tab, and every tap goes there. The moment Home starts holding work of its own, two screens begin disagreeing about the same count — which is the fault the six-step ladder was built to end.
