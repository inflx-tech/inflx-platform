Negotiation, and the pool

A creator who wants a different number counters, and the two sides trade a fixed number of rounds until someone accepts or declines. A creator who wants a different date or mode isn't countering at all — she's asking to be moved, and if the brand agrees she waits in the brand's pool until there is a set that fits her. Based on rules doc v1.17 (§7B, plus §7, §7A, §24, §25, §28). The agreement is item 7. Names, numbers and dates are example data.

For lock and the gap prompt

Rules behind these screens

TopicRule
What can be counteredPay for Sale: commission. Pay for Content: fee, deliverables (types and counts) and usage rights. Hybrid: fixed fee and commission. Never: the Aim, the follower offer, the minimum shoppable posts, or what she may sell.
Whole terms each turnEvery turn carries the complete set of negotiable terms for that Aim, so the screen shows the previous value beside the proposed one. A counter is accepted whole or not at all.
AcceptingAccepting writes the agreed terms onto the invitation itself. The agreement (item 7) is always generated from the invitation, never from the thread, which is kept for reference.
RoundsA round is one counter. The limit is an admin setting, two by default, between one and five, and is stamped on the negotiation when it starts. After the last counter the receiving side may only accept or decline. Changing the setting never moves the goalposts for a negotiation already running.
TimeNo time limit on a reply and no automatic decline for silence. The set’s invitation cutoff is the only deadline: an open negotiation ends when the invitation expires. Whoever must move sees it in Needs You with reminders.
DecliningThe creator declines with one of the five invitation reasons. The brand may decline with an optional short reason, which the creator sees. Either ends the invitation, and the brand may invite again with new terms while there is still time.
Date or modeNever countered, because a set has one date and one mode for everyone. The creator asks instead. The brand may agree, keep the date (the invitation carries on unchanged and she may still accept or decline it), or decline.
The poolAgreeing keeps the money terms, closes the invitation as moved to the pool, and puts the creator in the brand’s pool — not a campaign’s. An entry records the creator, the Aim, the agreed terms, what she asked for and where she came from.
Using the poolReached from the top of the Campaigns tab and from inside any set’s creator picker. Grouped by what the creators asked for. Creating a set from a selection fills in the Aim, the date and the mode; the terms screen fills in the agreed money. Only entries with the same Aim carry their terms across.
Always a fresh invitationA creator from the pool is invited to the new set like anyone else, with the agreed terms pre-filled and marked as such, and her invitation settings checked again at that moment. She accepts, counters or declines.
Pool lifetimeAn entry lasts 30 days by default (admin setting), then closes and the creator is told. Either side may remove it earlier. A pool entry never blocks any other invitation.
Negotiation switchOne switch per campaign, on by default, changeable at any time. It governs only new counters; a negotiation already open always runs to its end. When off, the creator sees Accept and Decline only, with one line saying why.
Where it livesCreator: under Invites until the agreement arrives, with Your move or Waiting. Brand: inside the set, with “Your move” first, then Negotiating.
Minimum warningsA brand counter below the creator’s own minimum shows the same amber warning as at invitation time and never blocks.
Nothing movesNo money is locked or paid at any point in a negotiation, and nothing is committed until both signatures exist on the agreement.
States on listsLoading, empty and couldn’t load, as in items 2–5.
