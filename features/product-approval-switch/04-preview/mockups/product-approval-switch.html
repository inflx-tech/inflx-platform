Product approval — the admin switch

One switch. When it is on, a brand's published product waits for INFLX before it is in the store; the admin approves or sends it back with a reason. Five frames: the switch, the queue, the send-back dialog, what the brand sees, and the wizard's finish.

Drawn by the existing switch renderer. Ships off. Turning it off while products are waiting asks first, because they go live.

Product approval
When this is on, a product a brand publishes waits for INFLX before it is in the brand's store, in any creator's list or in any offer. You approve it or send it back with a reason from Product Queue. A product approved once stays approved; hiding and showing it again needs no second look. Products already live when you switch this on are not touched. When you switch it off, every product still waiting goes live at once.

Turn product approval off?

Every product still waiting for approval goes live in its brand's store right now. From then on a brand's published product is live at once.

You can switch it back on at any time; nothing already live is touched by that.
