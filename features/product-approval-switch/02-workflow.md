# Workflow — product approval

## The admin turns it on

1. Admin → **Settings → Platform** → Edit → **Product approval** switch on → Save.
2. The audit line reads *product approval off → on*. Nothing already in any store changes.

## The brand publishes

3. Brand → Dashboard → Products → lists four jeans through the wizard → **Publish & go live**.
4. The finish screen says **Sent to INFLX for approval** · *Your products go live in your store
   once INFLX approves them.*
5. On the products list each shows the blue pill **Waiting for INFLX approval**. They are not in
   the brand's store, not in any creator's product list, cannot be put in an offer's live set.

## The admin decides

6. Admin sidebar → **Product Queue** (badge: 4) → *Waiting* tab lists the four, with brand, photo,
   price, category and *sent 2 minutes ago*.
7. **Approve** on three → each becomes live at once; the brand gets *"Slim jeans is live in your
   store"*; its creators (if any hold it) get the usual *back on sale* notice.
8. **Send back** on the fourth → a reason is required: *"The photo is a stock image."* → the brand
   gets *"Wide jeans was not approved: The photo is a stock image."*

## The brand fixes and re-sends

9. Products list: **Sent back** pill, reason under the name. The brand edits the photo, then flips
   **In store** on → the pill reads *Waiting for INFLX approval* again; the queue shows it again.
10. The brand changes its mind and flips In store off while waiting → the product leaves the queue
    (*withdrawn*); the pill reads Hidden.

## The admin turns it off

11. Settings → Platform → switch off → a confirm says every waiting product goes live now →
    Turn off. Every `pending` product is released, their brands told, the audit line reads
    *on → off*. From now on publishing is immediate again.

## Nothing else moves

- An approved product hidden and shown again never re-enters the queue.
- A product listed while the switch was off is `approved` and untouched by turning it on.
- Orders, offers, posts, money: untouched. A waiting product behaves exactly like a hidden one.
