Before

After — searching

KPHB — Kukatpally, Hyderabad

After — failed
kphb
Search isn't responding right now. Retry or switch to Maps link or Manual.
Why search looked broken. Verified live from the inflx.in origin: CORS passes and the endpoint returns 5 real predictions — but the first call timed out at 30s and the second took 8.2s. The backend cold-starts. With no spinner and no error branch, a slow lookup was indistinguishable from a dead field. The Maps-link path felt fine because it has a button with a visible spinner — and needs no Google key at all, since coordinates are read straight out of the URL.

This does not make search faster. It makes the wait legible.

Business type. Shopping & Retail collapses to one option — fashion — because that schema already carries Footwear, Jewellery, Accessories and Bags as sections. Tradeoff: a footwear-only brand loses the granular Men's Casual/Formal/Sports/Kids sections and gets one "Footwear" section.
