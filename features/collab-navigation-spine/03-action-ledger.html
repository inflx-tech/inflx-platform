The reading rule. If an action is EITHER, both apps get the identical screen — same buttons, same words, same diff. If it is BRAND or CREATOR, the other side sees its state and no button. That single rule removes most of the design questions.
Tally: 160 actions. 77 work today · 21 have a working backend and no screen · 22 change behaviour · 39 are new · 1 is removed. By owner: brand 52 · creator 31 · either 24 · system 20 · admin 4. Roughly three-quarters of this programme is arrangement and rewiring, not new construction.
Ten of the twenty-one "needs UI" rows are brand-side approach — groups, bulk-add, asking a group, broadcasting, the reach preview. That whole capability was built and tested in Part 2 and has never been usable by anyone on any surface. It is the single largest block of finished-but-invisible work in the system.

The biggest single finding in this ledger. Fourteen actions have a working backend and no screen anywhere — and ten of them are the brand's approach tools: groups, bulk-add, asking a group, broadcasting, and the reach preview. That capability was built and tested in Part 2 and has never been usable by anyone on any surface.
Two hard dependencies, worth building before their screens. #50, the diary — only the latest counter is stored, so no history screen can be drawn on either app until it exists. The product picker — four separate flows need "choose products, grouped by category, with images"; building it four times is how they drift apart.
