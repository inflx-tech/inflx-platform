Removed: the block. “Save this set” was greyed out and “Choose a shopper offer, or build one.” sat under it in red until you picked one.
Nothing underneath ever required it. campaign_sets has no offer columns at all — checked against the live database. The offer lives on campaign_influencers, per creator, and is nullable. The wizard invented the rule; the data never had it.
Still Batch B, not this one: moving the offer to creator level, and bringing the phone's builder up to the web's. Those need screens drawn and agreed first.
Live bug, unfixed until Batch B: every offer built on the phone is silently a whole-store offer. buildDiscount hardcodes scope: 'store'. Ask for “20% off sarees” and you get 20% off everything. It is safer to leave the offer optional than to make people build one through a builder that does this.

Two vocabularies, half the words colliding. Rebuilt properly, not patched.
Deliberately not touched: anything a pay-for-content set needs. Its posting window, its budget and its “what you are buying” counts all stay. Your screenshots were all commission sets, and that is the only path these removals apply to.
What to check when you walk it: build one commission set and one pay-for-content set. The commission one should feel short. The content one should be exactly as it was — if anything is missing there, that is the bug this batch could introduce.
