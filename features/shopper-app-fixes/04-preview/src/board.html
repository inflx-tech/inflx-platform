The rest

Fixed with nothing new to see

These eight change what the app does, not how a screen looks, so there is nothing to draw.

FixTodayAfter
F2 · a number starting with 91A mobile number that starts with 91 cannot sign in ("phone must be a valid Indian mobile number").The ten digits she types are always sent as +91 and those ten digits.
F3 · staying signed inAfter about 15 minutes a signed-in shopper is silently treated as a guest on the pages a guest may also see: her likes, saves and follows look empty and Comments asks her to sign in.The app renews her sign-in by itself when it has run out, so she stays herself everywhere. If it cannot be renewed because she was signed out elsewhere, she becomes a guest quietly (no "Session expired").
F5 · coming back to PlayPlay starts again from the first video of a fresh list every time she comes back to it (from comments, a store, the bag, a product page), and the video she tapped is no longer first.Coming back to Play, she is on the video she left.
F7 · Comments after sign-inAfter signing in from Comments she lands back on Comments still reading "Sign in to like and comment".Comments shows her as signed in, with the box to write.
F14 · likes and heartsA like, a bookmark or a heart she has just given looks undone on the next screen. In a product collection a signed-in shopper never sees her hearts.What she liked, saved or hearted stays so on every screen until she undoes it. Collections show her hearts.
N5 · lists carry onCreator store, brand store, offer posts, My orders and Comments stop at the first 20 (comments 30).The next ones load as she reaches the end of the list, as the feed does. No "Load more" button.
N7 · card and post titlesA deal card reads "₹999 · " for a product with no sizes and "1 sizes" for one size. A shared post or a code match can show the internal titles "Price post" / "Untitled post"."₹999", "1 size". A post with no caption is titled by its first product ("Blue polo and 2 more").
N8 · two sentencesThe refund policy in the app says cancelling needs no reason; Cancel requires one.
