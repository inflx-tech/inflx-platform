# Workflow prediction — shopper-app-fixes (the screens on the board)

One line per screen on the board: what she does → what she sees. Nothing here is a new design; every screen keeps the look of the locked shopper board.

- **A1** · She taps Place order, then closes the payment page without paying → she is back on Review order with her items listed, the red line "Payment cancelled. Nothing was charged." and Place order still there; her bag is as it was, and the items held for her are let go at once, so she can try again straight away.
- **A2** · She pays, but the payment arrives late, after the items held for her were let go and the last one was bought by someone else → she stays on Review order and reads "It sold out while you were paying. Your payment is being refunded."; she never sees "Order placed" for it.
- **A3** · A new shopper types the code she was sent → she is asked "Your name" once: one box and Continue. Someone the app still calls "Follower" is asked the next time she signs in.
- **A4** · As a guest she taps Continue in her bag and goes to sign in → the screen says "Sign in to place your order"; if 30 seconds pass, "Resend code" appears under the code boxes.
- **A5, A5b** · She looks at a product that has sold out in every size → in "Products in this post" its tile reads "Out of stock" and has no bag icon; on its size sheet every size is crossed out, the sheet says "Sold out in every size", and both buttons are grey.
- **A6** · In a creator's store she taps a video → it opens full screen on top with a Back arrow top-left; Back puts her in the store again, where she was.
- **A7** · She opens her bag at a moment it cannot be read → "Couldn't load your bag" with Try again; it is never shown as empty, and nothing she adds can wipe what she had.
- **A8** · On a size sheet she taps XL, a size with its own price → the price on the sheet becomes XL's price, and the bag later shows the same price with no "Price changed".
- **A9** · She opens the app and Welcome cannot load → a sentence saying so, with Try again and Skip, instead of a wheel that never stops.
- **A10** · On "Your deal" she removes the only item → she is back on the deal page, which reads "Nothing picked yet"; the item is gone there too.
- **A11** · She opens Profile → Orders reads "2 on the way", Following reads "3 creators", there is a Notifications row, and Returns reads "Return an item from My orders" and opens My orders; with no name given, her name line reads "Add your name".
- **A12** · She taps Following → a plain list of the creators she follows; tapping one opens that creator's store; with none it reads "You don't follow anyone yet".
- **A13** · She taps Notifications, or a notification that arrives on her phone → the list, newest first, unread ones in bold; tapping one opens that order.
- **A14** · In Saved › Products she wants to clear a product whose post has ended → it shows the grey box and a Remove she can tap; every saved product has Remove.
- **A15** · In My orders she searches for something she never ordered, or picks a filter with nothing under it → "Nothing matches", not "No orders yet"; Packed and Out for delivery are among the filters; on Order details a cash order reads "To pay ₹899 on delivery" until it is delivered.
- **A16** · She opens her bag after choosing a free gift → the gift is there once, as the green "Free gift" line; the Delivery line shows the real amount, "Free" only when it is 0.
- **A17** · She opens "Products in this post" on a post that has since ended → "This post is no longer in the shop", not "Check your connection".
- **The table** · Eight more fixes she feels but cannot see (F2, F3, F5, F7, F14, N5, N7, N8): a number starting with 91 signs in, she stays signed in, Play is where she left it, Comments knows she signed in, likes and hearts stay, lists carry on, titles read right, two sentences are corrected.
