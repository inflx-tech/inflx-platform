# Workflow prediction — Universal Share Links

On an influencer store, brand store, product page, or offer, the follower taps
**Share** (or the ↗ icon). The native share sheet opens with a clean link
(`inflx.in/s|b|p|o/<id>`). They send it; the recipient opens it and (with the
app's native link handler) lands on that exact store/product/offer. Today the
copy/share works immediately; the in-app open arrives with the next app build.
