For lock and the gap prompt

Rules behind these screens

TopicRule
The gateThe same function as item 10, on every path: signed by both and, for Pay for Content and Hybrid, funds locked and the first 25% released (§10). Pay for Sale opens on signatures alone.
Who starts itThe creator, with her delivery address. The brand has nothing to do until it arrives. The mode comes from the set and never changes; Remote and Store Visit actions refuse each other.
Her addressName; contact number pre-filled with her login number, editable, a valid Indian mobile; the four-part address of §2B with state and city pre-filled from her profile and editable. Optional: delivery note ≤ 200, sizes ≤ 100.
Pincode checkChecked against the courier network before sending. No forward delivery: cannot send, told why. No return pickup only: can send; the brand is warned before marking anything Return.
The lockThe address locks when sent (§28) and never follows a profile change. One correction is allowed, only after the courier returns the parcel because the address was wrong.
PrivacyOnly that brand sees her address, number and sizes, on that collaboration, after she sends them. She never sees the brand's street address; the return label carries it.
The productsPay for Sale and Hybrid: from her selling scope. Pay for Content: the brand's whole published catalogue. Unavailable hidden. Each line: product, variant, quantity; ≥ 1 line, ≤ 20 lines, ≤ 5 of a variant. Each marked Return or Gift. Freely editable until booked. No shopper stock moves.
BookingPickup branch (main by default), box preset or dimensions, packed weight. Price shown GST included. Booking takes the price from the brand's available wallet and locks the quoted return price when any line is Return. Short balance: cannot book, shortfall shown (§9 pattern). Booking locks the list, produces the label. Cancel until collected: everything back, list reopens.
The aggregatorShiprocket, through INFLX's own account and INFLX's own shipping layer so it can be replaced. Brands never hold a courier account. INFLX picks the courier.
One parcelOne outbound parcel per collaboration. Never shared between creators or passed along (§32).
DeliveryTracking and notifications come from the courier: collected, out for delivery, delivered, attempt failed. Back to the store: both told, return-to-store charge to the brand, the brand may book once more (after her one correction if the address caused it).
Weight chargesA later courier weight difference is taken from the brand's wallet with the courier's reason; if short, recorded as owed and taken from its next money in. Never blocks the creator.
CheckingShe marks each line received, missing or damaged, note ≤ 300. A difference never stops her; the brand is told; Admin sees it; the brand may report it (dispute, item 18 — item 11 records the report). Missing Return items are not expected back. No deadline; Needs You with reminders.
Shoot doneShe marks it after checking. The brand does not confirm (§25). Opens the content step. Moves no money.
ReturnsDue within the stamped Remote return window (7 days) from the Posting Date, or from shoot done if later. She books the pickup from shoot done, choosing a day; all Return items she received go. Paid from the amount locked at booking; the rest back to the brand. The brand confirms each item; silence for the Admin number (7 days) from delivery confirms it. Missing or damaged may be reported (dispute).
OverduePast the due date with no pickup booked: shown to both, she is reminded, the brand may report it. Nothing moves on its own.
Return to GiftThe brand may turn a Return item into a Gift until she books the pickup; never a Gift into a Return once booked. No Return item left: the return lock goes back.
DelayedNot marked done by the Posting Date: flagged Delayed as item 10 does, with the stage — address not sent, not booked, on the way, not checked, shoot not marked. Both told once. Nothing moves; everything stays usable.
MoneyOutbound price at booking; return price locked at booking, settled at the return; courier adjustments — all the brand's, shown as courier charges in its wallet. The creator is never charged. The collaboration's locked fee is never used.
Needs YouCreator: send address; check what arrived; mark shoot done; book the return. Brand: pick products and book; confirm the return. Reminded at 48 hours, as in item 10.
AdminNew: Remote return auto-confirm, 7 days, stamped at delivery to the branch. Existing: Remote return window, stamped at the Agreement.
Out of scopeShared or relayed parcels; a second outbound parcel; cash on delivery; same-city riders (not yet in Vijayawada or Guntur); the brand choosing a courier; international. Content is item 12; disputes are item 18.
Testing before ShiprocketA simulated courier implements the same shipping interface: serviceability (test pincodes ending 999 no delivery, 998 no return pickup), price, booking, a label marked TEST, cancel, return pickup. Admin moves test parcels on from R18; every update goes through the real webhook path. Every parcel records its courier provider; simulated parcels never reach a real courier. Switching to Shiprocket is a founder decision after a few real test shipments.
