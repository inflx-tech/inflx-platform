Home: brand and creator

Home is four sections, always in the same order: what needs you, what you are waiting on, your money, and what is running now. Each section shows two or three things. Tapping the section, or See all, opens the full list. Drawn from what the code can serve today. Names, numbers and dates are example data.

Since v2

What changed in v3

Only the decisions at the bottom: all six are now recorded as decided. The screens are the ones you saw in v2. The four cards below are the changes v2 made to v1, kept for the record.

Four sections, nothing else

Needs you, Waiting on others, Money, and now Campaigns (brand) or Running now (creator). The latest-sales list has moved off Home; it is one tap away under Money.

Two or three things each

Needs you shows the three most urgent rows, not five. Waiting on others shows three rows in place of three bare counts. Money is three lines.

Every section opens

The section heading and See all both open the full list. Needs you and Waiting on others get their own full screens; Money and Campaigns open the tabs that already hold them.

Home fits one scroll

The whole of Home is one screen now (drawn full length below), where v1 needed two.

Left out on purpose

What is not on Home, and why

Tempting elementWhy not
The latest-sales listMoved off Home in v2. It is in the Wallet, one tap from the “Sales money coming” line.
Total revenue, return on spend, sales per creator or per campaignNo trustworthy total exists. The old sums overstate after a part-cancelled order; only the wallet's per-sale records are right.
Post views, reach, likesCollected only to order the shopper's feed. Nothing delivers them to a brand or a creator.
Follower growth or any trend lineOnly the latest measurement is kept in a form the app can read.
Recommended creators or brandsNo such signal exists. Discover and Find brands already own search.
A low-wallet warningNo threshold exists. A real shortfall already shows where it matters: on Sign and on Book the parcel.
Plan and usage meter · offers endingNo phone screen for either; offers live on the website only.
“Step 3 of 6” progressThe step counter is wrong for Pay for Sale and Remote collaborations.
Decided

Your decisions, 9 Oct 2026

“Yes to all” on the six. The seventh is the one thing still yours to call; it is drawn as kept.

D1
The app opens on Home Decided: yes

Today an approved brand opens on Creators and a creator on Collabs.

Decided: Yes. Once Home holds the to-do list it should be the first screen.
D2
A bell for the brand Decided: yes

The creator app has a bell and a list. The brand app has neither, though the server already sends its notices.

Decided: Yes: the same list on the brand app. In the same work, the eight kinds of creator notice that do nothing when tapped get their screens.
D3
Orders on the brand’s Home Decided: yes

The brand phone has no orders screen; packing and shipping are on the website.

Decided: One row, “2 orders to pack”, that opens the website’s orders page. Orders on the phone would be its own item.
D4
A count on the Home tab Decided: yes

No tab carries a count today.

Decided: Yes: the number of things that need you, on the Home icon, visible from every tab.
D5
One list, or setup separate Decided: yes

“Instagram needs fixing”, “Add payment details” and “Invitations are paused” could sit in a strip of their own.

Decided: One list. They sort to the bottom unless they block something, such as a withdrawal.
D6
How the list is ordered Decided: yes

It could be by campaign, by date sent, or by urgency.

Decided: By urgency: dates in the next three days, then what holds up someone’s payment or next step, then the oldest.
D7
The creator’s store link on Home Kept unless you say otherwise

It is outside the four sections: one line with Copy at the bottom of her Home.

Drawn as: Keep it. It is the thing she shares most and it is three taps away today. Say if you would rather Home held the four sections only.
Unchanged and not drawn: the Under review Home for accounts not yet approved, with its Finish-your-profile sheet; the Wallet, Campaigns and Collabs tabs that Money and the fourth section open. Source: four read-only passes over the server, both phone apps and the website on 9 October 2026. Production data was not read.
