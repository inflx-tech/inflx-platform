Home: brand and creator

Home is a placeholder in both apps today: an approved brand or creator sees “Home is coming soon”. These screens are drawn from what the code can serve now, not from the older documents. One idea runs through both: Home is the list of what is waiting for you, and one tap takes you to the exact screen. Names, numbers and dates are example data.

What the apps do today

Home is empty

An approved account opens on Creators (brand) or Collabs (creator). The Home tab says “coming soon”. Only accounts still under review see anything there.

A brand's to-do is four taps deep

Campaigns → campaign → set → the creator's row. Nothing on the way says which campaign or set holds a “Your move”, so the brand has to open every live set.

A creator's to-dos are in four places

Invites, Active, Pitches and the Create tab. Nothing tells her which one holds the urgent item, and cards are ordered by invitation date, not by whose move it is.

Some moves have no door at all

“Send the products back” is counted and reminded, but no card opens it once the shoot is marked. The Delayed label is stored and reaches no list. Eight kinds of creator notification do nothing when tapped.

The brand phone has no bell and no orders

The server already sends the brand “New order, pack it” and every collaboration notice with the screen to open. The brand app has no list to show them, and orders exist only on the website.

The rule for “your move” already exists

The set screen and the Collabs card each work out the one button a row needs: Sign, Reply, Plan the visit, Review it, Confirm live. Home reuses that rule across everything, with the same words.

The idea

Home answers three questions, in this order

What needs me?

One list across collaborations, pitches, posts and setup. Each row says who, what and by when, and its button opens the exact screen.

What am I waiting on?

Three counts: with the other side, with INFLX, and not yet answered. So a quiet Home still shows that work is moving.

How is the money?

Two figures and the latest sales. The Wallet tab keeps the full picture.

Brand app · Home tab

The brand's Home

Rows use the same button words as the set screen. The list is ordered by urgency: dates in the next three days first, then anything that holds up a creator's payment or next step, then the oldest.

Left out on purpose

What is not on Home, and why

Tempting elementWhy not
Total revenue, return on spend, sales per creator or per campaignNo trustworthy total exists. The old sums overstate after a part-cancelled order; only the wallet's per-sale records are right.
Post views, reach, likesCollected only to order the shopper's feed. Nothing delivers them to a brand or a creator.
Follower growth or any trend lineOnly the latest measurement is kept in a form the app can read.
Recommended creators or brandsNo such signal exists. Discover and Find brands already own search.
A low-wallet warningNo threshold exists. A real shortfall already shows where it matters: on Sign and on Book the parcel.
Plan and usage meterNo phone screen, and its campaign count reads retired data.
Offers ending (brand)Offers live on the website only.
“Step 3 of 6” progressThe step counter is wrong for Pay for Sale and Remote collaborations.
Campaign cards, invite lists, post lists, the four wallet figures, the profile checklistAnother tab owns each. Home links to them and does not repeat them.
For you

Decisions before the lock

D1
The app opens on Home

Today an approved brand opens on Creators and a creator on Collabs.

Recommended: Yes. Once Home holds the to-do list it should be the first screen.
D2
A bell for the brand

The creator app has a bell and a list. The brand app has neither, though the server already sends its notices.

Recommended: Yes: the same list on the brand app. In the same work, the eight kinds of creator notice that do nothing when tapped get their screens.
D3
Orders on the brand’s Home

The brand phone has no orders screen; packing and shipping are on the website.

Recommended: One row, “2 orders to pack”, that opens the website’s orders page. Orders on the phone would be its own item.
D4
A count on the Home tab

No tab carries a count today.

Recommended: Yes: the number of things that need you, on the Home icon, visible from every tab.
D5
One list, or setup separate

“Instagram needs fixing”, “Add payment details” and “Invitations are paused” could sit in a strip of their own.

Recommended: One list. They sort to the bottom unless they block something, such as a withdrawal.
D6
How the list is ordered

It could be by campaign, by date sent, or by urgency.

Recommended: By urgency: dates in the next three days, then what holds up someone’s payment or next step, then the oldest.
