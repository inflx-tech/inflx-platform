# Workflow prediction — item 19, Home and Needs You

**Brand:** opens the app → Home → sees the count on the bell and the three most urgent rows → taps
"Sign" on a creator's row → the agreement opens directly → signs → back on Home the row is gone, the
count is one lower and that creator now sits under Waiting on others → taps "See all" on Needs you →
the full list in order of urgency → taps the orders row → the website's orders page opens → glances
at Money and at the live campaigns → taps the Campaigns heading → the Campaigns tab on Live.

**Creator:** opens the app → Home → "Review & sign" → the agreement → back, the row has moved to
Waiting on others → "Start" on "Shoppable post needed" → a new post for that collaboration → Home's
Money shows the next payment date → Copy on her store link → the bell → a notice → its screen.

**Edge cases:** nothing needs you (one line, the rest of Home unchanged); first day (steps and one
yellow button, no empty sections); an account not yet approved (the Under review Home, unchanged); a
row whose deadline passed while the list was open (the action screen refuses in its own words; Home
drops the row on return); a collaboration past its posting date (Delayed on the row, still usable);
more than three rows (the count says how many, See all shows them); a creator with no store link
(no link line); a brand with no live campaign (one line saying so).
