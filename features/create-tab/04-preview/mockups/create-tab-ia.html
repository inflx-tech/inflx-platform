What these two screens fix

01No double-listing. A signed deal lived in both Offers → Accepted and Campaigns → Active. Now it's one row that moves through stages.
02One canonical door. Contract and deliverable open the same screen whether reached from a row, a Home tile, or a notification.
03Stage-aware CTA. The button is always the next action — Accept, Negotiate, Sign, Upload.
04One design system. Posts stop looking bolted-on — no emoji, no off-palette hex. White + black + one yellow signal, everywhere.
05Rejected ≠ dead-end. A rejected post offers edit-and-resubmit instead of forcing a rebuild.
