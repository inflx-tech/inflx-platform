How a post is doing, on INFLX and on social media

Two sources of numbers for every shoppable post, shown to both the creator and the brand. On INFLX: views, likes, comments, shares and saves from followers in the INFLX app. On social media: views, likes, comments and shares scraped from the social posts she links when she goes live. Rules: Docs/OFFER_TERMS_POSTS_RULES.md §4.15–4.16.

NEW doesn't exist today CHANGES exists today or on an earlier board
What exists today, and what's new
  • Social links at going live: today only Pay for Content creators hand in their live links (the content step). New: Pay for Sale and Hybrid creators add, for each shoppable post, the links of the social posts where they shared it.
  • Scraping: today nothing reads those links for numbers. New: every linked Instagram and YouTube post is scraped for views, likes, comments and, where the platform shows them, shares.
  • INFLX engagement: likes and saves exist but are shown to no creator or brand. Shares are recorded but never counted. Comments don't exist. New: all five counts, and comments.
  • All numbers are for reporting only. They never change anyone's pay.
Creator app · Going live

"Where did you post it?"

After her posting date, each approved shoppable post asks for the social posts where she shared it: an Instagram reel or post, a YouTube video or Short, a Facebook post. Stories can't be scraped, so a story gets a screenshot instead. For Pay for Content, the links she already hands in at the content step are used; nothing extra is asked.

Before you lock this board

Six questions these screens raised

Each has my recommendation, and the screens follow it.

1 · Who can like, comment and save on INFLX posts?Shoppers browse as guests until they order.Agreed 28 Sep: liking and commenting need sign-in. Saving and following work for guests (kept on the phone until sign-in). Sharing works for guests. Views count for everyone.
2 · Moderating commentsRec: text only, up to 300 characters. The creator can reply and hide; followers can report; INFLX removes what breaks the rules. The brand reads but can't hide.
3 · Must she add her social links, and by when?Rec: yes, for every approved shoppable post, within 2 days of the posting date. If missing, it's flagged to the brand, with no penalty (the same as minimum posts).
4 · Which platforms are scraped?Instagram and YouTube can be read reliably. Facebook is harder, and WhatsApp can't be read at all.Rec: Instagram and YouTube numbers from the start. Facebook links are accepted and shown, with numbers added later. Stories and WhatsApp: screenshot or nothing.
5 · How often are social numbers fetched?Each fetch costs money on the scraping service.Rec: daily for the first 30 days after posting, then weekly while the post is live, stopping when it leaves the shop. Pay for Content: daily until the final payment.
6 · The link must be her own accountRec: yes. A link from another account is refused when added, and flagged if the scrape shows a different owner.
