# Workflow

## Item 1 — what she sees she will earn

1. A brand runs a **pay-per-sale** campaign at 18% and invites a creator.
2. She opens it. The headline reads **18%**, with **"of everything you sell"** beneath.
3. The terms list says **Commission · 18%**. There is no fee line, because there is no fee.
4. Tapping Accept confirms *"Kanchi Silks · 18% commission"*.
5. Passing offers **"Commission too low"**, not "Fee too low".

## Branches

* **Pay for content** — unchanged: the fee as the headline, labelled Fee, no note.
* **Both** — the fee leads, with *"plus 5% of what you sell"* beneath.
* **Nothing agreed yet** — a sales deal reads **"Not set yet"**, never ₹0.
* **A row that contradicts its campaign goal** — the money present decides. A conversion
  set carrying a real fee shows the fee.

## Expected end state

No creator is ever shown ₹0 for work that pays a percentage. The decline reason stored is
unchanged (`fee_too_low`) — every report and brand-side screen already reads it; only the
words she is shown differ.
