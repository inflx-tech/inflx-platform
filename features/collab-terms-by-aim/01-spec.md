# What is on the table depends on how she is paid

Ten changes the founder raised on 2026-08-24 after using the apps, redrawn as eight boards
and confirmed on 2026-08-25 ("yes changes landed, then move to remaining changes one by one").

## The rule underneath all of them

Paying for content and paying for sales are different deals, so different things are on the
table. The app treats them almost identically, and that is what produces the ₹0 and the
wrong questions.

| Term | Pay for content | Pay per sale |
|---|---|---|
| Money | fee · negotiable | commission % · negotiable |
| Time | post by · negotiable | post by · negotiable |
| Where she works | **negotiable** | **negotiable** |
| Deliverables | **negotiable** | **not asked** |
| What she promotes | optional | optional |

**A fee buys specific work**, so both sides must know what it buys. **A commission buys
nothing** — she earns only if she sells, so counting her posts measures the wrong thing.

Two consequences the founder confirmed:

* **"Where she works" loses requirement/preference.** A term cannot be both negotiable and
  "cannot be countered". It moves up beside money and time; the marker survives only on
  what she promotes.
* **Pay-for-content loses the posting window.** The founder: *"not post any time from this
  date — by that time they should post and campaign should start."* So it is a deadline,
  the same meaning the sales date already has. Both aims use one date.

## The items

1. Sales collab leads with the **percentage**, not ₹0 — creator app
2. **Date pickers** wherever a date is entered — both apps
3. Pay-for-content: **one date**, a deadline — both · backend
4. **Deliverables asked of the brand** on a fee deal — brand · backend
5. Deliverables **not asked** of the creator on a sales deal — creator app
6. **Where she works becomes negotiable** — both · backend
7. **Counter in place**, beside each term, with a change count — creator app
8. **Specific products** as a scope option — brand app
9. **Category from the listing tree**, not typed — brand · backend
10. Pitch settings moved out of the pitch inbox — **shipped 2026-08-25**

## Deliverables vocabulary — reused, not invented

`reel · post · story · youtube_video · youtube_short`

These already travel from a campaign brief into a contract. This programme has twice had
to clean up a vocabulary written two different ways; a third would cost more than it saves.

## Out of scope

**Constraining her post to the agreed category.** Not a collaboration-screen change — it is
a rule about posting, touching the post composer, the product picker and the scope check.
Its own pass.
