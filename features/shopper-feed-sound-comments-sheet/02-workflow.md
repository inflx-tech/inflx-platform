# Workflow Definition — sound on feed videos, and comments from the bottom

## Actor
A shopper (signed in or a guest) in the INFLX shopper app.

## A · Sound
1. She opens the app. Home's first video plays, silent. At its bottom-left, just above "Shop this
   video", is a round button showing sound off.
2. She taps it → the video has sound; the button shows sound on.
3. She scrolls → the next video plays with sound; she did not turn it on again.
4. She taps the button → silent again, for this and the following videos.
5. She taps anywhere else on the video → it opens full screen, as today (with Play's own setting).
6. She closes and reopens the app → the feed is silent again.

## B · Comments
1. On a post in Home she taps the comment mark → the comments rise from the bottom, over the lower
   three-quarters of the screen. The post is still in sight above. A playing video waits.
2. Signed in: she types in the box at the bottom of the sheet and taps Send → her comment is first
   in the list, the title and the number beside the comment mark go up by one.
3. She closes the sheet (✕, a tap above it, or Back) → she is on the same post; the video carries on.
4. A guest: she reads; "Sign in with your number" takes her to sign in; back from it the sheet is
   open on the same post with the box to write.
5. The same on an opened post, an offer's posts and the full-screen video feed.

## Success definition
She can hear a feed video without leaving the feed, and comment without leaving the post.

## Failure / edge workflows
- A phone on the older app build (no player): a picture stands for the video; no sound button.
- A post that has ended: the sheet says so in the server's words; it can be closed.
- No connection: "Couldn't load. Check your connection." with Try again, inside the sheet.
- She closes the sheet with a comment half-written and opens it again on that post → it is still in
  the box.
