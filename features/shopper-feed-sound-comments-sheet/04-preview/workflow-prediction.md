# Workflow prediction — sound on feed videos, and comments from the bottom

What a shopper will see on the boards of `mockups/shopper-feed-sound-comments-sheet.html`.

## 1 · Sound (S1, S2)
The first video in Home plays silently, as today, with a small round sound button at its
bottom-left. One tap and she hears it; it stays on as she scrolls to the next videos. Another tap
and the feed is quiet again. Tomorrow the feed opens quiet.

## 2 · Comments (C1, C2)
She taps the comment mark. Instead of a new page, the comments slide up over the lower part of the
screen; the post is still visible above. She types, taps Send, sees her comment, closes the sheet
and carries on scrolling from the same place.

**What changes versus today**
| | Today | After |
|---|---|---|
| Hearing a feed video | open it full screen | tap the sound button on it |
| Feed sound when the app opens | silent | silent |
| Reading or writing a comment | a separate page | a sheet over the post |
| Leaving the comments | Back | ✕, a tap above the sheet, or Back |
| The number beside the comment mark after commenting | the old number | one more |

**Predicted questions**
- "I turned sound on in the feed; why is the full-screen video silent?" → That screen keeps its
  own setting and button.
- "Does the video keep playing while I read comments?" → It waits, and carries on when you close.
- "Where did the comments page go?" → Nothing opens it any more; it is removed.

**Blast radius** — shopper app: the feed card's video, the four screens with a comment mark, and
the comments themselves (moved, not rewritten). No server, no website, no other app.
