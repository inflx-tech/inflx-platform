Decisions taken for you — say if any should change.
  • Sound, once on, stays on for the next feed videos until she turns it off or closes the app — she does not turn each one on. Every time the app opens the feed is silent again.
  • The feed's sound button is separate from the full-screen video feed's (▶), which keeps its own remembered setting and its button where it is, top-right.
  • The same button on every feed that plays a video: Home, an opened post, an offer's posts.
  • The comments sheet everywhere there is a comment mark: Home, an opened post, an offer's posts and the full-screen video feed.
  • A video waits while its comments are open and carries on when they close — the same rule as the product popup.
  • The number beside the comment mark updates the moment she sends a comment (it used to stay at the old number).
  • A half-written comment is kept if she closes the sheet and opens it again on the same post.
  • The old comments page is removed; nothing leads to it any more.
