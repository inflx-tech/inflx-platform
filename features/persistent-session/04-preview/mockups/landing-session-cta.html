The gate cookie — what actually broke

Why a refresh logged you out. The dashboard calls tryRefreshToken() on every mount, and that writer set the cookie to 15 minutes — instantly downgrading the 30-day cookie login had just written. Once it lapsed, middleware.js ran on the server, saw no cookie, and redirected /dashboard → /login before any JS could refresh — while your 30-day refresh token was still perfectly valid.

Second, independent cause. SameSite=Strict withholds the cookie on inbound navigation from anywhere else, so opening an INFLX link from WhatsApp or email looked signed-out even mid-session. Lax is correct for a first-party session gate.

Four writers, three attribute sets — whichever ran last won. That is why the symptom came and went rather than failing cleanly. There is now exactly one writer.

Scope honesty: this is per-browser persistence. Recognising the same person on a different device would require identifying them — i.e. logging in.
