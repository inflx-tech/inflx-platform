For lock and the gap prompt

Rules behind these screens

TopicRule
Where it comes fromGenerated from the invitation’s agreed terms, never typed and never editable. The agreement is not a negotiation surface. Each creator gets her own, even when a set is shared.
What it containsAlways: both parties, campaign and set, Aim, posting date, mode of work (branch name and city for Store Visit), the signing deadline, a reference number and the date. Pay for Sale: commission, selling scope, the minimum shoppable posts as an obligation, and when commission is paid. Pay for Content: fee, the 25/25/50 split in rupees, deliverables, usage rights, and Remote return terms. Hybrid: the fixed fee with its split plus everything from Pay for Sale. The brand’s note appears when there is one. The follower offer never appears.
Standard termsINFLX’s own terms are part of every agreement, opened from a link and stamped with their version, so a signed agreement never changes when the terms are updated.
OrderThe brand sends, the creator signs, the brand signs. The brand signs last because the brand’s signature is what takes the money.
SigningThe signer types their full name, ticks that they have read the agreement and the standard terms, and taps Sign. INFLX records the typed name, the moment, the device and the IP address. The brand’s signer is a person signing for the brand, and is named on the document.
DeadlineBoth signatures must exist before the set’s invitation cutoff (posting date − 3 days). Unsigned at the cutoff, the agreement expires, nothing is locked and both sides are told — the creator especially when she had already signed.
MoneyPay for Content and Hybrid: the brand’s signature locks the whole amount — the fee plus the INFLX campaign fee for Pay for Content, the fixed fee alone for Hybrid — and releases the first 25% to the creator. Pay for Sale has no fixed money, so signing simply completes it.
Not enough moneyThe brand cannot sign. The shortfall is shown as an amount, adding money to the wallet is one tap, and the signature is completed on returning. The creator’s signature is never affected.
Backing outThe brand may withdraw an agreement the creator has not signed; she returns to waiting for one and the same agreement may be sent again. Withdrawing never unlocks the terms — those were fixed when she accepted — so different terms mean removing her and inviting again. The creator may decline at signing, with a reason, which ends the collaboration for that set.
After signingCommercial terms can never change inside the collaboration. A materially different deal needs a new set and a new agreement.
What signing startsThe collaboration leaves Invites and appears under Active on both sides, and the next step opens: the shoot plan for Store Visit, the shipment for Remote.
The documentThe data is the record. The PDF is produced when both have signed, stored so it can never drift, and downloadable by both. Before signing, the on-screen version is the agreement.
Phase 1 identityNames and cities only. Legal entity details, GST and PAN come with the payout work.
PrivacyNeither side sees anything about other creators. The creator never sees the campaign description or the brand’s internal notes beyond the note written for her.
