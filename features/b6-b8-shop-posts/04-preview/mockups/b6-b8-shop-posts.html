B6 + B8 · Shop posts — her Create tab, and your admin desk

Two halves of one flow: she builds shoppable posts on INFLX, you approve them. Drawn together because neither makes sense alone. NEW · CHANGE · KEEP · ADMIN

Shop posts have no deadline. She builds them any time — before the shoot, after it, whenever. They only have to be approved before the launch day, or a follower who taps her link arrives at an empty shelf.

Admin approves them, not the brand. That was a founder lock and it stays: a brand reviewing a creator's shop posts would be a brand editing her storefront.

A minimum applies per collaboration — you set it, say five — so nobody launches with one product and a dead link.

Both are new and both are small. The minimum has never existed; the wait is hard-coded at 30 days in the escrow service.

One thing has to change in the database before any of this works.

A shop post is tied to a brand — creator_posts.scope and scoped_brand_ids. It has no idea which collaboration it belongs to.

So nothing can answer "how many has she done for Label Fern's September set?" — which is the number the minimum, the launch gate, the reminder, the notification and the admin queue's sort order all depend on. Five features, one missing column.

The fix is one nullable column, campaign_influencer_id, on creator_posts. Nullable because a creator may build a post for her own store with no collaboration behind it, and that must stay possible.
What B6 and B8 cost. Four new screens on her side, one on admin, two settings. Six kept.

Almost all of it exists. The five-step composer works. The admin queue works — three tabs, counts, approve, send back with a reason. What is missing is the thread between them: a post does not know its collaboration, so nobody can count, sort, remind or gate.

⚠️ And she cannot see her own posts. One was rejected in production, with a reason stored, and there is no screen in her app that shows it to her or lets her fix it.
