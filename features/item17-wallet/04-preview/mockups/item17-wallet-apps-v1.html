The Wallet: brand and creator

Every rupee from items 9 to 16 in one place for each side. The brand adds money, locks it in collaborations, sees its share of every sale, and withdraws by request. The creator sees her collaboration payments with their dates, her commission sale by sale, and withdraws the same way. INFLX holds all of it; a withdrawal is on hold until INFLX pays it and marks it processed. Rules: master business rules v1.31 §17A. The Admin Money section is the next board. Names, numbers and dates are example data.
