Admin: all the money in one place

One Money section on the Admin website replaces today's scattered Escrow, Payouts and settings pages. INFLX sees what it is holding and for whom, what it has earned, every sale's three shares and every collaboration's locked money; it pays withdrawal requests from its bank and marks them processed; and it keeps three by-hand tools for disputes and mistakes, each with a written reason. Rules: master business rules v1.31 §17A. Taxes and invoices are a later round and are not here. Names, numbers and dates are example data.

Admin website · Money · Withdrawals

One queue: check, pay from the bank, mark processed

Brands and creators in one list. There is no approve or reject: INFLX checks the request, pays it from INFLX's bank, then comes back and marks it processed — that is the moment the person's balance goes down. If it can't be paid, "Could not process" with a reason frees the money.

Admin website · Money · Disputes and corrections

The three things done by hand, each with a reason

Not part of the daily work. After both have signed nothing is cancelled; if one side stops, INFLX settles it — and these are the tools. Each needs a written reason, is recorded with who did it, and only ever moves money into a wallet: the person still withdraws it by request.
