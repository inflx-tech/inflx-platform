# Workflow prediction — item 14, Pay for Sale going live

## Trigger
The shoot of a Pay for Sale collaboration (new creator terms) is marked done — confirmed by the
brand for a Store Visit, marked by the creator for Remote.

## Steps
1. Both apps show a door to **Going live**; the creator is told the step is open (Aim-correct copy).
2. She makes shoppable posts (Create tab); INFLX approves them; both sides see "N of M approved".
3. With ≥ 1 approved post she names rows (post + destination), uploads each version, sends the
   package → locked.
4. The brand approves, or asks for changes (rounds stamped at first sending; last-round warning;
   then approve or report only).
5. Approved → locked; she posts, gives a link per row (screenshot for a Story), marks posted; the
   self-confirmation date is stamped.
6. The brand confirms live (once) — or it confirms itself at the stamped date unless reported.
7. Confirmed + minimum reached → **Completed**; otherwise "live · waiting on posts" until reached.
8. Selling continues; later posts need links only.

## End state
Collaboration `completed`; the package, its rounds and links kept; no money moved; her posts live
in the shop; both apps show the collaboration's sales.

## Edge cases
- Old-terms collaboration → no step; plain message.
- Post taken down or removed from the shop after she named it → the row stays on record; the
  confirmation is about the social post, not the shop.
- Brand reports → frozen, no self-confirmation, posts keep selling.
- Posting Date passes → Delayed with the stage; told once; nothing moves.
- Setting changed after the stamp → the stamped date stands.
- A second tap on send / approve / confirm → no double effect.

## Blast radius
Item 12's content code is reused; Pay for Content must be unchanged. The shoot-done notice copy
changes for Pay for Sale and Hybrid only.
