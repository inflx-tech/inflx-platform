Four points the screens follow

Each is my recommendation and the screens are drawn to it. Say if any should change.

1 · Posts approved after she sends the package need links only

They don't go back to the brand. The brand already approved how she presents its products (S16).

2 · After Completed, sharing needs links only

The collaboration is finished; the selling isn't. Links are for reporting (S19).

3 · The social version uses item 12's change-request number

One Admin number for "how many times can a brand ask for changes", not two (A1).

4 · Facebook post is one of the places

From the posts rules' list. Its link is accepted and opened by the brand; its numbers come later (Level 5).
