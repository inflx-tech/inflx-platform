Walk fixes: the screens that change

Your walk of 3 and 4 October, drawn. Only screens that change are here, with your own posts and products on them: Akshaya Reddy, Aveen fashion boutique and desiner studio, the four posts of "Festive season offer". The look is the shopper board you locked on 30 September; nothing about it is new.

Decided by you
  • The bag's number and the Continue button are fine. Nothing changes there.
  • In the bag, the count is locked on a set, a bundle and buy one get one. It stays free on a plain discount, on buy more save more, and on spend-for-a-gift.
  • After Add to bag she is returned to shopping. Either place was fine for you, so she lands exactly where she was.
  • Your reference picture is the expected products pop-up: a grid, a bag icon on each product, a full product page, Back to the pop-up.
  • Returns are INFLX's rule, the same for every brand and every product: 7 days. It is one number in Admin › Money › Settings › "Return window". Set to 7 on live on 4 October.
Decided by me, for you to overrule
  • Nothing is drawn that INFLX does not hold: no star ratings, no review counts, no "inclusive of all taxes". A struck price shows only where an offer lowers the price.
  • On a deal post the product page's button is "Build your deal": a deal item is never added alone.
  • On a locked deal in the bag there is one tick and one Remove for the whole deal. One item's colour or size changes through Edit deal.

Before the build

Three things still yours

Returns are settled: 7 days, set by INFLX. What that leaves is the Return button.

Open
  • Where a video's picture comes from. INFLX's server makes it right after the upload — no new app install, and the five videos already uploaded get one too. Or the creator's phone makes it — that needs a new creator app build, and older videos stay without. I recommend the server.
  • Slow pictures. Not on this board: no screen changes. Product photos and approved posts would be served from a public picture address, and the server kept awake. Both are hosting changes and may cost money.
  • A Return button. The product page will say "Within 7 days of delivery", but the shopper app has no way to ask for a return yet: returns were left out of the 30 September step. Either the Return button on a delivered order is the next thing drawn and built, or the line stays off the product page until it is. I recommend building it next and showing the line with it.
