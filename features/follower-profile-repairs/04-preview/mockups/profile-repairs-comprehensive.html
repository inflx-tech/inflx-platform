Follower Profile — Slice 1 repairs

Contract features/follower-profile-repairs. Three faults verified in live code on 2026-08-14: a saved post can never be found again, an address can never be deleted, and Edit does nothing on either the app or the server. White law throughout — white surface, black ink, grey hairlines, red reserved for destructive actions only. No yellow: nothing here is a buy action.
