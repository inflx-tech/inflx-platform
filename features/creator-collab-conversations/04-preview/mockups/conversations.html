Area leads, industry follows. In a fashion-only app the industry chip row is the less useful of the two — and when only one industry has brands, it does not render at all.
Eligibility on the row is the real win. Today checkEligibility runs on submit — a creator picks a brand, writes a title, a message, terms, deliverables and three portfolio links, presses Send, and is told "this brand only accepts macro tier and above." The check already exists and returns a reason string. Running it per row and shipping it in the payload turns a wasted twenty minutes into a greyed line.

Three latent defects this arms, and fixes

#Defect once countering is two-wayFix
1acceptProposal always settles the creator's original terms (useCounterTerms=false). A brand pressing Accept after you moved to ₹14,000 would silently agree ₹20,000.Settle the latest terms, whoever last moved them
2Brand counterProposal is unbounded — a brand can counter forever, grinding a creator down.Bound by counter_rounds_per_side, the same admin setting the handshake uses
3respondToCounter only checks status='countered' — which after this change is also true when you countered. You could "accept" your own counter.Refuse unless the other side is the one countering
Backfill is a statement of fact, not a guess: only a brand can have countered until now, so existing rows with counter_terms IS NOT NULL get counter_by='brand', brand_rounds_used=1.
The brand has to be able to answer
A creator countering into a void is worse than no counter at all. Both existing brand surfaces gain the creator's counter and a way to respond.
