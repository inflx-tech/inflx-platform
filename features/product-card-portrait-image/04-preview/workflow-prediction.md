# Workflow prediction — product card portrait image

- Fashion/beauty/furniture product cards render the photo in a portrait 3:4
  frame; the full product shows instead of a sliced middle band.
- Detail page and food dish cards unchanged.
- Brands who upload portrait 3:4 (1080×1440) get a perfect zero-crop fill;
  2:3 uploads fill with a small edge trim; non-portrait sources letterbox on
  the pale card background rather than hard-cropping.
