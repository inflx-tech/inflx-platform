# Workflow — product card portrait image

1. Follower opens a world (fashion/furniture/beauty) with photographed products.
2. Each product card shows the photo in a portrait 3:4 frame — the full
   garment is visible, not a middle band with the top/bottom sliced off.
3. Tapping the card opens the detail page (unchanged) — still shows the full
   image.
4. Food world (FoodDishCard) is visually unchanged.
5. A rare non-portrait upload is letterboxed on the pale card bg rather than
   hard-cropped.
