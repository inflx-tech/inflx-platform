Two ways in, one road afterwards

A pitch and an invitation are different at the start and identical after it — both become terms going back and forth, and both end up agreed or closed. So the direction should be visible while it still matters, and stop mattering once it does not.

What today's design gets wrong

Talking merges both directions into one list. I built it that way on purpose — arguing a brand should not check two places — and you have looked at it and said the opposite. You are right: a request you sent and an approach you did not ask for are different jobs. One needs chasing; the other needs a decision.

And Agreed sits beside Talking as a peer, when it is really the far end of the same road.

Proposed: Talking splits into Sent and Received. Agreed keeps its own tab, because a settled deal waiting to be briefed is a different job again — it is work for you, not a conversation.

Why chips rather than rows

A filter row occupies the same space whether or not it is doing anything. A chip only exists once you have chosen something, and removing it is one tap rather than opening a page to un-choose.

With nothing filtered the top of the screen is just search and “＋ Filters” — two lines instead of five.

The rule that makes it clear

The button always names who, and how many. Never "Ask" or "Send" alone. A brand about to write to fourteen people should see the number before the form, not after it.

Everything after that button is identical — the same terms, in the same order, whichever way you arrived. That part is already true as of yesterday; this makes it visible.

No backend work, no migration

Every number on these screens is already computed and already sent: the city and niche counts, the tier bands, whose turn it is, the expiry clock. This is a rearrangement of what the apps already receive.

One decision I would like from you

I have drawn Sent and Received as a switch inside Talking, keeping four tabs across the top.

The alternative is five tabs — Sent · Received · Agreed · Find · Groups — which is more literal to what you asked for, but five labels start truncating on a phone and Find and Groups get squeezed.

Tell me which, and whether anything on these boards is wrong before I build.
