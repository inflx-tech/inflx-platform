# Workflow

## A brand opens Creators

1. **Talking** is first, showing the total. Inside it, **Sent** and **Received**, each
   with its own count.
2. **Sent** lists people the brand approached, ordered by who owes a move — the brand's
   turn first, then waiting, then closed.
3. **Received** lists creators who approached the brand, with **"Who can pitch you"** at
   the top: the current position in words, opening the settings.
4. **Agreed** lists settled terms, **expiring first**, each row showing what was actually
   settled — money, where she works, and what the fee buys.

## Finding and asking

1. **Find** opens with a search box and **＋ Filters**. Nothing else.
2. Tapping Filters raises a sheet: Where, Works in, Size — every option carrying a live
   count. Choosing changes the count in the button, not the list behind it.
3. **Show 4** applies them. They appear as chips; tapping the ✕ on one removes it.
4. With nothing ticked the bar reads **Ask all 4**.
5. Ticking rows switches the bar to **Ask 2** and **Save as group**.
6. Tapping a creator's row opens her profile, where the button reads **Ask Priya**.

## Groups

Opening a group shows its members and a bar reading **Ask 6**, with **Add more** opening
Find in picking mode.

## Empty

* **Received, open to pitches** — "Nobody has pitched yet." Says the brand is open, so
  silence means nobody has come.
* **Received, closed** — says so, and offers the setting.
* **Groups** — says what a group is for, and offers Find.
* **No results** — offers to widen rather than showing a blank list.

## Expected end state

* A brand can tell at a glance who owes a move, and separately who has approached them.
* No screen shows filter controls that are not doing anything.
* Every ask names who and how many before the form opens.
