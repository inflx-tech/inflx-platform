Part 4 — allocation scope chooser (Step 2, per creator)

A MOVE of the control you already have, not a new design. Step order, step count and goal placement unchanged.

The whole change in one line: the Whole store / Category / Products toggle already exists — it just lives inside the offer builder, once per offer type. That is the conflation: what a creator may sell is currently decided by the shape of their discount. It moves up one level to sit on the creator, and the offer keeps its own scope for pricing only.
