A fee is shown apart from commission — they behave completely differently.
Two additions. ₹14,000 and the Withdraw button do not move.
The rename is deliberate. "Available / Pending" becomes "Yours to keep / Still in the return window", with the reason written underneath. A creator who reads "pending" and assumes it is coming next week is the person who loses trust when it does not.
₹9,180 + ₹4,820 = ₹14,000. If those parts ever fail to add up to the total she already knows, the phase is not done. That is the first thing checked.
The fee sits apart on purpose. Money for posts and money from sales are different promises. Adding them into one figure would make both meaningless.

The labels already exist. Every product in the system carries its kind and most sit in a proper tree. Nothing needs sorting — the screens simply were never sent the label.
