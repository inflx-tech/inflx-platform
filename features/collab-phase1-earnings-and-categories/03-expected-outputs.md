# Phase 1 — how we will know it worked

Things to try. Each one either happens or it does not.

---

## What a collaboration paid

**P1 · Her total splits, and the parts add up to the whole.**
Open her earnings. The lifetime figure is unchanged. Underneath it, each collaboration shows
its own amount, and those amounts sum exactly to the lifetime figure. If they don't, we have
either double-counted a sale or lost one.

**P2 · Open one collaboration and see what it paid.**
It names the commission rate, the sales it came from, and what she earned.

**P3 · Money she can count on is shown apart from money still settling.**
Confirmed and still-in-the-return-window appear as two figures, never one. A creator seeing
one number and receiving a smaller one is the failure this prevents.

**P4 · Which products sold, and which didn't.**
Inside a collaboration, each of that brand's products shows how many sold and what she earned
from it. A product that sold nothing shows as nothing, not as absent — "Banarasi Gold: 0" is
information.

**P5 · Which of her posts sold them.**
Each sale counts for exactly one post — whichever the shopper came through. No sale appears
under two posts, and the per-post amounts sum to the collaboration total.

**P6 · A collaboration that has sold nothing reads zero, cleanly.**
No error, no blank, no "—" where a number belongs.

**P7 · A paid content collaboration shows its fee, not commission.**
Different kind of money, shown differently. It must not appear as commission earnings.

**P8 · The brand sees the same sales cut their way.**
By creator, by product, by post, inside their own campaign. Each cut sums to the campaign
total.

**P9 · And sees nothing beyond their own campaign.**
Her earnings from other brands, her other collaborations, and her total income are all
absent. Try to reach them and they are not there — not merely hidden on screen.

---

## Products grouped by kind

**P10 · Browsing a brand shows its products grouped.**
Silk, cotton, party, bridal — not one flat list.

**P11 · The same grouping inside a campaign.**
What she is allowed to sell, grouped the same way, with the same words.

**P12 · The same grouping on her own shelf.**
Across every brand she works with, filterable by brand as well.

**P13 · Unlabelled products still appear, under "Other".**
They stay sellable and visible. Hiding them would hide real stock.

**P14 · A group that would be empty is never offered.**
No tapping a category to find nothing behind it.

**P15 · The kind actually arrives at the screen.**
This is the one that matters most, and the one most likely to be quietly missed: the product's
kind must be present in what the server sends, not merely present in the database. A label
that exists but never reaches the app is a label that does not exist. That has happened in
this system before.

---

## Nothing broke

**P16 · Her lifetime earnings figure is unchanged.**
The same number as before this shipped, to the rupee.

**P17 · The real sales already recorded still reconcile.**
The sales sitting in the system today produce the same totals after as before.

**P18 · A phone that has not updated behaves exactly as it does now.**
Every screen still works. The server sends more than before and removes nothing.

**P19 · Nothing about payouts, rates or timing moved.**
No commission rate, no return window, no payment stage behaves differently.

**P20 · The full check suite passes**, with nothing weakened, skipped or deleted to get
there. Any failure is classified honestly — an out-of-date check, a rule that changed, a real
fault, or a bad check — never re-run until it happens to go green.

---

## What proves the phase is done

All twenty above, and one sentence we can say honestly:

> Asha can see that Kanchi Silks paid her ₹9,000 and Label Fern ₹5,000 — which saree sold,
> which post sold it, and how much of it is money she can already count on.

Today she can only see ₹14,000.
