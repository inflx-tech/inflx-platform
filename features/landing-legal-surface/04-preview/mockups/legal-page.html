In short

We collect only what we need to run campaigns, process payments and keep the platform safe. We do not sell your personal information. You can ask us for a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account at any time.

1Who we are

Omnara India Private Limited (“Omnara”, “we”, “us”), a company incorporated in India with CIN CIN-TBD and registered office at REGISTERED-ADDRESS-TBD, operates the INFLX platform. For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Omnara is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described in this policy, and you are the Data Principal.

2What we collect

What we hold depends on which side of the platform you are on.

WhoWhat we collect
EveryoneMobile number, name, city, device and app-version information, and how you use the app.
BrandsBusiness name and category, business address, GSTIN where applicable, listings, campaign and wallet activity.
CreatorsHandle, niche, connected social profiles and their public metrics, bank or UPI details for payouts, and PAN for statutory tax deduction.

We do not store complete payment card details. Card and UPI credentials are handled by our payment partner and never reach our servers.

3Why we process it

  • To provide the service — create your account, run campaigns, attribute commission, fulfil orders.
  • To pay people — settle creator commissions and brand payouts, and deduct tax where the law requires it.
  • To keep the platform safe — detect fraud, abuse and fake engagement.
  • To meet legal obligations — tax, accounting, and lawful requests from authorities.

4Consent & your choices

Where we rely on your consent, we ask for it in clear language before we collect the data, and we tell you what it will be used for. You can withdraw consent at any time — it is as easy to withdraw as it was to give. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that already happened, and some features stop working without the data they depend on.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers for their own marketing.

6Retention

We keep personal data for as long as your account is active. After deletion we erase or anonymise it, except records we are legally required to retain — financial and tax records in particular — which we keep for the statutory period and use for nothing else.

7Your rights

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we shared it with.
  • Correct or complete anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Erase your data and close your account — see Delete My Account.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and escalate it if we do not resolve it.

8Security

Access is authenticated with one-time passwords and short-lived tokens, traffic is encrypted in transit, and access to production data is restricted by role. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the authority as the law requires.

9Children

INFLX is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly create accounts for children, and we do not use their data for tracking or behavioural advertising. If you believe a child has registered, tell the Grievance Officer and we will remove the account.

10Grievance redressal

Questions or complaints about this policy or how we handle your data go to our Grievance Officer, who acknowledges within 24 hours and resolves within 7 days.
