Rules already built into these screens
  • Six steps, always in the same order: type → sub-type → name & dates → products → numbers → review & publish. Dates come before products because locking depends on dates.
  • One product can be in only one offer at a time. A product that is in another offer with overlapping dates shows a 🔒 and can't be added.
  • No whole-store offers. Every offer is built from a list of products (a "pool").
  • Offers are for creators only. "Publish to store" is removed. "Pay at store / Online / Pre-book" is removed from the builder too; pay at store gets its own discussion.
  • No margin floor for now. The price checks that remain are the bundle checks and the free-gift check.
  • "Price" everywhere means the product's one price (MRP was retired in July).

Every publish check, by offer kind
  • All kinds: end date after start date · one product, one offer (buy pool and gift pool both locked).
  • Volume: quantity or spend increases tier by tier, and so does the reward. 1 to 4 tiers.
  • Pick-any and Mix & match bundles: costliest item ≤ 1.5 × cheapest · cheapest combination saves at least 10%.
  • Fixed bundle: bundle price below the total price of its items.
  • Free gift: costliest gift combination below the minimum qualifying purchase · buy pool and gift pool share no product · every gift has a stock limit.
  • Flat discount: ₹ off less than the cheapest item in the pool. BOGO: no price checks.
  • Margin floor: not checked (decided 28 Sep, discuss later).

NEWO13 · Listings → Gift items (added 28 Sep, in the listing engine)

Gift items: things a brand gives away but never sells

A silver coin, a water bottle, a tote. They live in their own tab in Listings, apart from normal products. Only 4 fields: name, photo, value, stock. No category, no sizes, no price. They never appear in the shop, in the pool builder's buy side, in "products without offers", or in creator terms. The only place they can be picked is a free gift offer's gift pool. Things the brand also sells (like a cap) are not gift items. They stay normal products and can be picked into a gift pool from the Browse/Search tabs.

NEWO12 · Editing a product's price in Listings

Warning before a price change on a live product

This is the approach agreed on 28 September: warn before saving, then tell creators, then recalculate the post's price band. Nothing is blocked, but if the new price breaks a bundle rule the brand is told plainly.

Answered · board locked 28 Sep 2026

Six questions these screens raised

The founder locked the web board on 28 September ("mockups for web is locked and fine"), so every recommendation below stands as drawn. Offers are built on the website first; the phone pool builder (P1–P4) comes after.

1 · What can a brand change on a live offer?Creators' posts already promise the deal, so changing it would make those posts untrue.Rec: only "remove products" and "end offer now" (O10, O11). Name, numbers, pool additions and dates are locked once published. To change the deal, end it and build a new one.
2 · Does a draft lock its products?If drafts locked, a forgotten draft could block products for months.Rec: no. Products lock only when the offer is published (Scheduled or Live). The final lock check runs at the moment of publishing (O7 shows the blocked case).
3 · Deleting offersRec: drafts can be deleted. A published offer can't be deleted, only ended, so sales history and creators' records stay intact.
4 · Status namesRec: Draft · Scheduled (published, start date not reached) · Live · Ended.
5 · "Auto-add new products"Your doc makes it optional and off by default.Rec: it works only for "Category → all" and "Category → filters" sources, and skips locked products. New products join the pool, and creators can pick them for new posts. Existing posts don't change.
6 · Phone or website?See P1–P4.Rec: build the website first (it's the bigger screen for pools), then the phone with the same six steps, since the drawings show it's workable. Your call.
