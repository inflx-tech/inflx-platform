Creators, nearest first

Every creator list a brand sees is ordered from its main branch city outward, with the distance on the row. A brand in Vijayawada sees Vijayawada, then Guntur, then Eluru. Her Store Visit preference is hers: cities picked means only those; none picked means anywhere.
