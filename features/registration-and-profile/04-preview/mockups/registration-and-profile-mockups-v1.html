For the record

What these screens do and don't decide

They do decide: the field list at every step, what is mandatory where, the two-step state-then-city picker and its wording, where the money fields live, what a pending account can reach, what the popup says and why, and what an admin sees before approving.

They don't decide: exact spacing, icon set, or copy that isn't shown here. Those follow the app's existing components.

Not drawn, because nothing changes: the phone and OTP screens, invitation and pitch settings, "Your numbers", and everything behind approval.
