Decisions taken for you — say if any should change.
  • The address is optional. It is not one of the "things left" before review, because many creators never do a Remote collab.
  • It is private. No brand and no INFLX screen shows the profile address. A brand sees an address only when she sends it for a collab — as today.
  • The courier check stays on the collab, not on the profile: whether a courier reaches her depends on which store is sending.
  • "Account holder name" is added beside the three you asked for (re-enter number, bank, branch) — a name that does not match is the commonest reason a bank refuses a transfer.
  • Creators who already saved a bank account keep it and can still withdraw; the new boxes are asked the next time they save bank details.
  • Creator app only. The brand app's bank form still asks two things — the same change there is a separate, small piece if you want it.
