# The walk

⚠️ **Server-side only so far.** The term validates, counters and describes itself. The screens
that let a brand *ask* for it come next — see 05-impact.

## W1 · A brand asks, on any aim

Send a collaboration request with commercial usage stated:

```
usage:      { commercial: true, extra: 8000 }
importance: { usage: 'preference' }
```

Accepted on **commission**, **pay for content** and **both**.

## W2 · She answers

* **Counter the price** — `{ commercial: true, extra: 15000 }` → allowed
* **Refuse it** — `{ commercial: false }` → allowed
* **Accept** — the agreement records what was settled

## W3 · A brand who cannot do without it

Mark it `requirement`. She can no longer counter it — she accepts or declines. Countering it
is refused in words: *"accept it or decline the request."*

## W4 · Nobody raises it

Leave `usage` out entirely. The agreement is valid, and the default applies: her content stays
hers, and the brand and INFLX may repost it on their own social accounts with credit.
