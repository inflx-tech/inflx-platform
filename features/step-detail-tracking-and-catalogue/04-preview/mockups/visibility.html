Two jobs, in this order. One: the shopping app has to start sending the signals — right now not one has ever been sent. Two: something has to be able to read them back; today they can only go in. The screen is third.
Why the empty state matters more than the full one. If this ships showing zeros, every creator concludes her posts are being ignored. Showing "we're not counting yet" alongside her real sales is the difference between an honest gap and a wrong number.
Nothing about the design needs inventing. Seen, opened, tapped, saved, shared, added to bag, which product, and which surface — all of it is already in the shape the app was built to record. This is finishing something, not starting it.
