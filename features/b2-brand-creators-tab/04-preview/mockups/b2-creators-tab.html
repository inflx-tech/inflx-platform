4b · The post date is a DEADLINE, not a day
Founder, 2026-08-30: "say creator agreed on post date means he can post within that period...say one creator agreed on 15 Sep means I can post before that...say he didn't agree means he is busy in all days till 15 Sep."

So the set's date is the last day, not the only day. She may post any time up to it. If she refuses it, she is refusing the whole window, not one square on a calendar.

CHANGE Three rules that follow

What a deadline means downstream

1 · The shoot

A store visit must finish 1 day before the deadline — so she still has room if she posts on the last day.

2 · Her shop posts

Not "a day before the deadline". Live before she posts — whenever that is. A dependency, not a date.

3 · Matching a pitch

Her pitch carries a window. A set fits if its deadline falls inside her window.

⚠️ Rule 2 is a change from what I drew in B1, where the shop posts were due a fixed day before. With a deadline that no longer works — she might post a week early.

This is why the rule is worth unifying. Kavya pitched and Meena was asked — and they end up in one set together, because they want the same thing.

One thing the pitch form is missing, and it decides whether this works.

A pitch carries goal, proposed_commission_pct, proposed_fee, proposed_deliverables and a posting window — and no shoot mode. Two of the three matching parameters are there; the third is not.

Without it, accepting a pitch drops her into whatever set fits the dates — which could commit her to travelling to a store she never agreed to visit.

The fix is one field on the pitch form: store visit · remote · either. "Either" is probably the common answer and makes matching easy. Pitches already in flight with no mode get asked once, when they are placed.

Good news: influencer_proposals.resulting_campaign_influencer_id already exists — a column built for exactly "which collaboration did this pitch become". It has never been used.
