One question this tab raises, and I want your answer.

What happens when you ACCEPT a pitch? Today it makes an agreement and drops it in the pool. There is no pool now.

My recommendation: accepting a pitch asks "which set?" — you pick an open set whose date and mode suit, or start one. If her pitch conflicts with every open set, she goes straight into deferred, and you build a set for her like any other. One road in, whoever started the conversation.
What B2 really costs. No new screens. Six changed, five kept, one swept.

The tab's job narrows and that is the point — it finds people and hears from them. Deals happen inside a set.

The hardest work is invisible here. The negotiation screen looks almost identical and has to be re-anchored underneath, from a standalone request to her row in a set. The negotiations table was built for exactly that shape and has never been used.
