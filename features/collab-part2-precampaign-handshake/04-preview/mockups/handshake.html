Admin — the limits, not hardcoded
Your call. Follows the pattern of referral settings, world rules and follower app config: one settings row with an admin surface.
Today these are constants in code — 5 active, 3 per day, 10 per week, 7-day expiry, 30-day cooldown. Changing any of them needs a deploy. And the brand side has no limits at all, because brand outreach never existed.
