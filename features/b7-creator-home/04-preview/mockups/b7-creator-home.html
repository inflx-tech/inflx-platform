One button on her Home has never worked.

HomeScreen shows Available to withdraw with a WITHDRAW button underneath it. There is no withdraw screen in the app. The endpoint exists, the ₹100 minimum is enforced, the 10% TDS is calculated — and the button leads nowhere.

It is the same gap B4/B7 found from the Earnings side. Two entry points, one missing screen.
What B7's Home costs. Two new screens, three changed, one confirmed unchanged.

Her Home was built to the right shape — one "your turn" tile, then the work, then the money. It does not need rethinking, only filling in: the tile becomes items instead of a count, the collabs show which step and whose move, and the money splits into what is hers and what is coming.

And every money line now says when it becomes hers. That single habit — a date beside every rupee — is what stops a creator believing she is owed something nobody plans to send.
