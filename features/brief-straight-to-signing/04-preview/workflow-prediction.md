# Prediction

1. Brand briefs a creator whose agreement is clean and unexpired.
2. Her row goes to `accepted`; a contract is created `pending_influencer`.
3. She opens the app: the collaboration is under **To sign**, not Invitations.
4. She taps it — a real screen, not a blank one — and the hero offers **Sign**.
5. The sign screen shows: campaign name (no set name), what she can sell, her commission, the
   shopper offer and its window, deliverables, production, payment schedule, her content rights.
6. **Sign** → typed name → `pending_brand`, waiting on the countersign.
7. **"I can't do this collaboration"** → reason → optional note → declined, note stored, unsigned
   contract deleted, back to Collabs.

## What must NOT happen

- A creator whose agreement drifted must still be asked to re-agree. She never signs terms she
  has not seen.
- A pay-for-content creator must not see a "What You Can Sell" card. There is nothing to sell.
- A creator with no library offer must not see an empty offer row.
- A signed contract must never be deleted by a decline.
