Offer Engine

A standalone place where a brand designs reusable offers against its product/service catalogue — independent of any campaign. Each finished offer can then be published into the brand store (every follower sees it, no influencer needed) and/or attached to chosen influencers to promote. The existing in-campaign discount builder stays completely untouched.

The 3 channels a library offer can flow through
Design offer→Save to library⤳① Publish to Store/② Conversion campaign/③ Engagement campaign
① STORE set on the offer page · all followers buy direct · no influencer · no commission · no tracking② CONVERSION attached to influencers inside a campaign · tracked link · commission per sale③ ENGAGEMENT allocated to campaign influencers · promotional only · no commission, no tracking · drives awareness to the store
