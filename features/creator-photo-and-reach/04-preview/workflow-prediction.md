# Workflow prediction

Written before the founder walks it.

1. **Creator app → My Profile.** The round yellow tile now has **ADD PHOTO** under it.
2. Tap → the phone's photo picker opens. Cancel → nothing changes.
3. Pick one → spinner in the tile → her photo appears, round, filling the tile. Label
   becomes **CHANGE PHOTO**.
4. **Brand app → Creators → tap her.** Her photo in the hero instead of two letters.
5. Directly under the hero, three tiles: **INFLX** (bordered, first), **INSTAGRAM**,
   **YOUTUBE**. YouTube reads **—** with *not connected*.
6. The old six-box stats grid is unchanged below it.
7. Brand app → Find → she carries the same photo in the list.

Most likely failure: the photo uploads and the tile goes back to initials — that is the
save step failing, not the upload, and the app should have said so rather than looking
like nothing happened.
