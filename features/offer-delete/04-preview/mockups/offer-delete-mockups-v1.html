X7What deleting does, for each kind of offer

The offer isWhere Delete showsWhat deleting does
DraftNot published yet On its row, beside Continue Erased for good. It was never published, so no creator had it, no post or order used it, and none of its products were held.Box: "Delete this draft?" · Note after: "Draft deleted."
EndedYou pressed End offer now, or its last day passed On its row, beside Open — and at the top right of its own page Off your lists. It leaves your Offers list and its counts, its page says not found, and it no longer shows in your creator-terms note ("not shown, ended") or as a product's "last in offer" on Products without offers. Its record is kept for the creators who had it, their posts, orders, payments and INFLX. The days its products were held are freed, so a new offer on those products is never refused because of it.Box: "Delete "{name}"?" · Note after: "Offer deleted."
ScheduledLive Nowhere — no Delete Not possible. End it first (End offer now on its page), then delete it. If a delete reaches the server anyway, it answers: "This offer is still running. End it first, then you can delete it."

Not in this change: undo, a "Deleted offers" view, deleting a running offer, and editing a Scheduled offer (its name, type, numbers and dates stay frozen once published — rule 1.10). No change for creators, shoppers, the phone apps or INFLX admin.
