Rules built into these screens
  • Pay for Sale and Hybrid only. Pay for Content terms don't change (fee, deliverables, usage rights, note).
  • At least one offer or one product without an offer is required.
  • Only offers valid on the posting date or the day after are listed. An offer ending within 7 days after posting shows a warning.
  • Offers are linked, not copied. Products without offers are frozen at sending and sell at normal price.
  • Offers and products can't be countered. Nothing can be added after signing; a new offer means a new set.

Before you lock this board

Three questions these screens raised

Each has my recommendation, and the screens follow it. Reply "agree all" or name the ones to change.

1 · The brand ends an offer while invitations with that offer are still unanswered or unsignedExample: Priya was invited with 2 offers on 1 Oct, and the brand ends one on 3 Oct before she replies.Rec: before ending, the brand is warned ("in 3 unsigned invitations"). The ended offer drops out of those invitations and the creators are told. If an invitation is left with nothing to sell, it's withdrawn automatically.
2 · "All products without offers" means the 40 on the day of sendingProducts the brand lists after sending don't join her list.Rec: yes, frozen, as your terms document says. To give her new products later, a new set.
3 · Commission shown per offer to the creator (C3: "10% of ₹2,999 = ₹300")Helps her choose which offers to post, but the figure is only an example.Rec: show it, worked out on the offer's example price, labelled "You earn".
