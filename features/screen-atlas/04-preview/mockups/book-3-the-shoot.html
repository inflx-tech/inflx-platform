Action Book 3 · The shoot

Getting the goods and the creator into the same place. 7 actions — 3 the brand starts, 4 the creator. Only one is new, but every single one forks on the mode, and the two modes are not mirror images of each other.

NEW does not exist · CHANGE exists and changes · KEEP works as it is · BOTH both phones shown · STORE VISIT REMOTE
The mode is fixed by the set and never switched. It was agreed in Book 1, written onto the contract in Book 2, and by the time anyone reaches this book it is settled. Nothing in this book changes it — a creator who needs the other mode was moved out of the set before she ever got here.

They are not mirror images, and that is the single biggest source of bugs in this phase. Code that treats "mark it done" as one thing will close a store visit on one signature. Code that treats "start the shoot" as one thing will wait forever for a brand to arrange a remote shoot he was never meant to arrange.

Can go wrong
Fixed 30 Aug — "the date picker is not working". Two separate faults in one screen.

One · the window came back from the server one level too deeply wrapped. The app read dates.range where the value was at dates.data.range, got nothing, concluded there was no window, and refused to open the picker.

Two · the window itself was a day wrong. A DATE column arrives as a Date at local midnight; doing UTC arithmetic on it loses a day. On a tight schedule that closed the only day that would have worked. Every date is now a calendar day in India (utils/indiaDay.js).

The buffer also went from 2 days to 1, at the founder's decision.
The screen read content_end_date, which is never written. A dead column silently produced no window at all. It now reads window_end || content_end_date.

Fixed 30 Aug — a store visit closed on one mark. The founder's report: "without doing anything from both it is saying shoot plan is done". There was one completion flag, so whoever pressed first closed it for everyone.

This is where a remote shoot begins, and nothing asks for it today. That is why remote shoots have no honest starting point in the current app — the brand is waiting for something the creator was never prompted to send.
Leaves behind
production_events — ship_to_address, or a reference to a saved one.
influencers — the default address, if she saved one.
Can go wrong
An address visible anywhere it should not be. It must be readable by the one brand on the one shoot, and by nobody else — not on her profile, not in a creator search, not in a campaign export.

No prompt, so no address. If she is not asked at the moment the contract is signed, she will not think to go looking for the screen, and the shoot simply never starts.

Her go-live day does not move on its own. A missing parcel pauses her shoot, but the set's day belongs to everyone in it. Only the brand can move it, and moving it moves everybody — which is usually the wrong answer for one late courier. The realistic outcomes are a fast replacement, or she comes out of the set.

Can go wrong
A paused shoot with no owner. "Something is missing" has to land in the brand's Needs you. If it only sits on the shoot screen, nobody looks at it until the go-live day arrives and she has nothing to post.

Steps
  1. I HAVE SHOT IT. One press, and the shoot is closed.
  2. The screen says why it is hers alone: they are not there, so they cannot confirm it.
This is the asymmetry, stated once. A store visit needs two marks because two people were there. A remote shoot needs one because only one person was. Anything that treats "complete the shoot" as a single rule gets one of the two wrong.
Leaves behind
production_events.influencer_completed_at, and status='completed' immediately — because for remote, isShootComplete needs only her.
Can go wrong
Waiting for a brand mark that will never come. The exact opposite of the store-visit bug, and just as easy to write. If REQUIRES_BOTH is ever applied to remote, every remote shoot hangs forever with no error and no one to blame.
What this book leaves for the next one
Every path out of Phase 3
  • Store visit, both marked — she has material. → Book 4.
  • Remote, she marked — she has material and keeps the goods. → Book 4.
  • Parcel missing or damaged — paused, in his Needs you, and someone has to decide between a replacement and taking her out of the set.
  • No shoot at all — a sale set using her own material went straight to Book 4 from Book 2.
The one thing to get right
One function answers "is this shoot finished", and it takes the mode as an argument. isShootComplete(mode, marks), mirrored in the app so both sides agree. Three separate bugs in this phase — closing on one mark, completing on creation, and the picker that would not open — were all versions of the same thing: two places deciding something that only one place should decide. That is now the sixth, seventh and eighth instances of the pattern.
Still unverified on a phone
All three 30 Aug fixes are live and none has been walked. OTA v192 and v199 shipped on 30 Aug. The store-visit two-sided completion, the remote completed-on-create repair, and the date picker have been proved by tests and by production data — but nobody has yet done a store visit and a remote shoot end to end on a handset. That walk belongs in the final verification pass, not per-slice.
