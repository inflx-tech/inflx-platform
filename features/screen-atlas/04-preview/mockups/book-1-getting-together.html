Action Book 1 · Getting together

Finding each other, agreeing terms, and what happens when you do not. 14 actions — 9 the brand starts, 5 the creator starts. Six of them do not exist today.
Screens are referenced by address, never redrawn — they live in atlas-brand.html and atlas-creator.html.

NEW does not exist · CHANGE exists and changes · KEEP works as it is · BOTH two people, so both phones are shown at the moment it matters
The one rule this whole phase turns on. A set is one aim, one go-live day, one way of shooting. Money is settled inside a set and changes nothing for anybody else. A day or a shoot mode cannot be settled inside a set — other people already agreed to it — so disagreeing about either one moves her out. Every fork below is a consequence of that single sentence.

BR-02Build a setCHANGE
StartsFrom BR-01, or + ANOTHER SET on a campaign
WhoBrand, alone
ScreensSB-24SB-25SB-26SB-27
Steps
  1. 1 of 4 — the aim. SB-25 This choice decides what the next three steps ask for.
    PAY PER SALE — she earns a share of what she sells. No money up front. Needs products and an offer. Deliverables are optional.
    PAY FOR CONTENT — a fee for reels or stories. Money is locked when he publishes. Deliverables are required.
    BOTH — a fee and a share. Requires everything.
  2. 2 of 4 — the basics. SB-26 The three things that define the set are on one screen: the go-live day, the shoot mode, and when invitations close.
  3. The go-live day opens the date picker. SB-27 Days too soon to invite, sign and shoot are closed.
  4. 3 of 4 — who. → BR-03.
  5. 4 of 4 — terms. → BR-10 (Book 2).
  6. Back to the campaign. SB-24 The set sits there as a card until he publishes.
The go-live day is a day, not a deadline. Everyone in the set posts on social media that day. She cannot beat it and she cannot miss it. Shop posts on INFLX are different — they can go up any time before it, and only have to be approved by then.
Leaves behind
campaign_sets — aim, go_live_date, shoot_mode, invites_close_at.
Every one of these is new on the row. Sets exist today as handpick / open_group and carry none of it.
Can go wrong
The wizard sends an empty deliverables list for a sale-only set, and the server calls that an error. Two places decide whether content is being bought and they disagree — the fifth time that same fault has cost a bug. One function answers it (buysContent(goal)) and both sides call it.

Changing the day or the mode after people have agreed. Everyone who agreed must be asked again. Easiest safe answer for now: once the first invitation is answered, those two fields lock.

Treating "moved out" as "declined". She has not refused the work. She refused this set. Any screen that counts her as a decline will under-report a campaign that is going fine.

What this book leaves for the next one
Every path out of Phase 1
  • She accepted — or he agreed to her money counter → a contract exists → Book 2.
  • She is deferred — waiting in the pool with a day and a mode → back into BR-07 → and then into Book 2 by the normal path.
  • It ended — she declined, or he did. A reason is recorded either way.
  • An agreement exists with no campaign — sits as a record until he puts her in a set.
The three things this book says must be built first
  1. A negotiation that belongs to a set row. The largest single piece of backend work here, and BR-05, BR-06, CR-02 and CR-03 all wait on it.
  2. A counter that knows what kind it is. Money and structural counters are the same shape in the data and have opposite consequences.
  3. A deferred row that keeps what she asked for. Without it the pool cannot be grouped, and BR-07 has nothing to build from.
Carried into Book 2 unfixed
cancelInfluencer writes an invalid contract status. Confirmed live. It will throw the first time it runs. Not fixed here because nothing in this book calls it — but BR-05's "drop her" is one step away from it.
