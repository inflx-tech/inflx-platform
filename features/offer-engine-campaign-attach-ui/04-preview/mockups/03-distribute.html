How an offer gets distributed

A library offer reaches followers two ways: a Store toggle on the offer page, and — separately — by attaching it to influencers inside campaign creation, assigned per influencer. Works the same in conversion and engagement campaigns; only the treatment differs.

Attach offers — per influencer

New step in campaign creation. The wizard asks if you want to attach an offer; choosing yes opens your offer library as a picker, and you assign an offer to each influencer individually (same or different). Toggle the goal to see the treatment change.
