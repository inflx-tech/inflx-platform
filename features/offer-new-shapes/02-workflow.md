# Workflow — offer-new-shapes

## Quantity-volume
1. Admin: Offers → New → Volume → "Reward on: Quantity bought".
2. Applies to: Category (e.g. Tees) or Products.
3. Tiers: Buy 3+ → 20% off, Buy 5+ → 30% off.
4. Follower cart with 3 Tees → preview shows "20% off"; PDP shows the tiers as
   "Buy 3+ → 20% off".

## Same-product BOGO
5. Admin: Offers → New → BOGO → pick one product + buy qty (1) + free qty (1).
6. Follower cart with 2 of that product → preview shows 1 free (₹0) via the
   engine adjustment; PDP shows "Buy 1, Get 1 Free — same product, any
   colour/size".
7. "Buy A, get a different B free" → the builder points the admin to Free gift.

## Back-compat
8. Existing volume (spend) + old BOGO (buy_product/get_product) offers resolve
   and display exactly as before.
