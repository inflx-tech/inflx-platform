# Workflow prediction — offer-new-shapes

- Volume builder shows a "Reward on: Amount spent / Quantity bought" toggle;
  quantity tiers use "Buy at least (qty)".
- BOGO builder shows one product finder + buy/free qty, with a note steering
  "buy A get different B" to Free gift.
- PDP renders quantity tiers ("Buy 3+ → 20% off") and same-product BOGO
  ("Buy 1, Get 1 Free — same product").
- Cart preview (Phase 2 endpoint) shows the free same-product item and the
  quantity-volume discount automatically.
- Existing spend-volume + old buy/get-BOGO offers resolve and display unchanged.
