# Workflow prediction — concept-p2-posts

Mockup `mockups/post-composer.html` shows the 4 composer steps side by side.

1. Creator with zero collaborations sees the honest empty state on step 1 and
   cannot proceed (posts pull products from signed campaigns only).
2. With Denim Co selected → step 2 shows the 47 templates as chips grouped
   Category/Price/Offer + the 14 collections behind the segmented switch. A
   category chip demands a leaf via the full-screen leaf list; price/offer
   chips allow "All categories". "Show my products" prints the LIVE universe
   count — expected small/zero on today's thin prod inventory, which the flow
   treats honestly (stop + message, no dead-end crash).
3. Step 3 uploads each picked photo immediately (progress %); removing a
   slide re-indexes existing tags safely.
4. Step 4: tap → sheet → pick → pin renders at the tap point with name+price
   (Soft Editorial default). Submit → alert → My Posts "In review".
5. Admin /admin/posts shows the card with pins overlaid at the same x/y%;
   approve/reject flows notify the creator (visible on their bell +
   My Posts reason line).

Admin queue page follows the established admin-card grammar; no separate
mockup (delegated-lock note).
