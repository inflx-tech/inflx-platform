# 02 — Workflow

## Slice A (now)
1. Open the cart (View cart) → items + bill (unchanged).
2. Below the bill, a **"You might also like"** rail loads — products from the **same
   categories** as the cart items, excluding what's already in the cart.
3. Tap a recommendation → its product page.

## Slice B (next)
- A bottom bar in the cart: **Address** (saved + Change) + **Payment** (PhonePe default, tap
  to change) + **Place Order**.
- Place Order → the backend splits the cart by brand → one order per brand (each brand gets
  its items + the customer's delivery info) → payment (mock until real Razorpay).
