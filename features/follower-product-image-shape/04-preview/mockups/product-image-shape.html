One component, two screens. The brand-store fix is a single change to ProductCard, which also drives the category list — the biggest win for the smallest diff.

Two hidden constraints the line numbers didn't show. Saved's image wrapper carried its own height: 150 that would have clipped the new 3:4 child, and its no-image placeholder used flex: 1, which collapses to zero once that wrapper is unpinned. Both were fixed with it, or the placeholder would have silently vanished.

Product detail keeps contain deliberately. Paste-URL, Excel, photo-tray and mobile-gallery uploads bypass the 3:4 crop entirely today, so the one screen dedicated to inspecting a product must not slice an un-cropped image. Forcing those upload paths through the crop is a separate contract.

Cart stays square. 3:4 would take the line-item thumb from 54 to 72px and the cross-sell from 104 to 139px — visibly taller rows, fewer items per screen. Not worth it at that size.
