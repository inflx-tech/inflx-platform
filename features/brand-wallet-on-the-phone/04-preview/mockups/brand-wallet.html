Every number here comes from a field that already exists — the wallet endpoint already returns available, reserved and settled; the ledger already records every top-up, lock, fee and payout. Nothing on these screens needs a new calculation.

Three decisions inside these screens

1. The shortfall is offered exactly, not rounded up. W4 pre-fills ₹12,960 — the precise amount owed — rather than nudging her to ₹25,000. She can choose more; the screen does not choose it for her.

2. Adding money commits nothing. Said twice, in W3 and W6, because the whole point of the wallet is that money sits safely until a signature moves it. A brand who fears that adding money spends it will not add money.

3. Test mode is admitted, not hidden. W5 says plainly that no real money moves yet. When live payments are switched on, that paragraph disappears and not one screen changes.
