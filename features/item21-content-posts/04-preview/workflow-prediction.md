# Workflow prediction — item 21, Pay for Content shoppable posts + Entire store

**Brand sets terms (Pay for Content):** Set › Pick creators › Terms → Fee ₹8,000 → What she delivers:
2 reels, 1 story → **Shoppable posts in INFLX: 3** → **What she posts about:** Entire store / Offers /
Products without offers → summary "She posts about: your entire store · 3 shoppable posts · no commission"
→ Send.

**Creator:** Invitation shows Fee, You post (2 reels, 1 story), Shoppable posts (3 · no commission), You post
about (Everything in Zara's store, now and later ›). Counter → she may change fee, deliverables, posts number,
posting date, mode; not what she posts about. Agreement carries all rows; both sign; 25% out.

**After the shoot:** Going live → Path 1 "0 of 3 approved" → Create a shoppable post (brand + collaboration
filled in; offers = every running or future offer, price products = everything without an offer today) →
Send to INFLX → approved → Path 2 unlocks → she uploads the 2 reels + 1 story (item 12 unchanged) → brand
approves → she posts, links → brand confirms live → ₹2,000 released; "Final ₹4,000 due 31 Oct once your 3
posts are approved" → she makes posts 2 and 3 → approved → 31 Oct: ₹4,000 released → Completed.

**If she stops at 1 post:** 31 Oct passes, nothing releases; Needs You "2 more posts to release ₹4,000";
posting date passes → Delayed "shoppable posts not approved"; brand may report → frozen until INFLX settles.

**A sale:** a shopper buys through her post → brand's Sales money shows three shares with Creator ₹0; her
stats say "1 sale · ₹2,000"; her wallet is untouched.

**Entire store on Pay for Sale:** Terms → Entire store → "She can sell: your entire store · 10% on every
sale" → signed → next week the brand lists 5 new products → they are in her picker that day; a new offer
published for Diwali appears too; ending an offer removes her posts for it (as today).

**Edge cases:** Entire store on an empty store (refused); old-terms Pay for Content (one path, no posts
door); counter that touches the allocation (refused); n above 10 (refused); a post approved late (counts,
flagged); the brand confirming live before any posts beyond the first (allowed; final waits).
