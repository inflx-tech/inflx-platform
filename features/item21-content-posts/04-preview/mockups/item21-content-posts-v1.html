Pay for Content gets shoppable posts. Every Aim gets Entire store.

Pay for Content keeps its fixed fee and its fixed social media posts, and gains a brand-set number of shoppable posts in INFLX on what the brand chooses — with no commission to her. The going-live step becomes two paths, as Pay for Sale already is. Entire store is a third way to say what she can sell: everything, now and later.

Nothing here is built. Decisions D1–D6 were taken in chat; R1–R10 are my defaults.

D1The brand sets the number of shoppable posts (1–10) on a Pay for Content invitation; she may counter it like deliverables. Pay for Sale and Hybrid keep the admin minimum.T1, T4, C2
D2What she posts about = offers and products without offers, as Pay for Sale — or Entire store.T1, T2, C4, C5
