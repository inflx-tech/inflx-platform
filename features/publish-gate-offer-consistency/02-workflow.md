# The walk

## W1 · The screen he was on

1. Build a commission campaign with one set and one creator. **Give her no offer.**
2. Allocate what she can sell.
3. Review.
   * **Publish is black and works.**
   * One warning: nobody has a shopper offer. It is a warning, not a refusal.
   * **No "has no creators in it yet"** — the set has one.
   * The expiry warning still appears if her agreement is close to lapsing.
4. Publish. It goes.

## W2 · An offer on her row clears the warning

1. Same again, but tap **Give her an offer** and pick one.
2. Review. **The offer warning is gone.**
   This is what could not work before: the check read the set-level offer, so an offer on her
   row cleared nothing.

## W3 · One control, not two

At step 4 of the set wizard there is **no** set-level offer picker. Section 2 says the offer is
given per creator. The only way in is her row.

## W4 · The preview survived

Open her offer sheet, pick an offer, tap **See what a shopper pays**. The basket preview opens,
priced against **her** commission.

## W5 · The real refusals still refuse

Leave a creator with **nothing to sell**. Review. That is still red, and Publish is still greyed.
