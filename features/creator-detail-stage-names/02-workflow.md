# The walk — the creator app

## W1 · The screen he found

1. Open the creator app as **Ravi K. (practice)** → **Collabs** → **Invitations**.
2. Tap the **"New test campaign"** row.
3. **It opens with "Review this invite"** and three buttons: accept, negotiate, pass.
   Not "Closed — This collab is closed."
4. Tap **Accept**. It accepts. Tap **Pass**. It offers the two reasons.

## W2 · The wider half he had not reached yet

Open a collaboration at each stage and confirm none of them says "closed":

* **Terms agreed, no campaign yet** → *"Terms agreed — waiting for the brand to build the
  campaign"*, no buttons
* **To sign** → *"Review & sign contract"*
* **Shoot** → *"The shoot"*, opening the shoot screen
* **Content** → deliver, or with the brand for review
* **Done** → *"Collab complete"* with earnings

## W3 · Her sections come back

Deliverables · Performance · Earnings** — not just "Terms & brief".

work started.
