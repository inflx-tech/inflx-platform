M3 · The confirm — because this one is not a number

Every other setting on this page moves one rule by a few days. This one changes what the whole platform allows, so it is the only one that asks.

Turn subscription plans off?

Every brand becomes unlimited until you turn this back on:

  • no quota can block anyone — sets, campaigns, invitations, products, media
  • every feature is included for everyone
  • no “upgrade to continue” message can appear

Billing is not affected. Brands on a paid plan keep their plan and are still charged on the same date.

Your tiers, prices and per-brand overrides are kept, and apply again when you switch this back on.
