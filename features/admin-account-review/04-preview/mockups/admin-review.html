The admin brand page shows dashes where the data exists, and fee controls where the facts should be. Here is why, and what it should be instead.

These are ten small faults with one cause

Both pages were written against a different shape of data than the server sends, and nothing ever failed loudly — a name that does not exist simply prints a dash. That is why it looks like missing data rather than a broken screen.

The deeper problem

Wrong screen for the job

Even with every field fixed, that page would still be the wrong page — because it answers the wrong question.

An admin opening a pending account is asking one thing: is this a real business, and do I let them trade here?

What the page leads with instead is Override Platform Fee, L3 Commerce Commission Override, Feature Overrides and Wallet Balance. Every one of those is about how an already-approved brand trades. None of them help you decide whether Preeti Chouhan is real. You cannot set a meaningful fee for someone you have not let in yet.

And the decision is somewhere else entirely

Having read the account, you cannot act on it. Approve and Reject live on the Approvals page. So you read here, remember, navigate away, and find them again in a list. That is how the wrong person gets approved.

Proposal: one page, two states, driven by status.

  • Pending → a review page. The facts you need, what is missing, anything that looks wrong, and the two buttons. Nothing else.
  • Approved → an account page. Those same facts stay at the top, and the fee and feature controls appear below — where they finally mean something.

The one genuinely new idea: does the pin agree with the address?

She typed "Karnal" and her phone dropped a pin that resolves to Karnal, Haryana. Those agree, so the page says so and you move on.

When they disagree — a Vijayawada address with a pin in Dubai — that is the single most useful thing an approval screen can tell you, and it costs nothing because both values are already stored. This is the only check I would add. I am not proposing document uploads or verification workflows; you have five brands, not five thousand.

The handle is a link you can open. Thirty seconds on Instagram beats any number this screen could show you.

Why the handle link matters more than the numbers. A follower count on your screen is only ever a claim — someone typed it, or a scraper read it weeks ago. Opening the real profile is the actual check, so the screen should make that one tap.

Why "never scraped" is stated out loud. Every one of your creators shows a follower count that has never once been verified against Instagram. Right now the screen presents those numbers exactly as confidently as verified ones. It should not.

Payout details deliberately excluded. Whether she has given a UPI ID or PAN is stored, and it is not an approval question — it matters when she is owed money, not when she joins. Putting it here invites approving someone because their bank details look complete.

And the list has to exist

Admin → Influencers is a CSV upload box. Before any of the above is reachable, that page needs to become a list of your creators — name, handle, city, followers, status — matching the Brands list you already have.
