# ui-walk-fixes — workflow

Frame ids (A1, B3 …) are on the board `04-preview/mockups/ui-walk-fixes-mockups-v1.html`.

## W1 · A brand names a new set (A)
1. New set opens (from a new campaign, "+ Add another set", or the pool). The Set name box is empty
   with a grey example; nothing is typed for her; the keypad stays closed.
2. Save with the box empty → red line "Enter a set name."; nothing is saved.
3. She types a name → saved as typed. Editing or renaming an existing set still shows its name.

## W2 · A brand adds creators to a set (B)
1. From the set: Add creators. No bottom tabs (they are on the five main pages only).
2. Top: title + campaign/set line, tabs, one row with search and Filters. The list has the rest.
   Each creator is three lines: name; niche · city · distance · languages; her three numbers.
3. Filters opens the same sheet as today; "Open to invitations only" is in it.
4. Typing in search: the footer steps away; the list fills the space above the keypad.
5. Tick creators → the basket line and "Next: terms for N creators" as today.
6. A creator who can't be invited is greyed with "Can't invite" and a short reason ("Not taking Pay
   for Sale"). Tapping her shows the sentence ("Asha isn't taking Pay for Sale collaborations.").
   Paused and blocked both read "Not taking invitations".

## W3 · Anyone types into a box (C)
1. Tap a box in the lower half of a form → the box, its counter and its hint are visible above the
   main button's strip, which sits directly above the keypad.
2. In a pop-up sheet → the sheet stops below the status bar, its body scrolls, the note box is
   visible and the sheet's main button stays above the keypad.
3. Close the keypad → the screen is exactly as its locked board.

## W4 · A brand or creator sets deliverables (D)
1. One line per deliverable: type, minus/number/plus, remove.
2. "+ Add another" or a tap on a type opens the five types in place; added ones are grey "Added".
3. Pick one → the list closes, the line shows it with quantity 1. Five added → no "+ Add another".
4. The same control on the brand's terms, the brand's counter sheet, the creator's counter, the
   pitch form and the pitch counter. What is saved and sent does not change.

## W5 · A creator answers an invitation; a brand sends an agreement (E)
1. Accept is full width; under it Counter and Decline are level and equal. Counter absent → Decline
   full width.
2. "Send this agreement to …?" → "Not yet" and "Send" level and equal; while sending, "Sending".
3. After both sign: the button that says where the shoot stands is full width; Download under it.

## W6 · A brand sends an agreement and knows what happens next (F)
1. On the set, her row has a button "Send agreement ›". Tapping the row or the button opens it.
2. Send → the brand stays on the agreement: "Agreement sent." and a blue box "Sent to … on ….
   Waiting for her to sign by …"; both signature boxes read "Not signed yet"; Withdraw is offered.
3. Back on the set, her row is under "Waiting for the creator", with a clock tag "Waiting to sign".
   Tapping it reopens the agreement; the ⋯ menu offers "Open the agreement" and "Withdraw agreement".
4. Send refused (for example the signing date has passed) → the reason is shown inside the sheet.
5. Withdraw → "Agreement withdrawn."; she is back under "Your move" with "Send agreement ›".
6. Every other row that needs the brand carries a button that names the step; every row waiting on
   the creator carries a tag with a clock.

## W7 · A brand plans the visit (G)
1. Date and Time are two empty boxes. Date → calendar; Time → a list of 15-minute times.
2. Today's past times are grey. Changing the date so the time is past clears the time.
3. Send with either empty → "Choose a date and time."
4. "Move the visit?" opens showing the agreed time if it is still ahead, empty if it has passed.

## W8 · A brand or creator opens a content file (H)
1. One tap on a tile → the file opens inside the app, on black, with a close button.
2. A photo fits the screen. A video plays with play/pause, time and progress.
3. An older app build cannot play video inside the app: it says so and offers "Open in browser".
4. Brand, Pay for Content review: "Add a note" is offered in the viewer. Creator, while editing:
   "Remove".

## W9 · A creator links her live posts (J)
1. Paste a link → "Saving…" in the box, then a tick; the count turns green.
2. As soon as every link box has text and every Story has its screenshot, "I've posted everything"
   is on. Pressing it closes the keypad, finishes saving, then marks it posted.
3. A refused link → red line under that box; the button stays off and says a link needs fixing.
