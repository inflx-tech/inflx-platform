Pay for Content: choosing what she delivers

You reported that choosing what a creator delivers on a Pay for Content set is messy. Today every deliverable shows all five type buttons in a pile with a typing box beside it, although the board you locked drew one tidy line each. After the fix each deliverable is one line: a type box that opens its list in place, a minus / number / plus count from 1 to 20, and a remove button. The same block sits on five screens; four are drawn here and the fifth, countering a pitch, gets the identical rows.
