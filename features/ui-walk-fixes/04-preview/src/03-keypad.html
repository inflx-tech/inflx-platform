Typing: the box you type in stays visible

You reported that the keypad covers the box being typed in. That is true, but the keypad is not what hides it: the phone does lift the form, and then our own button bar rides up with the keypad and sits on top of the box, with the bottom tabs taking more room under it. Pop-ups cannot get shorter, so when one is longer than the room left above the keypad, its note box and Send button slide under the keypad. This was read from the code, not seen on a phone: before you lock this board, open Plan the visit on a phone, tap "Their number" and check that C0 is what you see. The keypad is drawn shorter here than a real one, so a real phone shows a little less above it than these frames do.

The rule this board locks, for every place someone types in the brand and creator apps: (1) the main button sits in its own strip under the form, never floating over it — as 34 of your locked frames already draw it; (2) the bottom tabs hide while the keypad is open; (3) the box being typed in, with its counter, hint and error line, is always visible above the button strip; (4) pop-ups stop below the status bar, their body scrolls, and their main button stays above the keypad; (5) with the keypad closed every screen looks exactly as its locked board. The frames below draw the title row tight under the status bar: the empty band the app has there today goes on every screen (frame B9), and the bottom tabs show only on the five main pages.
