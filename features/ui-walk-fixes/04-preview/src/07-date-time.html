Shoot plan: choosing the date and the time

You reported that choosing the time on the shoot plan is badly designed. Today it is a strip of day pills, fourteen hour pills and four minute pills; it shows a day and a time as if chosen when nothing is, and it offers hours that have already gone. It becomes the two boxes your item 10 board drew, Date and Time: nothing is chosen for you, Date opens the app's calendar and Time opens one simple list, both from the bottom of the screen, and a time that has passed cannot be tapped. This replaces the pill picker chosen in item 10; what stays from that choice is that it is built inside the app, not the phone's own Android clock.
