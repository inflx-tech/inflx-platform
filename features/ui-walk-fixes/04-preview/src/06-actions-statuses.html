What to do next, and what you are waiting for

You reported that "Send agreement", waiting and the other statuses are hard to recognise, and that nothing tells you what happened after you send an agreement. Both are true: today an action and a status are the same tiny pill — "Send agreement" looks exactly like the "Live" tag and is the only way to send — and after Send the screen closes before its message can be read. The rule this board locks: anything a person can tap to move the work forward is a labelled button that names the action and ends in a small arrow, at least 44 high to touch, with a dark outline and never yellow (yellow stays the one main button of a screen); a status is a small pale tag with an icon and never looks like a button. There is one action per row, tapping the row does the same thing as its button, and after every action the person who acted is told, in a sentence, what happens next.
