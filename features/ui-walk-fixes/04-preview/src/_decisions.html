What you decided, and the new words

One rule covers what is not drawn: anything on a screen today that a frame's caption does not say changes, stays exactly as it is.

1 · The creator row when adding creators

The compact row, and leaner than first drawn: you asked for the tag line (Pay for Sale / Pay for Content / Hybrid / +2) to go — "no need to mention this". The row is name, place and numbers. The Creators tab keeps its full card.

2 · A creator who can't be invited now says why

You asked for the sentence "Creators whose own settings rule this set out can't be picked, and the reason is never shown to them." to be removed, and for the reason to be told when the brand taps her — "this creator not accepting the pay for sale or something". Her row says it in a few words and a tap says it in full (frames B1, B2, B10). This reverses the item 5 rule that a brand is never told the reason. One thing does not change: a creator who has paused invitations and one who has blocked the brand read exactly the same.

3 · Bottom tabs only on the five main pages

Yes. Home / Campaigns / Creators / Wallet / Profile show on the five main pages and nowhere else, in both apps, as your locked boards draw them.

4 · The blank strip above every title

Yes, removed on every screen of both apps (frame B9). It was read from the code, not seen on a phone; it is built from the phone's real status-bar height, so it is right on any phone, and the first walk confirms it.

5 · A link pasted without "https://" in front

Yes, accepted — the app adds it.
