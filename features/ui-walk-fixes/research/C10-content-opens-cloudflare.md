# C10-content-opens-cloudflare — Content opens on Cloudflare site instead of in-app

## TODAY
Content screen (brand: creator's name as title; creator: "Your content"). Each deliverable row (e.g. "Instagram reel 2 of 2") shows small square tiles, 76x76: a photo tile shows the photo itself; a video tile is a plain black square with a white play icon and a duration badge (no preview picture). Tapping a tile does NOT open it: it opens a bottom menu with "View" (plus "Add a note" for the brand while reviewing, or "Remove" for the creator while editing) and "Cancel". Tapping "View" leaves the INFLX app: the phone's browser opens on an address ending r2.cloudflarestorage.com and shows the photo or plays the video there. The link dies after 5 minutes, so coming back to that browser tab later shows a storage error page. Exceptions: when the creator's package is locked in review, and for Story screenshots on the brand's "posted" screen, a single tap on the tile goes straight to the browser with no menu. The same thing happens on the Pay for Sale / Hybrid content screens (both sides). The signed Agreement PDF and the brand's shipping label PDF also open on the same cloudflare address in the browser / PDF viewer.

## ROOT CAUSE
The "View" action asks the server for a short-lived private link and passes it to Linking.openURL, which always leaves the app. Brand: Packages/Mobile/src/screens/brand/BrandContentScreen.js:71-76 (`Linking.openURL(r.data.view.url)`), wired to the menu at :245-248 and directly to Story screenshot tiles at :185. Creator: Packages/Mobile/src/screens/influencer/ContentScreen.js:126-131, wired at :267 and :359-362. The link is a presigned R2 GET built in backend/src/services/contentStorage.service.js:141-155 against the endpoint `https://<account>.r2.cloudflarestorage.com` (:54), valid 300 seconds (:35), served inline with the right content type and "private, no-store"; access is checked first in backend/src/services/content.service.js:263-291. It was a recorded decision, not an accident: Docs/ITEM12_CONTENT_GAP_REPORT.md:345 and :711-713 and features/item12-content/PROMPT.md:43 (Q11: "Signed URL in the phone's own player for now. react-native-video goes on the next APK list"), repeated in the file headers (BrandContentScreen.js:10-11, components/content/bits12.js:14 "No native module ... videos open with Linking"). There is no in-app photo or video viewer for collaboration content anywhere in the code (no full-screen image component, react-native-webview is installed but never used). The condition for lifting the stop-gap is now met: commit 61919622 (30 Sep 2026) added react-native-video 6.4.5 and rebuilt all three APKs; I confirmed the player's native class (com/brentvatne/exoplayer/ReactExoplayerViewManager) is inside APKs/INFLX-Brand.apk, INFLX-Influencer.apk and INFLX-Follower.apk (all dated 30 Sep 08:22), and the OTA runtime stayed '3' (services/otaUpdater.js:28) so over-the-air JS reaches them. The shopper app already uses the player with a runtime safety check (components/shop/ShopVideo.js:22-33 `hasPlayer()`), but nobody went back and switched the brand/creator content screens over.

## FIX RISKS
- Private video cached on the phone: ShopVideo passes bufferConfig cacheSizeMB: 200 (ShopVideo.js:107). Copying that into the content viewer would store a creator's private, unapproved video on the brand's phone, against the 'private, no-store' rule in contentStorage.service.js:152. The viewer must not set a cache.
- Expired link during playback is likelier than the finding implies: any seek, replay, or dropped connection after 5 minutes makes a new request with the dead signature and fails. The viewer needs on-error handling: fetch a fresh link, reload, seek to the last position. Each re-fetch also writes a server log line (content.service.js:289).
- Test loading: react-native-video is not mocked in jest.setup.js. If the new viewer imports it at the top of the file, the contentScreens and saleLiveScreens suites may fail to load. It must be required lazily behind the runtime check, as ShopVideo does.
- Moving hasPlayer can break the shopper app's test seam: video16.test.js calls _setPlayerForTests from ShopVideo. The singleton must stay shared (ShopVideo re-exports from the new module) or the shopper video tests and the 'Update INFLX' fallback diverge.
- Navigation crash risk: this project has shipped three native crashes from Stack.Screen registration mistakes. The viewer should be a Modal component inside the four screens, not a new route in BrandNavigator and InfluencerNavigator.
- The player's native control bar inside a Modal on Android is unproven here: ShopVideo uses no native controls. In react-native-video 6.4.5 the `fullscreen` props are iOS-only (src/types/video.ts:248-250), and there is no slider library in package.json. The scrub bar must be either the player's built-in controls (look not fully controllable) or a hand-built JS bar. The mockup should not promise a custom scrub bar until this is tried on a device.
- Hardware back button: the Modal needs onRequestClose to close the viewer and stop playback; otherwise back does nothing or audio keeps playing.
- Large photos: content photos can be 10 MB (copy12.js:46). A full-screen core <Image> on Android can render blank for very large dimensions unless resizeMethod="resize" is set. The device walk needs a 10 MB photo as well as a large video.
- Brand side loses the file kind: BrandContentScreen.viewFile(id) and BrandSaleLiveScreen.viewFile(id) pass only an id, and Story tiles pass a synthetic {kind:'photo'}. The viewer should pick photo vs video from the server's response (kind, mime), not from the tile.
- Release gates for a mobile UI change: gov-check requires the new viewer mockup locked on the backing contract, a non-empty 06-diff/notes.md, and release.maestro_green before released=true. The item 12 contract's lock covers the old board only; a new or amended contract is needed.
- OTA reach: one publish each for brand and influencer. The follower bundle is unaffected only if ShopVideo's behaviour is unchanged. Phones on an APK older than 30 Sep receive the same JS and must fall back cleanly for video.

## FIX FINAL
Option A stands, with these refinements. No backend change, no migration, no new APK; one OTA each for brand and influencer.

1. Build one Modal-based viewer component (e.g. components/content/MediaViewer12.js), not a new navigation route. Use it on all four screens: BrandContentScreen, ContentScreen, BrandSaleLiveScreen, SaleLiveScreen.
2. Fetch a fresh signed link every time the viewer opens; never reuse the thumbnail link. Choose photo vs video from the server's kind/mime.
3. Photos: core Image, fit whole, resizeMethod="resize". This works on every app build and delivers what item 12 already promised.
4. Videos: react-native-video, required lazily behind the runtime check, with no on-phone cache. On error or expiry, fetch a fresh link and resume from the same second. Keep the 300-second rule unchanged.
5. Older app build without the player: show "Update INFLX to play videos here" with "Open in browser" as the fallback.
6. Move the player check out of ShopVideo.js into a small shared module and have ShopVideo re-export hasPlayer and _setPlayerForTests, so video16.test.js and the shopper app are untouched.
7. "Add a note" appears only on the Pay for Content brand review (when can.askChanges). "Remove" appears only for the creator while editing. Neither appears on the brand Pay for Sale / Hybrid screen.
8. Also make the creator's Story screenshot tiles open in the viewer (ContentScreen.js:286; SaleLiveScreen.js:371, :429).
9. Add jest cases: tile tap opens the viewer, Linking.openURL is not called, old-build fallback shows, expired link re-fetches. Update the stale comments and 06-diff notes.
10. Leave the Agreement PDF, shipping label and Instagram/YouTube links opening outside the app.

Before the device walk: confirm the 30 Sep app build is installed on the test phones, then play a real large video and open a 10 MB photo on both apps.
