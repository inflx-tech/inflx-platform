# C08-no-status-after-action — No status after sending agreement / other actions

## TODAY
BRAND, sending an agreement (top to bottom, in order of what happens):
1. Agreement preview screen: title "Agreement · Sneha", grey reference line "DRAFT · DRAFT" (the word is printed twice), the terms cards, a grey line "Nothing here can be edited...", and one black button "Send for signature".
2. Tap it: a bottom sheet "Send this agreement to Sneha?" with "Not yet" and "Send".
3. Tap Send: the sheet closes and the whole agreement screen closes straight away. The black "Agreement sent." toast is raised on the screen that is closing, so it flashes for a fraction of a second or not at all.
4. The brand is back on the set screen. Sneha's row has LEFT the top "Your move" group (where it had a green "Send agreement" badge) and now sits in a group headed "Waiting to answer", which is listed BELOW "Signed". The row shows her name and one small grey line "Agreement sent 1 Oct". There is no badge on the right, only the three-dot menu. Creators who have not even answered their invitation sit in the same group, looking the same.
5. Tapping that row opens the three-dot menu: "View terms sent", "Open profile", "Remove from set". Nothing opens the agreement that was just sent, so the "Withdraw" button that exists on the agreement screen cannot be reached.
6. If the sent agreement screen were opened, it has no status box at all for this state: no "Waiting for Sneha to sign", no signature boxes (they only appear once somebody has signed). Only the terms and a red "Withdraw" button.

BRAND, other set rows: a row only gets a badge when it is the brand's turn (orange "Your move") or the creator is mid-negotiation (blue "Waiting"). When the brand is waiting on the creator after signing (she is uploading content, making posts, sending her address) the row shows a grey sentence and NO badge.

BRAND, pitch: accepting a pitch jumps to the pool screen with no confirmation message; rejecting a pitch just goes back silently.

Everything else already shows a status after the action and stays on the screen: brand counter ("Waiting for Priya. She has to reply."), shoot plan sent ("Waiting for Tanvi to answer on the time." with a clock), parcel booked (green box + tracking), content sent back ("Tanvi is making the changes you asked for."), content approved ("You approved it. Tanvi is posting it now."). CREATOR side is healthy throughout: after signing she sees a blue box "Signed. Waiting for Urban Threads to sign by 17 Oct." and a blue "Waiting" badge on her Collabs card; after accepting, "Waiting for the agreement from Urban Threads."; after sending content, "Urban Threads are reviewing this..."; after posting, a card "Waiting for them to confirm".

## ROOT CAUSE
Four separate causes, all in the phone app (the server already sends everything needed).

1. The brand's agreement screen leaves instead of updating. BrandAgreementScreen.send() calls show(C7.brand.sent) and then navigation.goBack() on the next line (lines 74-75). The toast belongs to the screen being closed, so it dies with it. Every other two-party screen either stays and re-renders from the server's reply, or hands the message to the next screen through the route (the useRouteToast pattern, e.g. BrandReviewSendScreen line 81, BrandShootPlanScreen line 85). The server's send reply already contains the full sent agreement (agreements.controller.js line 30), so the screen could simply stay. withdraw() has the same toast-then-goBack fault (lines 102-103).

2. The brand's agreement screen has no sentence for "sent, she has not signed". The status boxes at lines 136-140 cover only ended / both signed / she signed. copy7.js has no brand-side waiting sentence at all (only the creator's waitingFor at line 168). The signature boxes are hidden until someone has signed (line 228).

3. The set row for a sent agreement has no badge and the wrong heading. In bits5.js SetCreatorRow5, case 'waiting' (lines 119-125) sets the grey line "Agreement sent ..." but never sets a chip. The server files the row under group key 'waiting' whose title is "Waiting to answer" (invitationRows.service.js lines 36-39, 62) and that group is ordered after 'signed'. The row's meta style is 12px in the lightest grey (bits5.js line 275).

4. No door back to the sent agreement. BrandSetDetailScreen's row tap (lines 214-216) and three-dot menu (lines 130-143) never use the server's actions.viewAgreement / actions.withdrawAgreement flags (invitationRows.service.js lines 245-246), so the sent agreement and its Withdraw button are unreachable.

Separately: pitch accept passes a "highlight" value that the pool screen never reads and shows no toast (BrandPitchScreen line 51, PitchThreadScreen line 69; BrandPoolScreen has no reference to highlight); pitch reject is a bare goBack (BrandPitchScreen line 60, PitchThreadScreen line 81).

## CORRECTIONS
- WRONG about tests. The finding says of fix A: 'The existing test only asserts the API call, so it will not break.' Packages/Mobile/src/screens/brand/__tests__/item7Brand.test.js:88 asserts getByText('DRAFT · DRAFT'). Fixing the doubled word breaks that assertion; it must be changed deliberately and recorded as assertion drift under the no-fake-green rule.
- WRONG about what happens after Withdraw (Screen 3 and fix B: 'Priya's row back under Your move with chip Send agreement'). backend/src/services/agreement.service.js:419 sets contracts.status = 'withdrawn' (and sent_at = NULL). backend/src/services/invitationRows.service.js:57 (agreementOwed) and :243 (actions.sendAgreement) both require contract_status === 'draft', so the row falls to STATUS_GROUP['accepted'] (line 43) = 'Accepted, waiting for the agreement', with no chip (bits5.js:126) and sendAgreement false. agreement.service.js:125 also gives can.send = false for 'withdrawn'. The send endpoint itself does accept 'withdrawn' (agreement.service.js:298) and backend/tests/item7-agreement.test.js:668-681 proves re-send only by calling the API directly, never through the set row. Result: a dead end on the phone after Withdraw, contradicting the P14 sheet wording 'you can send the same one again'.
- Therefore 'needs_backend_change: false' for fix B is wrong once Withdraw is made reachable. Either the server treats 'withdrawn' like 'draft' in groupOf/sendAgreement/can.send, or the phone must special-case row.agreement.status === 'withdrawn'.
- OVERSTATED: 'Tapping that row opens the three-dot menu'. Only when she never negotiated. invitationRows.service.js:240 sets actions.thread = !!neg_id || !!move_requested_at, and BrandSetDetailScreen.js:215 opens BrandThread first. A creator who countered before accepting opens the old conversation instead of the menu. Either way the agreement is unreachable, so the conclusion holds.
- The finding lists the two 'Your move' chips as matching the board. They do not match on colour: bits5.js:102 and :105 use tone 'active' (green #1A7A3D per tokens.js:518); the locked P1 markup uses class 'chip s-move' (orange #B04600) for both 'Send agreement' and 'Sign' (item7-agreement-locked.html line 398, CSS line 102).
- Mockup P2's reference line 'INFLX-2026-00123 · DRAFT' cannot be reproduced as drawn: the reference number is only stamped at send (agreement.service.js:250 comment 'null until sent', and :286-289). The realistic fix is a single 'DRAFT' (bits7.js:17 falls back to C7.brand.draft and BrandAgreementScreen.js:134 passes the same word as the suffix).
- The 'no door back' defect is wider than stated. actions.downloadAgreement (invitationRows.service.js:247) is also unused on the phone, and BrandShootScreen has no navigation to BrandAgreement despite the comment at BrandSetDetailScreen.js:110-111 saying the agreement 'is still one tap away from there'. A grep for 'BrandAgreement' in Packages/Mobile/src finds only BrandSetDetailScreen.js:109 and the Add-money return (BrandAgreementScreen.js:210). So a signed agreement and its Download button (P12) are unreachable after leaving the screen too.
- Minor: 'toast flashes for a fraction of a second or not at all' is fair but imprecise. The stack is native-stack (BrandNavigator.js:101), so the closing screen stays mounted during the pop animation and the toast slides away with it. Citations 74-75 and 102-103 are correct.
- The mobile P1 test fixture uses order ['yourMove','waiting','signed'] (item7Brand.test.js:248), which is not the real server order ['yourMove','negotiating','accepted','signed','waiting',...] (invitationRows.service.js:36). The phone tests therefore do not protect, or reveal, the ordering problem.

## FIX RISKS
- Withdraw dead end: making the sent agreement reachable (fix B) exposes the server gap where a withdrawn agreement cannot be re-sent from the phone. Needs a backend change in invitationRows.service.js (lines 57, 243) and agreement.service.js (line 125), which is production-impacting (Render deploys on push) and needs an explicit go. Older phone bundles handle the corrected server output safely: they already render a yourMove row with sendAgreement.
- Preview flag: 'preview' is read from route.params (BrandAgreementScreen.js:33) and load() depends on it (line 65). If the screen stays after Send using only local state, any refocus calls agreementPreview again. The server preview still succeeds because the invitation status stays 'accepted' (agreement.service.js:243), so the screen would revert to the draft with a live 'Send for signature' button; a second send is refused and that refusal is hidden behind the sheet. Use navigation.replace('BrandAgreement', {..., preview:false, toast}) plus useRouteToast, as BrandShootPlanScreen.js:85 does, or navigation.setParams({preview:false}).
- If setParams is used, load changes identity, useFocusEffect re-runs, the screen drops to the skeleton, and the toast vanishes during it because {toast} is only rendered in the main return (line 301), not in the loading Frame (line 114).
- item7Brand.test.js:88 asserts 'DRAFT · DRAFT' and must be updated. The send mock at line 81 returns an agreement with her signature already present and can.sign true, so a stay-on-screen change will render the 'she signed' state in that test; the fixture needs correcting to a true sent-unsigned shape.
- Showing the signature boxes in the sent state means changing the condition at line 228; it must stay hidden in preview (P2 draws none).
- copy7.js line 3 says it mirrors backend/src/services/agreementCopy.js and 'the two are kept identical'. A new brand waiting sentence should be added on both sides. I could not find a test enforcing the parity, so I cannot confirm whether anything fails if it is not.
- C7.brand.bothSigned is used by the creator's AgreementScreen.js:103. Add new keys; do not reword existing C7.brand strings or the influencer app changes too.
- SetCreatorRow5 is asserted in components/shoot/__tests__/shoot10.test.js and in item5Brand, item6Brand and item7Brand tests. item5Brand.test.js:105 and item6Brand.test.js:236 assert the heading 'Waiting to answer'; a new 'Waiting' chip can create duplicate-text matches for any getByText('Waiting') in those suites. Split on row.agreement.status === 'pending_influencer' or actions.withdrawAgreement rather than on sentAt alone.
