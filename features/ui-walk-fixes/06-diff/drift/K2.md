## New wording (spec U22 — for the founder's eye at the walk)

Creator app › Profile › Invitation settings. Both live in `components/settings/copy.js`
(`COPY.creator`); each is drawn in exactly one place.

| Where she reads it | Was | Is now |
|---|---|---|
| Under the "Brand categories" chips (`categoriesHint`) | "None picked means any category. Only you see this." | **"None picked means any category. Brands never see your list — a brand outside it is told only that you aren't taking its category."** |
| On the "Brands see on your profile" card shown after a save (`previewHint`) | "Your categories, cities, minimum fee and commission stay private." | **"Brands never see your category or city lists. A brand you've ruled out is told which rule it was, nothing more."** |

Why: since D2 a brand that cannot invite her is told which of her rules ruled it out ("Not taking
your category", "No store visits in Hyderabad" …). Her lists themselves are still never shown.
The minimums half of the old card sentence was already untrue — the brand's Terms screen has
shown her minimums, as a warning, since item 5.

What did NOT change:

- **The pitch direction.** The brand's Pitch settings screen uses its own keys (`COPY.brand.*`),
  not these two, and nothing about pitches moved in this contract. "Only you see these. Creators
  who don't match are told they don't meet your criteria." and "Blocked creators see you as not
  accepting pitches. They aren't told they're blocked." stay word for word (now pinned by a test).
- **The server.** There is no server copy of either sentence (`backend/src` searched for the old
  and the new words). The sentences the brand is told are the server's own
  (`invitationCopy.js` `CANT_INVITE`), and the new words agree with them. No server file was touched.
- **Everything else on the screen** (U10): the card's two rows under the sentence ("Brand
  categories", "Your minimums"), every other hint, every switch and chip.

  No word was added or changed — one box got its own words back.

## Left as it is, on purpose (told, not fixed)

- **"Only you see these."** still opens the hint under "Your minimums, by Aim" on the same screen
  (`COPY.creator.minimumsHint`: "Only you see these. Brands can still invite below them, with a
  warning."). U22 itself says the brand's Terms screen has shown her minimums since item 5, so
  this sentence is open to the same objection. It is not one of U22's two sentences, so it was
  not touched (U10). For the founder to decide at the walk.
- **How long "Counter sent." is on show.** As on every screen that is replaced and handed a
  message (item 6's conversation, the shoot conversation, the brand's shoot page), it is drawn
  with the thread, not over the loading skeleton, and lasts 2.6 seconds from arrival; a
  conversation that takes longer than that to load shows none. Drawing it over the skeleton
  would mean rewriting a source check in `creatorStrips.test.js` ("drawn once — these screens'
  early returns draw none") for one screen only; it is better decided for all of them together.
- **After a counter, Back passes through the older copy of the conversation** (the counter screen
  replaces itself with a new one on top of the one it was opened from). Unchanged; the same on
  item 6's conversation. The older copy reloads when it is shown, so nothing stale is read.
- **One more key written twice, outside the copy files:** `services/api.js` names
  `influencerApi.createPost` twice (the old post composer's call, then the shoppable-post one).
  The later one wins and all four callers want the later one, so nothing is wrong on the phone;
  the first line is dead. Not a copy file, not in this slice — left alone.
- **Photos in the app's picture cache** — see `drift/H.md`, last section (spec U26): next
  app-build list.
