## Behaviour that moved (for the walk) — what a test did NOT pin before

- **The Save strip is in the column.** `SaveBar` was `position: 'absolute', bottom: 0` over the
  form; it is now a `BottomBar` under the form. Same testIDs (`save-bar`, `save-bar-fix`,
  `save-button`), same "Fix N things above to save" line above the button. (Board C rule 1.)
- **The strip is 8 shorter at the bottom.** The old bar had 14 padding and 22 under the button;
  the shared strip has 14 all round plus the phone's bottom inset. The yellow button therefore
  sits 8 lower than before on the eight Save screens, the same as every other screen's strip.
  (Board C draws one `.savebar` for every screen; the task says "build it on BottomBar".)
- **The dead space at the end of the eight forms is gone.** `paddingBottom: 140` (six screens) and
  the 120-high spacer block (Pitch settings, Invitation settings) existed only to scroll clear of
  the floating bar. The forms now end with their ordinary 18 padding. (Research C03 FIX RISKS,
  last point.)
- **The lines under the box being typed in come into view.** On the eight screens the form is a
  `FormScroll`: when a box takes focus or the keypad opens it scrolls so that 64 under the box
  (counter, hint, error line) is above the strip. Before, Android stopped at the box's own bottom
  edge — under the floating bar. (Board C rule 3, frames C1–C3.)
- **The toast on the eight screens sits just above the strip.** It is drawn inside the box that
  holds the form, 16 above the strip, whatever the strip's height. Before it was 110 above the
  screen's bottom edge, which a strip with the "Fix N things" line or a bottom inset reaches.
  On the loading / couldn't-load states of Business details and Profile info (no strip there) it
  is 16 above the screen's bottom edge. Every other screen's toast is where it was: 110.
- **"What you sell" (no text box)** gets the same column: strip under the page, no dead space.
- **The form never goes back to a box she has scrolled away from** (review round). A box keeps
  the cursor after the keypad is closed with the phone's Back key, and while she drags the form or
  taps a chip. FormScroll now looks only when the scroll area gets SHORTER and the box was at
  least partly on show before it did (Android's own rule), when the keypad shows and the box is
  on show, or when a box takes focus. The keypad closing, and the strip gaining or losing its
  "Fix N things above to save" line while the box is out of sight, leave the form where she put
  it. (Board C rule 4 / U10.)
- **A pop-up written inside the form does not move the form behind it** (review round). The
  State / City pickers on Add a branch and Profile info are drawn by `OfferedStateCityFields`
  inside the form; on this app's renderer the phone does not refuse a measurement from the
  pop-up's search box to the form. FormScroll now also checks that the box is on screen where
  the form's content puts it (both axes, to within 2) and does nothing otherwise.

## Not provable without a phone

FormScroll's scroll on a real keypad (the phone's answers are supplied by hand under jest); that
the strip, not the keypad, was what hid the box (research C03 FIX FINAL 0); the toast's look

running (they are queued together).

## Not built exactly as the frame says (final review, 2 Oct 2026 — spec decision U20)

- **Frame C2's caption: "It is one swipe away, and back in place when the keypad closes."** On a
  form longer than the screen — Plan the visit, Terms, the creator's content page — closing the
  keypad leaves the form where typing put it; the top (the crumb line, the grey "From the set"
  card) comes back with one swipe, not by itself. Forms that fit on the screen (New campaign, the
  pop-ups) do return by themselves: Android puts them back. This is how the live app behaved
  before this build, so nothing got worse, but the caption's second half holds only for forms
  that fit. Why it was left: FormScroll finishes a scroll Android started and never starts one
  (the "she has scrolled away" rule above) — a form that moves by itself when the keypad closes
  can pull the box she has just tapped out from under her thumb. **To be shown at the walk
  (checks 9 and 11).** If the founder asks for the top to return by itself, the build is in
  `useRevealFocusedInput`: remember the scroll position when the area first gets shorter with a
  box focused; forget it when she drags; on the area growing back, go to the remembered position
  but never further than keeps the focused box on show. That build rewrites the tests named
  below and cannot be proved under jest.
- Pinned as it stands by `FormScroll.test.js` › "the keypad closes (Back key — the box keeps the
  cursor): the area grows and the form stays put", "the area grows with the box on show: still
  nothing…", "never listens for the keypad going away…", and — new in the final review — "U20 — a
  long form moved up by typing stays where typing put it when the keypad closes: FormScroll moves
  it once, up, and never back" (the whole walk in one test: the keypad opens, the form is moved
  once to 308; the keypad closes, no second move and the phone is not asked). The file goes from
  62 to 63 tests; no assertion was changed.
