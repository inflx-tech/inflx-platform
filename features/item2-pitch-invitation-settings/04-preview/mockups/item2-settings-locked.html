  • Screen states: loading, couldn't load, default, unsaved changes, errors, saving, saved, save failed, paused, discard-changes prompt.
  • Existing items keep going: pausing or blocking never withdraws pitches or invitations already sent, or signed collaborations.
  • Checked twice: when the other side chooses (buttons disabled) and again by the server when a pitch or invitation is sent.
  • Privacy: only paused status, Aims and modes are public. No threshold, list or block is ever revealed.
  • Data the gap prompt will look for: brand pitch settings, creator invitation settings, a block list for both directions.
  • Lists: creator niches from sign-up, 8 major fashion categories (also the "industries" for commission and the brand's category), shared state → city list, languages.
  • Read by other screens: a public "accepts / open to" summary on the brand overview and creator profile, and an eligibility check the pitch (item 8) and invite (item 5) flows call.
  • Audit note: the only pitch settings found in the backend are admin-level, so these are expected to be new.
