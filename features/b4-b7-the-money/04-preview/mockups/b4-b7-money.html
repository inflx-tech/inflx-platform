B4 + B7 · The money

His wallet and her earnings are two ends of one payment. Drawn together, because drawing them apart would mean drawing the same rupee twice. BRAND CREATOR

Everything on these screens has never run once.

escrow_entries 0 rows · wallet_transactions 0 rows · influencer_withdrawals 0 rows · every brand wallet ₹0 · all 25 creator wallets ₹0 withdrawable, ₹0 pending.

The code is there — three tranches, escrow fees, TDS, payouts. Not one rupee has moved through it. These screens are drawn from the rules in the source, and the first real payment will be the first test any of it has had.
The two aims are paid completely differently, and the screens must not blur that.

Pay for content — his money is locked in escrow when the campaign publishes, then released in three tranches: 25% when the contract is signed, 25% when the content goes live, 50% after the wait admin sets. He pays whether or not anything sells.

Pay per sale — nothing is locked and nothing is owed. A follower buys; the money arrives at us and splits three ways — her commission, our fee, and what the brand keeps. Hers goes to her pending balance, his to his sales balance, and both free up after the 30-day return window.

So a brand's wallet has two halves that must never be added together. Campaign money is what he put in to pay creators. Sales money is what he earned and can withdraw.
