Ordering and deals: the final screens

Everything agreed on 30 September in one place: simple posts, how a deal opens, the product pop-up, the four deal types with every sub-type, the review, the bag, address and paying, orders and cancelling. Rules: Docs/OFFER_TERMS_POSTS_RULES.md §6 (v1.12). Not in this step: returns and exchanges, pay at store.

The rules on these screens
  • Every item remembers the post it came from; offers work only inside one post; one bag, one payment, one parcel per brand.
  • Simple posts (normal price, % or ₹ off): Add opens the size sheet (O1).
  • Deal posts: Shop this post → Build your deal (D1) → a full deal page for that kind of offer.
  • On every deal page: tap a product → the product pop-up (photos, details, colours, sizes, other choices) → Pick → it closes → the card shows it picked → the next product (P1–P3).
  • Every deal ends on Review your deal (D4) → Add to bag or Buy now; in the bag a deal stays one group with Edit deal (D11).
  • Guests sign in at Continue; manual addresses; UPI / cards / net banking, or cash on delivery up to ₹5,000; free delivery at launch; prices fixed at Place order; cancel until Shipped, deal items together, gifts follow, automatic refunds.

Deal 3 of 4 · Bundle

Three sub-types: pick any, mix & match, and fixed

Pick any: any N products from one category for one price. Mix & match: the same, from several categories. For both, every full set costs the bundle price; a pick outside a full set pays its normal price, and the costliest picks fill sets first. Fixed: an exact set of products sold only whole; she only chooses colours and sizes.

Deal 4 of 4 · Free gift
