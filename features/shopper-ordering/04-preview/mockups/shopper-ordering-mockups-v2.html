Deals · how they open

A deal post opens a full deal page

A deal can carry 20 products and 10 gifts, so it is never built in a pop-up. Shop this post says what the deal is and how it works, then Build your deal opens a full page for that kind of offer. Every deal page has the deal at the top, every product in a grid, a size sheet for each pick, a "Your picks" tray, a sticky progress bar, and a review before the bag.

Step 1: choose what you buy. Step 2: choose your gift. Then review.
