Deals: the four types, every sub-type, and the product pop-up

Only the deals: BOGO, Buy more save more (volume), Bundle and Free gift. Simple % and ₹ discounts need none of this. The bag and the ordering flow are left aside for now. Rules: Docs/OFFER_TERMS_POSTS_RULES.md §1.6, §6.4, §6.5 (v1.11).

How a shopper picks in every deal
  • The deal page shows every product of the post in a grid.
  • Tap a product → the product pop-up opens right there: photos, name, price, details, colours, sizes with stock, size chart, any other choice.
  • She chooses, taps Pick → the pop-up closes → the card shows "✓ Picked · Blue · M".
  • She taps the next product the same way. Tapping a picked card reopens its pop-up to change or remove it.
  • Nothing is picked without its colour and size.

Deal 1 of 4 · BOGO

BOGO has one kind: pay for one, get the same product free

The rule: she buys any product of the post and gets the same product free, in any colour or size, whatever that one costs. It is never a different product (buy 511, get a 501: not allowed). The brand can set a maximum number of free items per order. The pop-up has two parts: the paid one, then the free one.

Deal 2 of 4 · Buy more, save more (volume)

Three sub-types: by quantity with %, by quantity with ₹, and by value

The rule: every item she picks from the post counts. The brand sets 1 to 4 tiers. Only the highest tier reached applies; tiers replace each other and never add up. By quantity counts items; by value counts ₹ spent at normal prices. A % reward comes off every item; a ₹ reward comes off her total for this post once.

Deal 3 of 4 · Bundle

Three sub-types: pick any, mix & match, and fixed

Pick any: any N products from one category for one price. Mix & match: the same, from several categories. For both, every full set costs the bundle price; a pick outside a full set pays its normal price, and the costliest picks fill sets first. Fixed: an exact set of products sold only whole; she only chooses colours and sizes.

Deal 4 of 4 · Free gift

Two sub-types: spend a threshold, or buy X items, and a gift when there's only one

Spend threshold: when her items from this post reach the amount (at normal prices), she chooses the offer's number of gifts. Buy X get Y: the same, counting items. Gifts are once per order, while stock lasts. A gift item (never sold) shows what it's worth; a store product given as a gift asks its size too. With one gift in the pool there's nothing to choose.
