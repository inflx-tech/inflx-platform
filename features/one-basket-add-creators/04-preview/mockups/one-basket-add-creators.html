3 · The rules this obeys

Combining sources changes who is in the basket. It changes nothing about who may be invited — every existing refusal still applies, one creator at a time.

SituationWhat happens
A creator is in the group and already ticked by handAdded once. The check screen says so before you tap.
A group member her own settings rule outNot added, and named under "Can't be invited" — exactly as today.
A group member already live in this setNot added. "Already in this set."
A group member live in another set of this campaignAdded. That is the fix deployed this morning — rules v1.21 §148.
You add two groupsBoth merge into the basket. Each creator keeps the group she came from.
A pool creator whose Aim matchesArrives with her agreed terms filled in, as today.
You tap Back from the group checkNothing is added. The basket is exactly as you left it.

4 · What it costs

Brand app — the picker footer, the group-check button, one new review sheet. Backend — accept a source group per creator instead of one per batch. Migration — none. campaign_influencers.source_group_id is already per row. Creator app — nothing. She never sees any of this. Ships as — one push and one brand OTA.
