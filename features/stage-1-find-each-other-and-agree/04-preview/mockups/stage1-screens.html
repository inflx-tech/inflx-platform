Her Collabs tab — six stages, and the badge
A brand's request arrives on the first screen she already uses. Today it sits under Profile → Brand Proposals → third tab, which is why one went unseen for a week.
Six stages replace eight. Today's list is Invites · Applied · Negotiating · To sign · Active · Tracking · Completed · Archive — and three of those are states a creator never sees. Empty stages stay visible and greyed rather than disappearing: a strip that changes shape as work arrives is disorienting, and hiding "Done" is part of what let the original bug live.
