Same screen, one row lighter. The button is untouched.
Removed: the strip, the StageRail component, and the vocabulary behind it — CAMPAIGN_STAGES, stageIndex, stageTrack. Deleted, not hidden: a dead idea left lying about gets rebuilt by somebody in six months.
Kept: the primary action. Fund wallet & publish, Review 3 applications, Confirm 1 live URL. It shares a helper with the rail, so the job is taking the stage half without taking the action half — that is the one thing that could go wrong, and every campaign status is checked.
Why it goes. Stage 3 agreed a different six — Agree · Sign · Shoot · Shop posts · Go live · Complete — decided on the server and shown per creator. Two sixes, half the words colliding: Contracts/Sign, Production/Shoot, Review/Shop posts. And they do not measure the same thing: one is the campaign, one is one collaboration.
Renamed too, because a file called campaignStages containing no stages is a trap: utils/campaignPrime.js, campaignPrimeAction(), components/brand/CampaignPrimeBar.js.
Not in here: rebuilding this screen around Stage 3's six. This removes the wrong thing; it does not build the right one. That stays its own stage.
