**Priya's "Festive Sarees" (K7M2F9) for Sri Lakshmi Silks has been live since 10 Oct.** Shoppers have opened
it 1,840 times, mostly as guests; 212 signed-in shoppers liked it, 64 saved it, 19 shared it, 38 left comments;
97 tapped Shop or a product and 23 added a saree to a bag. All of that is already in the database tonight.

**Priya opens Post status** → under Share · K7M2F9 and Stats, above "On social media", one new line:
"**In INFLX** · 1,840 views · 212 likes · 38 comments". She taps **Stats** → the money row she knows (1,284
visits · 37 orders · ₹4,120 to you), then a new block **In INFLX**: eight boxes — views 1,840 · likes 212 ·
comments 38 › · shares 19 · saves 64 · shop taps 97 · added to bag 23 · finished 410 (it is a video) — and one
hint line: "Shoppers inside INFLX since 10 Oct. Guests count for views, shares and taps; likes, comments and
saves need an account." Below it, "On social media" and the channel lists exactly as yesterday.

**She taps the comments box** → **Comments · Festive Sarees**, 38 comments, newest first. "Meghana · 2 h ago ·
Where is this available in Vijayawada?" — she taps **Reply**, writes "Sri Lakshmi Silks, Besant Road — or tap
Shop on the post", one yellow **Send**. Her reply sits under the comment as "You · just now"; in the shopper
app it shows under Meghana's with the creator's label. A comment "dm for cheap copies" — she taps **Hide**:
it greys out with "Hidden · only you see this" and an **Unhide**; it is gone from every shopper's list and the
count reads 37. A comment INFLX already removed from the reports queue is not in her list at all.

**The brand opens Set B › posts** → under K7M2F9's "1,284 visits · 37 orders · ₹41,200 sales · ₹4,120
commission" a second line "**In INFLX** · 1,840 views · 212 likes · 37 comments", then the social line. It taps
the post → the same In INFLX block between the money row and On social media; it taps the comments box →
the same list with no buttons and the line "Only Priya can reply or hide". A post nobody has opened reads
"In INFLX · nothing yet".

**Ravi's own-store post** (item 20) → the same block on his stats; money reads sales; he replies as the creator.

**What never happens.** No number here changes pay, commission or a wallet. Nothing is scraped; no Apify
run, no job, no "Update now" — the numbers are live when the screen opens. The shopper app changes nothing:
it already records every one of these and shows the counts on its cards. Impressions, dwell time and sound
taps stay recorded and unshown. The brand website shows nothing new (a later contract, with Level 3).
