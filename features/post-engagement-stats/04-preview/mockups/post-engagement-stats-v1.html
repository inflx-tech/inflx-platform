What shoppers do with her post inside INFLX

Every like, comment, save, share, open, shop tap and add-to-bag on an INFLX post is already recorded by the shopper app. This board puts them in front of the creator on each of her posts and the brand on each post made for it — one block on the screens they already open — and gives her the comments screen the rules promised: read, reply, hide. Nothing is scraped, nothing costs, nothing moves money.

Draft. Six frames; three are screens that exist today with one block or one line added (C3, C4, B3, B4), two are new (C5, B5). Every number traces to a column that exists (spec §1, §3).
S · The block, and the line

The same words on every screen

Questions · spec §7

Ten, decided 26 Oct 2026 as recommended

  • 1 · The seven numbers (eight for a video), or only §4.15's five? — the seven.
  • 2 · All-time since live, or the last 30 days? — all-time.
  • 3 · Comments (reply, hide, brand reads) in this contract? — yes, together.
  • 4 · Brand website now? — later, with Level 3.
  • 5 · Money row first, then In INFLX, then On social media? — yes.
  • 6 · "3 new comments" on Home / Needs You? — later.
  • 7 · The line on each Set posts row? — yes.
  • 8 · Can she delete her own reply? — no.
  • 9 · Guests count where they can act? — yes.
  • 10 · Own-brand posts included? — yes.
Unchanged

What this does not touch

  • The shopper app — it already records all of this and shows the counts on its cards.
  • Money: orders, commission, wallets, escrow — never read from here.
  • Level 2 (Instagram / YouTube numbers) and Level 3 (visits, orders by channel) — kept exactly; the block sits between them.
  • The feed's post_performance roll-up and the learning feed — untouched.
  • Admin — the reports queue and the readiness panel as they are.
  • The brand website — nothing this time.
