# Workflow — product detail hero fit

1. Follower taps a product → detail page opens.
2. The hero shows the FULL product photo (head-to-toe for a model shot) in a
   portrait 3:4 frame — nothing cropped at top or bottom.
3. Swiping the hero pages through the product's images as before; dots track.
4. Non-portrait sources letterbox on the pale background instead of cropping.
5. Food detail + the "more products" thumbnails are visually unchanged.
