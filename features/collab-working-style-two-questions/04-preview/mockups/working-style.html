How you'd work together — two questions, not three answers

Founder, 2026-08-22 — "actually it is two, one is store visit and remote… remote selected then ask for product shipping involved… if yes then that flow will continue"

The change
Only the working-style block of the collaboration request changes. Aim, money, time, and what-they'd-promote are untouched.
Why the third option was wrong. "Store visit", "We ship products" and "Remote" were offered as three answers to one question — but they are not the same kind of thing. The first two are about where she works. The third is about whether a box moves. Sitting them side by side forced a brand who ships products and expects a store visit to pick one and lose the other, and it hid the shipping question from every remote collaboration where it actually matters.
