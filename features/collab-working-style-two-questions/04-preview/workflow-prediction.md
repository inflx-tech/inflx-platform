# Workflow prediction

What the founder will see when he walks it, written before implementation.

1. Brand app → a creator → **Ask to collaborate**. Under WHAT THEY'D PROMOTE, the heading
   now reads **WHERE THEY'D WORK · OPTIONAL** with three chips: Not stated, Store visit,
   Remote. **"We ship products" is gone.**
2. Tap **Store visit** — nothing new appears. Only the requirement/preference row.
3. Tap **Remote** — a bordered block slides in below reading **ARE YOU SENDING HER
   PRODUCTS?** with **Yes, we'll ship** and **No**, defaulting to neither until tapped.
4. Tap **Yes, we'll ship**, then send.
5. Creator app → Collabs → the new request. Under HOW YOU'D WORK there are now **two**
   lines: **Where you'd work — Remote** and **Products — They'll ship to you**.
6. Open **What happened here** on either side. The term is labelled **How you work
   together**, not "Where you shoot".
7. (Item 2, if approved) Brand app → Contract for that creator. The production question
   shows **Store visit / Remote** with Remote already selected and **Yes, we'll ship**
   already selected, above a green line saying it was carried over from the agreement.

Predicted failure most likely to appear: step 3's block failing to hide again when the
brand switches back to Store visit.
